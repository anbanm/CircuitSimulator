## 1. Secunda's suburbs: naming, numbering, arrangement, differences by age and income

### Takeaway
Secunda is a Sasol-planned company town (designed from 1974 by Swiss architect Max Kirchhofer, first section proclaimed 1976) whose residential areas are officially numbered "Secunda Ext N" rather than named; estate agents overlay a few names (Secunda Central, Sunset Park, Terra Nova, Kruinpark, etc.). The plan is a garden-city / Radburn layout of neighbourhood units threaded by a green network, with housing class deliberately tied to location: the best housing sits against the main green strips ("Green Area", "Duckpond").

### Cited Findings
- [CONFIRMED] In 1974 Sasol (Transvaal) Townships Limited, a Sasol subsidiary, was instructed to establish and develop Secunda; the first section was proclaimed in 1976 and the first resident moved in that year. The name refers to the second Sasol coal-to-oil plant after Sasolburg. — [Wikipedia: Secunda](https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Secunda_(town)); [SA History Online](https://sahistory.org.za/place/secunda)
- [CONFIRMED] Sasol commissioned Swiss architect Max Kirchhofer to design the town in 1974; he envisaged about 13,000 residents; it now has more than 40,000, "with many more living in the surrounding areas" (article dated January 2024). — [Bloomberg, 19 Jan 2024](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-01-19/south-african-town-that-rose-with-coal-faces-uncertain-future); [BizNews republication](https://www.biznews.com/news/sasols-secunda-town-built-coal-uncertain-future)
- [CONFIRMED] Planners chose to develop Trichardt and Secunda as one town named Secunda, while Evander remained a separate town. — [Wikipedia: Secunda](https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Secunda_(town))
- [CONFIRMED] Kirchhofer's own account (1982): the plant site is on the southern slopes of the Bankspruit valley, about 7 km south-west of Trichardt and 8 km south-east of Evander; the plant site was to reach 3.5 km x 2.5 km (8.75 km2). The recommended town site lay south of a provincial road then being built to link Evander and Trichardt, and was contiguous with Trichardt on the opposite side of the railway line. — [Kirchhofer, "The Planning of Sasolburg and Secunda", Town and Regional Planning special issue, 1982](https://journals.co.za/doi/pdf/10.10520/AJA1012280X_443) (mirror: [distantreader PDF](https://distantreader.org/stacks/journals/trp/trp-3171.pdf))
- [CONFIRMED] Kirchhofer describes tree belts marking "the break between work and leisure"; along provincial road P9-5 a high-pressure Sasol gas main (to Vanderbijlpark and the Witwatersrand) gave the opportunity for a tree belt 32 m wide. A separate network of pedestrian/cycle paths was to give safe movement inside residential areas, particularly to schools and local shops. — [Kirchhofer 1982](https://journals.co.za/doi/pdf/10.10520/AJA1012280X_443)
- [CONFIRMED] Secunda's design drew on garden cities, British new towns and the self-contained neighbourhood concept of Radburn; the planning team travelled to Britain and the USA to study practice. — [Geoforum paper, "Entrenching systems of dominance in urban form: Exploring the origins of inequality in Secunda, South Africa", ScienceDirect 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0016718524001866)
- [CONFIRMED] At a meeting on 19 July 1979, planners including Kirchhofer set out four social classes linked directly to housing-unit types and their locations in Secunda; higher-tier units were located adjacent to the highest-tier green strips, named in the paper as "Green Area" and "Duckpond". Secunda was given the lowest residential densities in the region and eMbalenhle the highest. — [Geoforum/ScienceDirect 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0016718524001866)
- [CONFIRMED] Census 2011 treats Secunda as a main place (code 866006) of 173.71 km2 with 23 sub-places. Named sub-places returned by search: Secunda SP1 (largest: 10,339 people on 5.01 km2), Secunda Ext 2 (1,674 people, 0.83 km2), Ext 6, Ext 7, Ext 8, Ext 9 (2,440 people), Ext 10, Ext 12, Ext 13 & 32, Ext 15, Ext 16, Ext 17, Ext 18, Ext 20 (2,481 people, 1.91 km2), Ext 22 (5,361 people, 2.78 km2; the most populous extension), Ext 28 (28 people), Ext 31 (17 people), and a sub-place called "Sasol Secunda" (the plant area). — [Adrian Frith Census 2011: Secunda main place](https://census2011.adrianfrith.com/place/866006); [Ext 22](https://census2011.adrianfrith.com/place/866006003); [Ext 20](https://census2011.adrianfrith.com/place/866006020); [Ext 2](https://census2011.adrianfrith.com/place/866006004); [Sasol Secunda sub-place map](https://census2011.adrianfrith.com/place/866006022/map)
- [CONFIRMED] "Sunset Park" exists as an estate-agent suburb name within Secunda, with freestanding 3-4 bedroom houses on stands of roughly 930-1,220 m2 priced about R0.9m-R2.1m in 2025 listings. — [MyProperty Sunset Park listing](https://www.myproperty.co.za/properties/sunset-park-house-for-sale-ent0308591); [Chas Everitt](https://chaseveritt.co.za/results/residential/for-sale/secunda/sunset-park/house/4146850); [Property Central](https://propertycentral.co.za/suburbs/3429/Sunset%20Park/property-for-sale)
- [SINGLE/WEAK] Other area/complex names appearing in property listings for Secunda: "Secunda Central" (used by MyProperty for much of the town), "Terra Nova" (secure lock-up-and-go complex/estate), "Kruinpark" (with a complex called Kruinsig Square), "Cosmos Valley" (described as a secure estate), "The One Secunda" (24-hour security complex), and "Evander Estate" (in Evander). — [Mail & Guardian property: townhouses to rent in Secunda](https://property.mg.co.za/townhouses-to-rent-in-secunda-c110); [Property24 townhouses for sale](https://www.property24.com/townhouses-for-sale/secunda/mpumalanga/110); [Mitula](https://homes.mitula.co.za/homes/security-complex-secunda)
- [UNVERIFIED] Listing URLs returned under a Secunda search also contained the names "Suidheuwel" and "Noordrand"; I could not confirm that these are Secunda suburbs (they may belong to another town on the same portal). — [M&G property listing URL](https://property.mg.co.za/4-bedroom-house-for-sale-in-suidheuwel-114992804); [MyProperty listing URL](https://www.myproperty.co.za/properties/noordrand-house-for-sale-rl13293)
- [CONFIRMED] Sub-place demographics differ: Ext 22 was 69.3% white and about 65% Afrikaans first-language in 2011; Ext 9 was about 82% white and 76% Afrikaans; Ext 15 was about 79% Afrikaans and 14% English. — [Adrian Frith: Ext 22](https://census2011.adrianfrith.com/place/866006003); [Ext 9](https://census.adrianfrith.com/place/866006010); [Ext 15](https://census.adrianfrith.com/place/866006009)
- [CONFIRMED] Municipal documents (2026 Voluntary Local Review) flag rapid informal settlement growth at "Peace Village", "Marikana" and "Extensions 33 and 34". Which town these extension numbers belong to was not clear from the snippet. — [Govan Mbeki VLR 2026](https://www.gold.uclg.org/sites/default/files/field-document/govanmbeki_2026.pdf)

### Inferences
- Because the town was proclaimed in numbered stages from 1976, low extension numbers (and "Secunda SP1") are almost certainly the original mid/late-1970s core and higher numbers later additions; this is the normal South African convention but I found no source giving a date for each extension.
- Estate-agent names (Sunset Park, Terra Nova, Kruinpark) are overlays on the numbered extensions; residents and police/municipal paperwork would use "Ext N", adverts use names. A game can safely use "Secunda Ext 22" style addresses.
- The Radburn/garden-city influence implies curving residential streets and culs-de-sac backing onto green strips with footpaths to schools and local shops, rather than a grid. The plant lies south-west of the town; Trichardt adjoins on the east/north-east across the railway; Evander is north-west.

### Gaps
- No source obtained for a map-level arrangement of extensions around the centre (which Ext is north/south/east/west), nor the date each extension was proclaimed. The Geoforum paper tabulates erf size and house size by income group and quadrant, but only the table headers were visible.
- Which extension numbers correspond to Sunset Park, Terra Nova or Kruinpark is unknown.
- The full list of all 23 census sub-places was not retrievable.

## 2. House types in Secunda

### Takeaway
The typical Secunda house is a single-storey family bungalow on a stand of roughly 850-1,300 m2, with the accretions of South African suburbia (pool, lapa with built-in braai, carports, Wendy house, flatlet, JoJo water tank). Journalists describe tan-brick bungalows in earth tones on tree-lined boulevards. Newer stock is townhouse complexes and gated estates with electric fencing, CCTV and guards.

### Cited Findings
- [CONFIRMED] Described in January 2024 as "neighborhoods of bungalows built with tan bricks and painted in earth tones" along a tree-lined boulevard. — [Bloomberg/BizNews 2024](https://www.biznews.com/news/sasols-secunda-town-built-coal-uncertain-future)
- [CONFIRMED] The town was built to house construction and operations staff for Sasol 2 and 3. — [BizNews 2024](https://www.biznews.com/news/sasols-secunda-town-built-coal-uncertain-future)
- [SINGLE/WEAK, estate-agent adverts 2024-2025] Stand sizes in listings: 866 m2, 929 m2 (house 235 m2), 959 m2, 1,008 m2, 1,221 m2, 1,303 m2. — [MyProperty Secunda](https://www.myproperty.co.za/en-za/for-sale/mpumalanga/secunda); [Property24 listing 117804792](https://www.property24.com/for-sale/secunda/secunda/mpumalanga/4787/117804792); [MyProperty Sunset Park](https://www.myproperty.co.za/properties/sunset-park-house-for-sale-ent0286363)
- [SINGLE/WEAK, adverts] Recurrent features in Secunda house adverts: swimming pool (one heated, one covered), lapa (one "tiled lapa with built-in braai"), double garage plus two to five carports, Wendy house(s), separate 2-bedroom flatlet, JoJo water tank; one listing explicitly a "face brick home" at R2.88m. Typical asking prices R1.4m-R2.9m for 3-5 bedroom houses. — [MyProperty Secunda Central 5-bed](https://www.myproperty.co.za/en-za/for-sale/house/mpumalanga/secunda/secunda-central/5-bedroom/rl3721); [MyProperty 3-bed](https://www.myproperty.co.za/en-za/for-sale/house/mpumalanga/secunda/secunda-central/3-bedroom/ent0316782); [MyProperty Sunset Park with JoJo tank](https://www.myproperty.co.za/properties/sunset-park-house-for-sale-rl4912)
- [SINGLE/WEAK, adverts] Townhouse/complex stock: a "Tuscan-style" townhouse with 24-hour CCTV and night guard; Terra Nova described as a gated-entrance secure complex with a children's play park; The One Secunda with access control, cameras and electric fencing; a secure estate near Secunda Mall. — [Property24 townhouses](https://www.property24.com/townhouses-for-sale/secunda/mpumalanga/110); [Mitula](https://homes.mitula.co.za/homes/security-complex-secunda); [M&G property](https://property.mg.co.za/townhouses-to-rent-in-secunda-c110)
- [SINGLE/WEAK] Seeff's Secunda office describes stock as family homes, townhouses, modern flats and apartments, farms and commercial, and stresses parks and trees. — [Seeff Secunda](https://www.seeff.com/contact/secunda/141/)
- [CONFIRMED] Some residents were moved into houses of 75-90 m2 built on subdivided stands (context: lower-tier housing in the Geoforum study). — [Geoforum/ScienceDirect 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0016718524001866)
- [CONFIRMED] Recent Sasol-built housing: Sasol Mining handed over 218 houses in its housing project and planned about 500 houses in eMbalenhle and 98 in Secunda by 2020; a 2026 municipal document says Sasol handed over 260 houses to qualifying employees in eMbalenhle and Secunda. — [Sasol: Mining hands over 218 houses](https://www.sasol.com/index.php/sasol-mining-hands-over-218-houses-part-its-housing-project); [Ridge Times, 9 Jun 2018](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2018/06/09/sasol-mining-continues-with-housing-project-in-embalenhle-and-secunda/); [Lowvelder Sasol feature](https://www.citizen.co.za/lowvelder/410203/sasol-secunda-operations-partnering-community-transformation/); [Govan Mbeki VLR 2026](https://www.gold.uclg.org/sites/default/files/field-document/govanmbeki_2026.pdf)

### Inferences
- Load-shedding and water-outage adaptations (JoJo tanks in adverts; inverters/solar likely) belong in a 2020-2026 depiction. Only the JoJo tank is sourced.
- The many carports and flatlets/Wendy houses on single stands suggest widespread subletting of rooms to contractors (see section 3).
- Precast concrete ("Vibracrete") walls, palisade fencing and electric fence strands are standard in South African highveld suburbs of this era and would not look wrong, but only electric fencing in complexes is sourced here.

### Gaps
- No source obtained specifying roof material (concrete tile vs corrugated/IBR sheet), storeys, boundary-wall type, or which exact house designs Sasol built in 1976-1982. "Single-storey" is implied by "bungalows" only.
- No sourced description of blocks of flats in Secunda beyond the existence of "flats and apartments".
- No sourced information on Sasol single quarters or contractor hostels inside Secunda itself.

## 3. Guesthouses and contractor accommodation

### Takeaway
Contractor lodging is a core local industry: guesthouses explicitly market to "contractors" and "Sasol shutdown" guests, and during big shutdowns all public accommodation in the area fills and Sasol has to house thousands itself.

### Cited Findings
- [CONFIRMED] Sasol's report on its 2010 shutdown says all available public accommodation in the Secunda area was fully occupied and Sasol housed about 3,900 shutdown staff in its own and arranged facilities. — [Sasol: "Sasol Secunda successfully executes largest shutdown of its kind"](https://sasol.com/sasol-secunda-successfully-executes-largest-shutdown-its-kind)
- [CONFIRMED] For the 2021 statutory shutdown, the on-site workforce was expected to rise to nearly 20,000 including service-provider staff. — [Sasol statement on Covid-19 controls for shutdown](https://www.sasol.com/sasol-statement-sasol-enhances-existing-preventative-workplace-covid-19-controls-its-upcoming)
- [SINGLE/WEAK, listings] Examples of the type: "Qhambalala Contractors Guesthouse" (Secunda); a guesthouse with seven self-catering en-suite flats; "The Lofts" self-catering apartments at Lake Umuzi with a "Sasol Shutdown 2024" page offering short, medium and long-term stays; Jabliano Guest House (work desk, internet); "19th on Bloekom" (walking distance to the Spar at "Secunda Village"); Grace View Guest House; Kuiersaam Gastehuis; Sunset View Lodge; Secunda BackPackers. — [Plek](https://plek.co.za/products/qhambalala-contractors-guesthouse-secunda-secunda-mpumalanga-south-africa); [Lake Umuzi: The Lofts](https://old.lakeumuzi.co.za/?p=4046); [LekkeSlaap: Jabliano](https://www.lekkeslaap.co.za/accommodation/jabliano-guest-house); [LekkeSlaap: 19th on Bloekom](https://www.lekkeslaap.co.za/accommodation/19th-on-bloekom); [SA-Venues: Grace View](https://www.sa-venues.com/visit/graceview/); [Tripadvisor: Secunda BackPackers](https://www.tripadvisor.co.za/Hotel_Review-g312637-d27153963-Reviews-Secunda_BackPackers-Secunda_Mpumalanga.html)
- [SINGLE/WEAK] The municipal draft housing sector plan cites "demand for accommodation linked to industrial activity" and backyard rental pressure as housing drivers. — [Govan Mbeki draft Housing Sector Plan 2026](https://www.govanmbeki.gov.za/wp-content/housing/housing_sector_plans2026_draft_strategy.pdf)

### Inferences
- Many guesthouses are converted suburban houses (names like "19th on Bloekom" are street-address names; "Bloekom" is a street name appearing in the listing title). Expect bakkies with company decals, rows of carports, and rooms added in back yards.
- Shutdown season is when a detective would find every bed taken and strangers in every suburb; the 2010 and 2021 Sasol figures support that.

### Gaps
- No count of guesthouses, no description of purpose-built contractor camps/villages, and no confirmed month for annual shutdowns were obtained.

## 4. eMbalenhle

### Takeaway
eMbalenhle is the apartheid-era black township built for Secunda's workforce in the late 1970s, deliberately placed on the far side of the plant and mines; it had about 119,000 people at Census 2011 (three times Secunda's population on a ninth of the land), is overwhelmingly Black African and roughly half isiZulu-speaking, and since 2017 has been the scene of repeated violent service-delivery protests and chronic sewage and water failures.

### Cited Findings
**History and position**
- [CONFIRMED] Established in the 1970s as a black-only township for neighbouring Secunda; a substantial part of its housing was built by Sasol for its workers. — [Wikipedia: Embalenhle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embalenhle); [Greenpeace Africa](https://www.greenpeace.org/africa/en/blogs/12888/where-theres-smoke/)
- [CONFIRMED, municipal document] Established in 1978 on the farm Langverwacht 282 IS as a dormitory town for Sasol workers. — [Govan Mbeki IDP 2011-12](https://cogta.mpg.gov.za/IDP/GertSibande2011-12/GovanMbeki2011-2012.pdf)
- [CONFIRMED] Secunda was separated from eMbalenhle by a large buffer zone, "approximately 17 km by fastest route"; Evander, the Winkelhaak Mines and the Sasol industrial site served as the buffer. — [Geoforum/ScienceDirect 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0016718524001866). Conflicts with Wikipedia's "10 km away from Secunda, linked by the R580" and "9 km southwest of Evander" — [Wikipedia: Embalenhle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embalenhle). (Likely straight-line vs road distance; treat 10 km as straight-line and ~17 km as the drive.)
- [CONFIRMED] The R546 runs between eMbalenhle and Evander (protesters planned to march on it in 2017). — [Ridge Times, 16 Oct 2017](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2017/10/16/breaking-news-embalenhle-protesters-torch-municipal-building-trucks/)

**Population and layout**
- [CONFIRMED] Census 2011: 118,889 people, 35,404 households, 19.65 km2 (about 1,802 households per km2); 99.0% Black African; first language Zulu 59,227 (49.8%), Xhosa 12,964, Sotho 12,002. — [Adrian Frith Census 2011: Embalenhle main place](https://census2011.adrianfrith.com/place/866011); [Wikipedia: Embalenhle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embalenhle)
- [CONFIRMED] 16 census sub-places, numbered like Secunda's: Embalenhle Ext 00 (9,071 people, 1.36 km2), Ext 4 (612 households), Ext 6 & 13 (840 people, smallest), Ext 7 (351 households), Ext 11 (3,064 households), Ext 15 (16,490 people), Ext 17 (418 households), Ext 18 (17,462 people, 4,570 households, the largest), Embalenhle SP (6,550 people). — [Adrian Frith: Embalenhle](https://census2011.adrianfrith.com/place/866011); [Ext 18](https://census2011.adrianfrith.com/place/866011017); [Ext 11](https://census2011.adrianfrith.com/place/866011001)
- [SINGLE/WEAK] Other population claims: the 2011-12 IDP put eMbalenhle at "approximately 210 000"; a World Population Review estimate cited on Wikipedia gives 172,256 for 2026. Neither is a census count. — [IDP 2011-12](https://cogta.mpg.gov.za/IDP/GertSibande2011-12/GovanMbeki2011-2012.pdf); [Wikipedia: Embalenhle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embalenhle)
- [CONFIRMED] Informal settlement areas are listed by ward in the municipality's draft housing plan, including parts of Extensions 4, 5, 7, 9 and 11, and Extension 18 (Ward 14). IDPs list basic-services/formalisation projects for Extensions 16, 18, 20, 24/25 and 25. Named places elsewhere in reporting: Ext 15 (reservoir), Ext 20, Ext 25 (pump station), and an area called "Wellas". — [Draft Housing Sector Plan 2026](https://www.govanmbeki.gov.za/wp-content/housing/housing_sector_plans2026_draft_strategy.pdf); [IDP 2022-2027](https://www.govanmbeki.gov.za/wp-content/reports/final_%20idp_2022_2027.pdf); [Ridge Times, 1 Mar 2025 (Wellas)](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2025/03/01/wellas-community-is-fed-up-with-sewage-2/)
- [CONFIRMED] IDP reviews list backyard dwellers, housing shortage and growing informal settlements as weaknesses; the municipality adopted a land invasion bylaw in 2020. — [IDP review 2021](https://www.govanmbeki.gov.za/wp-content/strategic_documents/idp_review_2021.pdf); [Draft IDP 2023](https://www.govanmbeki.gov.za/wp-content/strategic_documents/draft_idp_2023.pdf)
- [SINGLE/WEAK] DA statement (March 2024): RDP houses in Govan Mbeki still without water infrastructure. — [DA Mpumalanga, Mar 2024](https://mpumalanga.da.org.za/2024/03/govan-mbeki-rdp-houses-still-without-water-infrastructure)
- [SINGLE/WEAK, directory listings] A "Sasol Synfuels eMbalenhle Hostel" and "Sasol Flats, Embalenhle" exist as listed places. — [Yellosa](https://www.yellosa.co.za/company/450939/sasol-synfuels-embalenhle-hostel); [WorldPlaces](https://south-africa.worldplaces.me/view-place/81705713-sasol-flats-embalenhle.html)

**Landmarks and services**

### Inferences
- Density contrast is the key visual: about 6,000 people/km2 in eMbalenhle versus about 230/km2 across Secunda's (much larger, partly open) main-place area.
- The township sits south-west/west of the plant, so the plant's stacks and flare dominate its skyline, and commuting to Secunda town means going around the complex; the plant is nearer to eMbalenhle than to Secunda's suburbs.
- Present-day street scene supported by sources: grey water/sewage running in streets in several extensions, burnt-and-rebuilt municipal offices, a rebuilt mall, people carrying water containers during outages.

### Gaps
- No sourced description of house forms (four-room "matchbox" houses, RDP houses, shack materials, backyard rooms) or of hostel buildings; only their existence is supported.
- No named taxi rank, clinic, school, stadium or church in eMbalenhle was verified. No GroundUp, Daily Maverick or Mail & Guardian article specific to eMbalenhle was found.
- No Census 2022 figure for eMbalenhle found.
- Wikipedia cites the R580 as the link to Secunda; local press cites the R546 toward Evander. The exact road layout was not verified.

## 5. Evander, Trichardt, Kinross, Bethal, Leandra/Lebohang, Charl Cilliers

### Takeaway
Secunda sits in a cluster: Trichardt (a 1906 church village, now physically joined to Secunda's east), Evander (a 1955 gold-mine company town about 8 km north-west), Kinross (an older village 5 km north of Evander, name of the 1986 mine disaster), with eMbalenhle to the south-west, Bethal about 34 km east and Leandra about 32 km west along the N17.

### Cited Findings
- [CONFIRMED] **Trichardt**: on the N17, 34 km west of Bethal and 32 km east-south-east of Leandra, adjacent to Secunda; began as a Dutch Reformed Church settlement, proclaimed 1906; named after Carolus Johannes Tregardt (1811-1901), son of Voortrekker leader Louis Tregardt. Contiguous with Secunda across the railway line. — [Wikipedia: Trichardt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trichardt); [Kirchhofer 1982](https://journals.co.za/doi/pdf/10.10520/AJA1012280X_443)
- [SINGLE/WEAK] Mediclinic Highveld private hospital (24-hour emergency centre) is listed in Trichardt, on Barney Molokwane Street, about 7.5 km from Secunda; another directory gives "3 Bethal Rd". — [LekkeSlaap: Mediclinic Highveld](https://www.lekkeslaap.co.za/attractions/mediclinic-highveld); [Polomap](https://za.polomap.com/secunda/240)
- [CONFIRMED] **Evander**: about 8 km north-west of Secunda; founded 1955 when Union Corporation began gold mining; originally part of Bethal district; named after Evelyn Anderson, wife of a Union Corporation director. Considered for incorporation into Secunda but kept separate. — [Wikipedia: Evander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evander,_South_Africa)
- [SINGLE/WEAK] Evander population: one source says about 10,200, another about 16,500 (conflicting; neither verified against census). — [Mzansi Mirror: Evander](https://mzansimirror.com/evander/); [Mindtrip](https://mindtrip.ai/location/evander-mpumalanga/evander/lo-b4XcsdY4)
- [CONFIRMED] The "Winkelhaak Mines" lie between Secunda and eMbalenhle as part of the buffer. — [Geoforum/ScienceDirect 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0016718524001866)
- [SINGLE/WEAK] The Evander mine was acquired by Harmony Gold from Gold Fields in 1998 (per a 2015 Sunday Times piece; current ownership not verified in this research). — [Sunday Times via PressReader, 15 Nov 2015](https://www.pressreader.com/south-africa/sunday-times-1107/20151115/281908772037837)
- [CONFIRMED] **Kinross mine disaster**: 16 September 1986, fire at Kinross gold mine near Evander killed 177 miners and injured 235; accounts say a welding/acetylene incident about 1,600 m underground at No. 2 Shaft; the NUM held a day of mourning on 1 October 1986. — [Wikipedia: Kinross mining disaster](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Kinross_mining_disaster); [SA History Online](https://sahistory.org.za/dated-event/memorial-service-victims-mining-disaster); [Minerals Council: We remember Kinross](https://mineralscouncil.org.za/industry-news/we-care-we-remember/176-we-remember-kinross)
- [CONFIRMED] **Kinross**: small gold-mining town 5 km north of Evander, on the Atlantic/Indian Ocean watershed, 42 km west of Bethal and 19 km east of Leslie; proclaimed a village 1915, municipal status about 1965; four gold mines in the region; Census 2011 main place 15,246 people, of which the old village core "Kinross SP" had only 1,873. — [Wikipedia: Kinross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinross,_Mpumalanga); [Adrian Frith: Kinross](https://census2011.adrianfrith.com/place/866004); [Kinross SP](https://census2011.adrianfrith.com/place/866004005)
- [CONFIRMED] **Bethal**: farming town in Govan Mbeki municipality (maize, sunflower, sorghum, rye, potatoes); known for potatoes; its National Potato Festival was discontinued in 2007. About 34 km east of Trichardt/Secunda. Seat of the regional court that fined the municipality in 2023. — [Wikipedia: Bethal](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethal); [Wikipedia: Trichardt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trichardt)
- [CONFIRMED] **Leandra**: formed from the former towns of Eendrag and Leslie (name is a blend); about 32 km west-north-west of Trichardt. Wikipedia gives Census 2011 population 2,023 on 9.63 km2 (this appears to be the old town only, excluding the adjoining township). — [Wikipedia: Leandra](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Leandra,_Mpumalanga)
- [CONFIRMED] **Charl Cilliers**: small settlement about 32 km north of Standerton, formerly Van Tondershoek, renamed 1917 after Voortrekker Sarel Cilliers. — [Wikipedia: Charl Cilliers](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Charl_Cilliers,_Mpumalanga)

### Inferences
- Kinross main place (15,246) being eight times the size of its village core implies most residents live in an attached township/mine housing; the name of that township was not verified (the brief's "Thistle Grove" could not be confirmed).
- Evander, being a 1950s mine town, will have older, smaller mine houses and mature trees compared with Secunda; headgears and slimes dams on the horizon are plausible but were not directly sourced.
- Distances for an in-game map: Secunda to Trichardt 0-5 km (joined), to Evander ~8 km NW, to Kinross ~13 km N/NW, to eMbalenhle 10 km straight / ~17 km by road SW, to Bethal ~35 km E, to Leandra ~35-40 km W. Charl Cilliers lies to the south; its distance from Secunda was not sourced.

### Gaps
- No verified description of Evander's streetscape, shafts or headgear; no confirmation of which shafts still operate or who owns them today (another researcher covers the mines).
- Lebohang (Leandra's township) and eMzinoni (Bethal's township) could not be verified in this session; no populations obtained.
- Thistle Grove (Kinross) unverified.
- No census language breakdown obtained for Evander, Trichardt or Kinross.

Secunda's suburban life is anchored by three public high schools (two Afrikaans-rooted, one English-medium), Curro private schools, two Mediclinic hospitals, the Sasol recreation club, and the Lake Umuzi waterfront/Duck Pond; the town was about 70% Afrikaans-speaking in 2011 while eMbalenhle is about half isiZulu.

## 7. Population and demographics: Census 2011 and Census 2022

### Takeaway
Govan Mbeki municipality grew from 294,538 (2011) to 310,117 (2022). In 2011 Secunda had 40,198 people (about 72% white, 70% Afrikaans) and eMbalenhle 118,889 (99% Black African). No 2022 place-level figures were found.

### Cited Findings
- [CONFIRMED] Govan Mbeki Local Municipality: 221,747 (2001), 294,538 (2011), 310,117 (2022). — [CityPopulation.de, from Stats SA](https://citypopulation.de/en/southafrica/admin/mpumalanga/MP307__govan_mbeki)
