## 1. Work rhythm: shifts, transport, clothing, contractors, unions, strikes

### Takeaway
The documented labour picture is one of a huge permanent-plus-contractor workforce that swells by thousands during annual maintenance shutdowns, several competing unions split partly along racial and occupational lines, and recurring friction over who gets jobs (locals versus "outsiders", permanent versus labour-broker staff). Concrete details of shift times, bus routines and overall colours could not be sourced this session.

### Cited Findings
- [CONFIRMED] Shutdown scale: Sasol's 2021 Secunda maintenance shutdown was expected to involve around 20,000 people; an earlier (2010) shutdown created 14,500 temporary job opportunities with over 100 external service providers, contractors and sub-contractors. — [Sasol media release, 2021 shutdown](https://sasol.com/media-centre/media-releases/sasol-maintenance-secunda-shutdown-2021); [Sasol, largest shutdown of its kind](https://www.sasol.com/sasol-secunda-successfully-executes-largest-shutdown-its-kind)
- [CONFIRMED] Unions with recognition at Sasol Mining (signatories to the 2016 three-year wage agreement): NUM, CEPPWAWU, SACWU and Solidarity. AMCU was outside that agreement and struck. — [Engineering News, 10 Nov 2016](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/unions-sasol-mining-ink-wage-deal-amcu-members-go-on-strike-2016-11-10); [NUM statement](https://num.org.za/news-reports-speeches/posts/num-concludes-a-3-year-minimum-wage-agreement-with-sasol-mining/)
- [PRE-2018 CONTEXT] AMCU strike at Sasol Mining, Secunda, from August 2016: about 1,600 workers downed tools after wage talks deadlocked and the CCMA issued a certificate of non-resolution; AMCU demanded a R12,500 monthly minimum; Sasol said unionised packages were already above that; Sasol said it would use its Labour Relations Act rights to end the strike. — [IOL Business Report, 25 Aug 2016](https://iol.co.za/business-report/companies/2016-08-25-sasol-workers-down-tools/); [Sowetan, 25 Aug 2016](https://www.sowetanlive.co.za/business/2016-08-25--sasol-workers-in-secunda-go-on-strike/)
- [CONFIRMED] 2018 Solidarity strike over Sasol Khanyisa: Solidarity declared a dispute in January 2018 over the exclusion of white employees from Khanyisa Phase 2 (a black-employee share ownership scheme). 83% of Solidarity members at Sasol Secunda voted for a strike (late August 2018). — [Ridge Times, 29 Aug 2018](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2018/08/29/83-of-white-sasol-workers-vote-yes-for-strike/); [Engineering News, 27 Aug 2018](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/majority-of-solidarity-members-at-sasol-secunda-in-favour-of-proposed-strike-2018-08-27)

- [CONFIRMED] During the strike the national bargaining council suspended Sasol's overtime licence, citing health and safety concerns (17 Sep 2018). — [Fin24, 17 Sep 2018](https://www.fin24.com/Companies/national-bargaining-council-suspends-sasols-overtime-licence-due-to-health-safety-concerns-20180917)
- [CONFIRMED] Sasol states that the CCMA mediation with Solidarity was "brought to a close"; a Ridge Times item of about 10 November 2018 reports Solidarity members getting the go-ahead (via Nedlac) for a legal protest strike. Terms of any settlement were not visible. — [Sasol statement](https://sasol.com/sasolsolidarity-ccma-mediation-brought-close); [Ridge Times, 10 Nov 2018](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2018/11/10/solidarity-members-get-go-ahead-legal-strike/)

- [PRE-2018 CONTEXT / CONFIRMED 2018] Unemployed eMbalenhle residents repeatedly protested at Sasol gates (named gates: Charlie 1, Charlie 3) over jobs and labour brokers in 2015-2017; in February 2018 Sasol said it was willing to engage locals. — [Ridge Times, 14 Jan 2016](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2016/01/14/unemployed-protest-against-sasol/); [Ridge Times, 23 Feb 2017](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2017/02/23/locals-desperately-need-jobs/); [Ridge Times, 19 Feb 2018](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2018/02/19/sasol-is-willing-to-engage-with-locals/)
- [CONFIRMED, DATE UNCLEAR] One protest against Sasol and labour brokers turned violent: an "eMbalenhle Community Forum" allegedly convened it, workers not from the area were targeted, and buses carrying people to work were attacked. — [Ridge Times](https://citizen.co.za/ridge-times/?p=108152)
- [CONFIRMED] Megabus is a bus operator carrying commuters in the area and was the other party in the February 2020 clash with the Vukanini Taxi Association (see section 6). — [The Citizen, Feb 2020](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/south-africa/violence-erupts-in-embalenhle-and-evander-as-megabus-vukta-taxi-association-clash)
- [CONFIRMED] Workers also use lift clubs (shared private cars); in February 2025 alleged taxi association members tried to stop passengers using them, and Sasol said it does not condone intimidation because employees may choose their own transport. — [Ridge Times, 7 Feb 2025](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/local-news/2025/02/07/alleged-taxi-association-members-try-to-stop-passengers-from-using-lift-clubs/)
- [CONFIRMED] A fatality occurred at Sasol Secunda Operations on 16 April 2026 (cause not visible in extract). A Business Times news analysis of 29 Aug 2026 is headlined "Deadly Secunda plant puts Sasol at crossroads". — [Sasol business performance metrics, 9 months to 31 Mar 2026](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/1466741/business-performance-metrics-for-the-nine-months-ended-31-march-2026-and-revised-guidance); [Business Times, 29 Aug 2026](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/business/2026-08-29-news-analysis-deadly-secunda-plant-puts-sasol-at-crossroads/)

### Inferences
- The town has an annual rhythm as well as a daily one: "shutdown season" brings in thousands of outside contract artisans and labourers, fills rented backyard rooms in eMbalenhle, and is when unions have most leverage (Solidarity timed 2018 to it). The 2015 landlord quote shows renting rooms to contractors is a household income stream.
- "Local versus outsider" is a live fault line: community forums have demanded that locals be hired first and have targeted non-local workers and buses.
- Union identity carries social meaning: Solidarity's membership is largely white and skilled (the 2018 strike was reported locally as "white Sasol employees strike"), while NUM, CEPPWAWU, SACWU and AMCU organise mainly black workers. A character's union says something about them.
- Plant gates have names residents use as landmarks (Charlie 1, Charlie 3).

## 2. Money: wages, Sasol's weight in the economy, unemployment, informal lending, debt

### Takeaway
Sasol is the local economy: sources put 70-80% of Secunda as dependent on it directly or indirectly, while eMbalenhle next door has high unemployment. Card-holding loan sharks are well documented across Mpumalanga but no Secunda/eMbalenhle-specific case was found.

### Cited Findings
- [CONFIRMED] "Around 80% of Secunda works for Sasol in one form or another," per a Sasol vice-president quoted by Bloomberg (19 Jan 2024); the article gives the town population as about 40,000 and says Secunda was built about 50 years ago as a company town on top of underground mines whose deposits are dwindling. — [Bloomberg, 19 Jan 2024](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-01-19/south-african-town-that-rose-with-coal-faces-uncertain-future); [BizNews reprint](https://www.biznews.com/news/sasols-secunda-town-built-coal-uncertain-future)
- [CONFIRMED] A 2026 report covered by Mining Weekly says more than 70% of Secunda's residents are directly or indirectly dependent on Sasol. — [Mining Weekly, 29 May 2026](https://www.miningweekly.com/article/report-highlights-challenges-risks-facing-sasol-amid-the-just-energy-transition-2026-05-29/)
- [CONFIRMED] Govan Mbeki Local Municipality population: 310,117 (Census 2022), up from 294,538 (Census 2011). — [citypopulation.de, Stats SA census table](https://citypopulation.de/en/southafrica/admin/mpumalanga/MP307__govan_mbeki)
- [CONFIRMED, YEAR UNCLEAR] Govan Mbeki expanded-definition unemployment rate: 34.9%, in a Wits water-services profile (year of the figure not visible). — [Wits Mandela Institute WSA profile](https://www.wits.ac.za/media/wits-university/faculties-and-schools/commerce-law-and-management/research-entities/mandela-institute/documents/research-publications/WRT_WSA%20Profile_Mpumalanga_Govan%20Mbeki_Narrative.pdf)
- [CONFIRMED, ATTRIBUTION UNCLEAR] A March 2020 Business Day/Bloomberg feature on eMbalenhle appears to cite 29% unemployment in the eMbalenhle context. — [Business Day, 17 Mar 2020](https://www.businesslive.co.za/bd/national/2020-03-17-sasols-complicated-and-dangerous-relationship-with-the-people-of-embalenhle/)
- [PRE-2018 CONTEXT] Wage floor reference points at Sasol Mining: minimum salary R8,300/month from 1 Dec 2016, R8,800 from 1 Jul 2017, R9,300 from 1 Jul 2018, under the 2016 three-year deal (7% or R700, whichever greater, for those earning R8,300-R10,723). — [NUM](https://num.org.za/news-reports-speeches/posts/num-concludes-a-3-year-minimum-wage-agreement-with-sasol-mining/); [SA Labour News](https://salabournews.co.za/34823-four-unions-excluding-amcu-sign-three-year-wage-agreement-with-sasol-mining)
- [CONFIRMED, PROVINCIAL] Mashonisas (unregistered lenders) holding borrowers' bank cards, SASSA grant cards, PINs and ID books is a documented Mpumalanga pattern: Hawks raid in Mbombela, May 2024, six arrested, bank cards, SASSA cards and IDs seized; two suspects arrested in Witbank CBD in 2023 with cards; a SAPS administration clerk in Hazyview found with 140 bank and SASSA cards in 2023, convicted 2025, no jail time; one "loan shark" found with about 500 bank cards by the Hawks (Nov 2021). — [Mpumalanga News, 21 May 2024](https://www.citizen.co.za/mpumalanga-news/news-headlines/local-news/2024/05/21/update-hawks-raid-alleged-mbombela-loan-sharks-offices-confiscate-id-books-and-bank-cards/); [Daily News, 1 Oct 2025](https://dailynews.co.za/news/crime-and-courts/2025-10-01-saps-employee-thrived-as-loan-shark-found-with-140-bank-and-sassa-cards); [TimesLive, 6 Nov 2021](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/south-africa/2021-11-06-hawks-snatch-500-bank-cards-from-loan-shark/); [Daily Sun, 7 Mar 2023](https://www.dailysun.co.za/dailysun/news/abomashonisa-bust-with-sassa-cards-20230307)
- [CONFIRMED, PROVINCIAL] Typical legal outcome is light: Mpumalanga loan sharks found with SASSA and bank cards were fined R10,000 in one case; holding cards or IDs as security and lending without National Credit Regulator registration are offences under the National Credit Act. — [IOL](https://www.iol.co.za/news/south-africa/mpumalanga/loan-sharks-found-in-possession-of-sassa-and-bank-cards-slapped-with-r10k-fine-2018247c-d5e4-4ca5-8637-940bbf8bcd35); [Mpumalanga News](https://citizen.co.za/mpumalanga-news/?p=403804)
- [CONFIRMED] Hollywoodbets (a betting chain) has a Secunda branch that sponsored the local football club's kit in 2019/2020, showing sports betting is part of the local money landscape. — [Hollywood Foundation](https://hollywoodfoundation.co.za/programmes/sponsorship/soccer-sponsorships/secunda-stars-fc)

### Inferences
- The gap between a Sasol permanent pay packet and eMbalenhle's unemployment is the core economic tension. A permanent Sasol job is the prize; contract work via a broker is the realistic route; nothing at all is common.
- A mashonisa who keeps a borrower's card and PIN and withdraws on payday is a believable, documented mechanism for a character's debt trap, but it must be written as a provincial pattern, not a reported Secunda case.

## 3. "Business forums", procurement mafias, coal/diesel/cable theft, illegal mining at Evander

### Takeaway
Illegal gold mining around Evander's old shafts and cable theft in Secunda are well documented locally. Business-forum extortion is documented in Mpumalanga and nationally (the "30% demand"), but no confirmed extortion incident at Sasol Secunda was found; what is documented locally is organised pressure on Sasol over procurement access.

### Cited Findings
- [CONFIRMED] How business forums operate (national pattern): groups arrive at sites demanding that 30% of contract value go to forum members; industry guidance has moved toward negotiating with them; the Global Initiative published a study in 2022. — [GI-TOC, "Extortion or Transformation?", 2022](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/GITOC-Extortion-or-Transformation-The-construction-mafia-in-South-Africa.pdf); [Engineering News, 27 Jun 2023](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/is-sa-inc-fighting-the-construction-mafia-or-adapting-to-incorporate-it-2023-06-27); [The Citizen](https://www.citizen.co.za/business/companies-extortion-normalised-construction-mafia)
- [CONFIRMED, PROVINCIAL] A 2023 Daily Maverick count listed 15 construction-mafia incidents in Mpumalanga, far fewer than Gauteng and KwaZulu-Natal. — [Daily Maverick, 2 Jul 2023](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2023-07-02-murderous-construction-mafias-have-brought-many-companies-to-their-knees-with-saps-help-the-fightback-has-started)
- [CONFIRMED, PROVINCIAL, NOT SECUNDA] October 2024: a contractor abandoned an R11m road project in Siyabuswa (Dr JS Moroka municipality) after threats; the local business forum's chair denied intimidation and said the forum wanted local businesses to benefit. — [Sunday Times, 6 Oct 2024](https://www.sundaytimes.timeslive.co.za/sunday-times/news/2024-10-06-mpumalanga-business-forum-forces-contractor-to-abandon-r11m-road-project/)
- [CONFIRMED] Extortion targeting the mining sector is reported nationally (News24, Dec 2022; S-RM risk note, June 2025). — [News24, 9 Dec 2022](https://www.news24.com/business/companies/extortion-mafia-hits-sas-r947-billion-mining-sector-20221209); [S-RM, 30 Jun 2025](https://www.s-rminform.com/global-risk-hub/something-for-nothing-extortion-in-south-africas-mining-industry)

Reported as a demand for inclusion, not as extortion. — [Ridge Times, 6 Oct 2020](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2020/10/06/local-youth-chamber-of-commerce-and-industry-demands-changes-in-sasol/)

### Inferences
- A fictional business forum in this setting is plausible if framed the way real ones frame themselves: as local empowerment ("locals must benefit") shading into intimidation. The 2020 youth chamber march shows the legitimate end of that spectrum in Secunda itself.

- Private security (Stratcon) takes part in police operations against illegal miners, blurring public and private policing.

## 4. Crime and policing as experienced locally

### Takeaway
Local reporting shows armed business robberies, house robberies and hijackings, answered by a dense web of private armed-response firms, a Community Policing Forum and AfriForum volunteers coordinating over the Zello push-to-talk app, often arriving alongside or ahead of SAPS.

### Cited Findings

## 9. Climate, seasons, land and air

### Takeaway
Secunda is a cold-winter, summer-thunderstorm Highveld town at about 1,600 m. Its air is the most heavily documented local health issue: eMbalenhle ranked among South Africa's worst for air quality, residents describe a rotten-egg smell, and a September 2026 study attributes about 1,000 deaths a year across the Highveld to the plant's emissions (an NGO estimate Sasol contests in general terms).

## 10. The energy transition and the town's future

### Takeaway
Sasol still says it will cut emissions 30% by 2030 but has slashed the money for doing so, written down the Secunda refinery, and struggled with poor coal from its own mines and a looming gas shortfall. Residents and analysts ask what a single-employer town does when its anchor shrinks; documented sentiment in Mpumalanga coal communities is distrust that any "just transition" will reach them.

### Inferences
- By 2025-2026 the company line has shifted from "decarbonise Secunda" to "keep Secunda running and fix the coal". For a resident this reads as reprieve mixed with dread: the plant is not closing soon, but the refinery is worth nothing on the books, people have died there, and nobody believes replacement industries are coming.
- The municipality's insolvency and Sasol's uncertainty compound each other: the one institution that could plan a post-Sasol town cannot keep sewage in the pipes.
