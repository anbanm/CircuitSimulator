## 1. The plant as seen from town

### Takeaway
The skyline is dominated by one 301 m four-flue concrete chimney at the steam plant (the tallest structure in the area), a group of natural-draught cooling towers, two main flares and steam plumes from 17 coal-fired boilers and 84 Lurgi-type gasifiers. The plant sits roughly 3 km south-south-west of Secunda town, with eMbalenhle township about 5 km to its west. The cooling tower count, the gate names and the plant headcount were not verified.

### Cited Findings

**Chimney**
- [Confirmed] The tallest structure in Secunda is the 301 m chimney at the Sasol Synfuels plant — [Wikipedia, Secunda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secunda,_South_Africa)
- [Confirmed] It is the steam-plant (Sasol III) chimney, an "Eskom type" multi-flue stack: four refractory-brick-lined concrete flues of 4.6 m diameter inside a concrete windshield of 26 m internal diameter. Built by Concor with Hochtief as part of the Synfuels steam-plant contract — [Wikiwand, Sasol II](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Sasol_II); [SkyscraperPage](https://skyscraperpage.com/b6525)
- [Conflict] One source gives 301 m flues; another gives a 292 m windshield with four 300 m flues. Use "about 300 m" or "301 m" — both are defensible — [Wikiwand, Sasol II](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Sasol_II); [SkyscraperPage](https://skyscraperpage.com/b6525)

**Cooling towers, boilers, gasifiers, flares**
- [Confirmed] The utility cooling towers are natural-draught towers built in the late 1970s and early 1980s. IWC won a R200 million contract in May 2009 to refurbish them; the article refers to "its four towers" (it is unclear whether four is the site total or the contract scope) — [Mining Weekly, 11 Feb 2014](https://www.miningweekly.com/article/iwc-performs-online-refurbishment-of-utility-cooling-towers-at-sasol-secunda-2014-02-11)
- [Confirmed] The steam plants have 17 boilers: Steam Plant West (Unit 43) has eight Babcock boilers; Steam Plant East (Unit 243) has eight Babcock boilers plus a ninth built by ICAL — [Sasol, Secunda Operations Synfuels document, 2025](https://www.sasol.com/sites/default/files/2025-03/Secunda%20Operations%20Synfuels.pdf)
- [Single source] 84 fixed-bed dry-bottom gasifiers, 42 at Sasol II and 42 at Sasol III; the original design had 80 and four were added by the "Additional Mark IV Fixed Bed Gasifier Project"; only 65 to 70 were said to be operating over a four-year period (date of that statement unclear) — [Portfolio Planning PLUS, Secunda CTL](https://portfolio-pplus.com/SiteMains/Details/1367)
- [Confirmed] The main flare system is two flares, one West and one East, with two relief headers. There are also local flares at the coal-lock units (010/210) — [SRK, Atmospheric Impact Report: Sasol Secunda Facility, 2021](https://cdn-web-content.srk.com/upload/user/image/ANNEXURE_A_SYNFUELS_Atmospheric_Impact_Report20210831154201071.pdf); [Sasol, Secunda Operations Synfuels document](https://www.sasol.com/sites/default/files/2025-03/Secunda%20Operations%20Synfuels.pdf)
- [Confirmed] The plant is laid out as mirrored West and East halves (Sasol Two and Sasol Three), which is why units are numbered in pairs (43/243, 010/210) — [Sasol, Secunda Operations Synfuels document](https://www.sasol.com/sites/default/files/2025-03/Secunda%20Operations%20Synfuels.pdf)

**Size and position**
- [Confirmed] Sasol's atmospheric emission licence lists the Synfuels operation's extent as 24.05 km² (a licensed extent, not a surveyed footprint) — [Sasol, Secunda Operations Synfuels document](https://www.sasol.com/sites/default/files/2025-03/Secunda%20Operations%20Synfuels.pdf)
- [Confirmed] An environmental impact report places Secunda town about 3 km NNE of the plant and eMbalenhle about 5 km west of it. So from town the plant is to the south-south-west — [RHDHV, Sasol gas turbine EIR](http://www.rhdhv.co.za/media/201210/EIR/Sasol%20SGP-Final%20EIR.pdf)
- [Conflict] Other distances for eMbalenhle: 9 km west of "Secunda CTL" (Mapcarta) and 10 km from Secunda town (Wikipedia). The differences are reference points — [Mapcarta](https://mapcarta.com/W476656895); [Wikipedia, Embalenhle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embalenhle)
- [Confirmed] The plant is about 140 km east of Johannesburg — [CREA health impact assessment, Sept 2026](https://energyandcleanair.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/CREA_HIA_Sasol-Secunda_09.2026.pdf)
- [Confirmed] Town residential areas are planned "quarter cells" bordered by main roads and green strips; the refinery is "within sight — and often earshot — of the town" — [Wikipedia, Secunda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secunda,_South_Africa)

**History and names**
- [Confirmed] Sasol Two was commissioned on 1 March 1980 and Sasol Three on 10 May 1982 — [Wikipedia, Secunda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secunda,_South_Africa)
- [Conflict] Wikipedia's Secunda CTL article instead says Sasol II was constructed in 1980 and Sasol III in 1984 — [Wikipedia, Secunda CTL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secunda_CTL)
- [Confirmed] Fluor was the EPC contractor for Sasol II — [Fluor](https://www.fluor.com/projects/sasol-ii-oil-from-coal-epc)
- [Confirmed] Current usage: "Secunda Operations" (Sasol's name, formerly Sasol Synfuels); "Secunda CTL" is the common outside label — [Sasol, Secunda Operations Synfuels document](https://www.sasol.com/sites/default/files/2025-03/Secunda%20Operations%20Synfuels.pdf); [Wikipedia, Secunda CTL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secunda_CTL)

**Scale of throughput**
- [Confirmed] About 35 million tonnes of coal a year go into the gasifiers (another source gives 37.3 Mt a year to Sasol Synthetic Fuels) — [Oxpeckers, Sept 2026](https://oxpeckers.org/2026/09/carbon-colossus/); [Portfolio Planning PLUS](https://portfolio-pplus.com/SiteMains/Details/1367)
- [Confirmed] Sasol group headcount was 27,411 in 2025 (group-wide, not Secunda) — [Wikipedia, Sasol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sasol)

**Smell**
- [Confirmed] Government has attributed regional "rotten egg" stench episodes to hydrogen sulphide and named Sasol Secunda Operations as a significant source; monitoring stations showed elevated peaks in Secunda, Springs, Irene, Midstream and Buccleuch. Sasol says its emissions are within licence limits — [gov.za, DFFE statement on sulphurous odour](https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/forestry-fisheries-and-environment-sulphurous-odour-across-parts-gauteng-12); [Daily Maverick, 9 June 2022](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2022-06-09-something-rotten-this-way-comes-sulphur-stench-in-gauteng-and-north-west-investigated)
- [Single source] An eMbalenhle resident recalled growing up with the stench of "rotten eggs" in the 1980s — [Oxpeckers, Sept 2026](https://oxpeckers.org/2026/09/carbon-colossus/)

**Emissions and air-quality rulings**
- [Claim, widely repeated] Secunda is described as the world's largest single-point source of greenhouse gas: 56.5 Mt CO2 a year as of 2020 (Wikipedia); Just Share calls it "the largest single point source emitter of GHGs on earth"; News24 headlines it as the "world's biggest emitter" — [Wikipedia, Secunda CTL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secunda_CTL); [Just Share](https://justshare.org.za/deadly-air-corporate-power/); [News24, 1 Aug 2023](https://www.news24.com/business/companies/sasol-appeals-against-greenhouse-gas-ruling-on-worlds-biggest-emitter-20230801)
- [Confirmed] Sasol reported 63.9 Mt CO2-equivalent group emissions in 2022, with 80 to 85% from Secunda — [The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/south-africas-massive-sasol-petrochemical-plant-faces-serious-challenges-new-report-241823)
- [Confirmed] "Deadly Air" case: in March 2022 the Pretoria High Court held that air pollution in the Highveld Priority Area breaches residents' constitutional right to a healthy environment. The area holds 12 Eskom coal power stations and Sasol's Secunda refinery — [Daily Maverick, 25 Mar 2022](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2022-03-25-south-african-court-rules-that-clean-air-is-a-constitutional-right-this-is-what-the-country-needs-to-change)
- [Confirmed] The Supreme Court of Appeal handed down judgment on 11 April 2025, dismissing the minister's appeal with costs — [Daily Maverick, 27 May 2025](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2025-05-27-sca-moved-environmental-rights-forward-but-left-childrens-rights-behind)
- [Confirmed] Sasol applied for an alternative load-based sulphur dioxide limit for the Secunda boilers, was refused by the National Air Quality Officer and appealed to the minister in 2023 — [Sasol media release](https://www.sasol.com/media-centre/media-releases/sasol-submits-appeal-minister-forestry-fisheries-and-environment-national-air-quality-office); [News24, 1 Aug 2023](https://www.news24.com/business/companies/sasol-appeals-against-greenhouse-gas-ruling-on-worlds-biggest-emitter-20230801)
- [Claim] A September 2026 CREA / groundWork / Just Share report says Secunda still runs under "alternative" SO2 limits, emitted an estimated 112 kt of SO2 in 2024, and links its pollution to roughly 1,000 deaths a year — [CREA press release, Sept 2026](https://energyandcleanair.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Press-release_CREA_HIA_Sasol-Secunda_09.2026.pdf); [Scrolla.Africa](https://scrolla.africa/sasol-pollution-linked-to-1000-deaths-a-year/); [Business Day, 7 Sept 2026](https://www.businessday.co.za/news/2026-09-07-sasols-carbon-colossus-is-a-killer-says-report/)
- [Confirmed] A proposed regulation would cut the allowable hydrogen sulphide limit from 3,500 mg/m³ to 600 mg/m³ and make H2S a criteria pollutant — [Mail & Guardian, 12 Mar 2026](https://mg.co.za/the-green-guardian/2026-03-12-residents-mull-source-of-pong/)

### Inferences
- A viewer in town looking south-south-west sees the stack and towers at about 3 km: close enough to dominate the horizon and to be heard on a still night.
- Because the site is two mirrored plants, an artist can draw two of most things (two flares, two steam plants, two gasification blocks), with a single tall chimney on the east (Sasol III) side.
- Seventeen coal boilers and the cooling towers mean permanent white plumes; they will be largest and most visible on cold, still Highveld winter mornings (general physics, not sourced).

## 2. The annual maintenance shutdown

### Takeaway
Sasol runs a statutory maintenance shutdown at Secunda every year in late winter or spring; the one precisely dated example ran 30 August to 24 September 2021. Large "total" shutdowns bring in well over ten thousand extra workers and fill every bed in the area. Figures for 2024 to 2026 were not found.

### Cited Findings
- [Confirmed] The 2021 shutdown ran from 30 August to 24 September 2021 and was described locally as a yearly event — [Ridge Times, 31 Aug 2021](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2021/08/31/sasols-yearly-shutdown-begins/); [Sasol media release, 2021](https://sasol.com/media-centre/media-releases/sasol-maintenance-secunda-shutdown-2021)
- [Confirmed] The 2021 shutdown was planned at 1.3 million man-hours and 102,000 activities. Sasol warned the town to expect an influx of people and heavier traffic — [Energy Voice](https://www.energyvoice.com/oilandgas/africa/petrochemicals-africa/346547/sasol-shutdown-secunda-covid/); [Sasol media release, 2021](https://sasol.com/media-centre/media-releases/sasol-maintenance-secunda-shutdown-2021)
- [Confirmed] In the 2010 shutdown ("the largest of its kind") about 14,500 additional workers came in, over 100 external service providers and contractors were involved, public accommodation in the Secunda area was fully occupied, and Sasol housed 3,900 shutdown workers in its own and arranged facilities — [Sasol, "largest shutdown of its kind"](https://www.sasol.com/sasol-secunda-successfully-executes-largest-shutdown-its-kind)
- [Single source, old] One local page says Sasol Synfuels takes part of the plant down every year in August for three weeks — [ShowMe Secunda](https://showme.co.za/secunda/news/biggest-shutdown-in-history-expected-at-sasol-secunda/)
- [Confirmed] Shutdowns come in two kinds: a "phase shutdown" (part of the plant) and a "total shutdown" of the East or West factory. A contractor post names the "Sasol Secunda Eastern Total and Phase Shutdown 2022" — [TMS Group, LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/tmsgroupindustrialservices_sasol-secunda-eastern-total-and-phase-shutdown-activity-6981586959362084864-mJ4i)
- [Single source] A 2017 article said a full plant shutdown comes round every eight years — [Engineering News, 27 Oct 2017](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/supplier-prepares-for-maintenance-shutdown-2017-10-27)
- [Confirmed] Unit-level shutdowns also run separately, for example a Rectisol shutdown and a sulphur-unit shutdown in 2024 — [Aurex Constructors](https://www.aurex.com/projects/rectisol-shutdown-2024/)
- [Confirmed] A fatal working-at-heights incident occurred on a warehouse roof at Sasol in 2023, and a 2023 ash-conveyor fatality occurred at Secunda Operations — [SAPIA learning from incident](https://fuelsindustry.org.za/download/257/fatal-incidents-learnings/13950/sapia-lfi-sasol-working-at-heights-fatality-2023.pdf); [Sasol learning from incident](https://www.sasol.com/sites/default/files/2024-10/Learning_IC_fatality.pdf)

### Inferences
- "September shutdown" is a safe phrase for a game: the documented case ran late August to late September. A setting with full guesthouses, contractor bakkies and buses, and traffic at shift change is consistent with the 2010 and 2021 reports.
- The contractor count scales with the type: phase shutdowns are smaller, total shutdowns are the 10,000-plus events.

### Gaps
- Exact dates and contractor numbers for 2024, 2025 and 2026: not found.
- Effects on rents, crime, bars and traffic beyond Sasol's own warning: no local reporting retrieved.

## 3. The collieries that feed the plant

### Takeaway
Sasol Mining's Secunda complex is a ring of underground bord-and-pillar mines linked to the plant by very long overland conveyors (21 km from Shondoni, 27 km from Impumelelo). The older Brandspruit, Middelbult and Twistdraai mines were replaced by Impumelelo, Shondoni and Thubelisha. Bosjesspruit and Syferfontein remain.

### Cited Findings
- [Confirmed] The original five Secunda underground mines were Bosjesspruit, Brandspruit, Middelbult, Syferfontein and Twistdraai — [Mail & Guardian, 17 Oct 2007](https://mg.co.za/article/2007-10-17-sasol-strike-enters-fourth-day-fuel-output-spared/)
- [Confirmed] Replacement programme (R14 to R15.3 billion): Impumelelo replaced Brandspruit; Shondoni replaced Middelbult; Thubelisha took over from Twistdraai as its export shafts were mined out — [Mining Weekly, 8 June 2017](https://www.miningweekly.com/article/sasol-on-track-with-r153-billion-mine-replacement-project-2017-06-08); [Engineering News, 26 Sept 2013](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/sasol-minings-r14bn-coal-projects-rollout-under-way-in-mpumalanga-2013-09-26); [Engineering News, 25 Oct 2013](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/sasol-minings-coal-to-liquids-horizon-extending-to-2050-2013-10-25)
- [Confirmed] Sasol's 2025 SEC technical exhibit lists the Brandspruit and Twistdraai environmental management programmes as in decommissioning — [Sasol Form 20-F Exhibit 96.1, FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/314590/000141057825001910/ssl-20250630xex96d1.htm)
- [Confirmed] **Shondoni**: "the longest single-flight conveyor without an intermediate booster drive in Africa", 21 km, to Sasol Coal Supply — [Sasol Mining page](https://sasol.com/index.php/what-we-do/energy/sasol-mining); [Engineering News, 13 July 2018](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/shondoni-coal-project-south-africa-2018-07-13)
- [Confirmed / Conflict] **Impumelelo**: a 27 km single-flight conveyor to the central coal-handling facilities (Sasol's site); 2013 reporting said 26 km — [Sasol Mining page](https://sasol.com/index.php/what-we-do/energy/sasol-mining); [Engineering News, 15 Nov 2013](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/advanced-conveyor-developed-for-sasol-mining-2013-11-15)
- [Confirmed] **Bosjesspruit**: current production is at the Irenedale shaft, connected to Bosjesspruit Main shaft by a 17.9 km underground conveyor — [Sasol Mining page](https://sasol.com/index.php/what-we-do/energy/sasol-mining)
- [Single source, dated] **Thubelisha**: total conveyor length about 20 km (circa 2012) — [IOL](https://www.iol.co.za/business-report/companies/sasol-inaugurates-new-mining-shaft-1301989)
- [Confirmed] **Syferfontein**: underground areas named Riversdale, Tweedraai and Weltevrede — [Sasol Mining page](https://sasol.com/index.php/what-we-do/energy/sasol-mining); [Mining Weekly, Tweedraai project, 13 Jan 2017](https://www.miningweekly.com/article/tweedraai-coal-project-south-africa-2017-01-13)
- [Confirmed] **Middelbult** got a new shaft with its own conveyor in 2016 — [Engineering News, 21 Oct 2016](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/conveyor-belt-delivers-coal-from-new-shaft-at-middelbult-operation-2016-10-21)
- [Confirmed] Coal converges on **Sasol Coal Supply (SCS)**, which has East and West stockpile areas (SCS(E), SCS(W)) feeding the two halves of the plant — [WSP geotechnical scoping report, 2026](https://www.wsp.com/-/media/service/south-africa/2026-documents/za0068671-2768---sasol-g2p-dsr/09-appendix-g1_geotechnical-scoping-report_part-1.pdf)
- [Confirmed] The **Twistdraai Export Plant**, a dense-medium separation plant since 1997, was converted into a 10 Mt a year destoning plant for Bosjesspruit and Thubelisha coal. It reached beneficial operation in December 2025 and was officially opened on 13 March 2026 at under R1 billion — [Engineering News, 13 Mar 2026](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/sasol-launches-destoning-plant-to-improve-coal-quality-strengthen-operational-performance-2026-03-13); [Engineering News, 14 Mar 2025](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/repurposed-twistdraai-destoning-plant-key-to-sasols-plan-for-recovering-secundas-output-2025-03-14)

### Inferences
- A fictional private colliery would not have a 20 km conveyor. It would truck coal by road to a buyer's stockpile or to a rail siding, which is also where the documented truck-swap frauds happen (section 9).
- The names Irenedale, Riversdale, Tweedraai and Weltevrede are farm names: mines and shafts here are named after the farm portion they sit on. An invented colliery should follow that pattern (a farm name plus "Colliery").

### Gaps
- **What a shaft complex looks like on the surface** (incline versus vertical shafts, headgear, silos, fans, change houses): no source retrieved. Do not state as fact that Sasol shafts lack headgear.
- Exact location and distance from town of each colliery: not retrieved.
- Whether Middelbult has fully closed or was absorbed into Shondoni: implied, not confirmed.
- Current production per mine and workforce per mine: in the SEC exhibit, which could not be opened.

## 4. Independent miners, third-party suppliers, mine sales and due diligence

### Takeaway
Sasol does buy coal from outside its own mines: about 5 Mt a year from Thungela's Isibonelo colliery under a 20-year contract, plus open-market purchases that grew during the 2021 to 2025 quality crisis. That gives a small independent supplier a realistic buyer. No documented sale of a colliery near Secunda to a small buyer was found; the examples of junior acquisitions come from elsewhere in Mpumalanga.

### Cited Findings
- [Confirmed] Sasol bought about 5.2 Mt a year from Isibonelo (Thungela, formerly Anglo American) — [Meridian Economics](https://transitionpetrochemicals.meridianeconomics.co.za/coal-3/)
- [Conflict] End of the Isibonelo contract: a Sasol investor deck (Sept 2023) says the 20-year supply ends June 2025; other sources say 2026 when the mine is mined out; the FY2025 SEC exhibit still lists Isibonelo as a supply source — [Sasol investor site visit, mining update, 21 Sept 2023](https://www.sasol.com/sites/default/files/2023-09/Secunda%20Site%20visit_Mining%20update_21%20September%202023.pdf); [Sasol Exhibit 96.1, FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/314590/000141057825001910/ssl-20250630xex96d1.htm)
- [Confirmed] Sasol's 2023 deck flagged a Secunda coal shortfall after Isibonelo and listed external purchases and new sources as options — [Sasol investor site visit, 21 Sept 2023](https://www.sasol.com/sites/default/files/2023-09/Secunda%20Site%20visit_Mining%20update_21%20September%202023.pdf)
- [Confirmed] In December 2021 Sasol said it would cover its shortfall by buying coal on the open market — [News24, 14 Dec 2021](https://www.news24.com/fin24/companies/industrial/sasol-secunda-production-drops-over-significant-coal-supply-shortfall-20211214)
- [Confirmed] In April 2025 Sasol said it would buy in more, higher-quality coal and cut its own production by a further 2 Mt until the destoning plant was running — [Engineering News, 17 Apr 2025](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/sasol-to-buy-in-more-coal-amid-ongoing-quality-problems-at-own-mines-2025-04-17)
- [Confirmed] Junior acquisitions in Mpumalanga (not at Secunda): Ingwe (BHP Billiton) sold Delmas Colliery to junior Kuyasa Mining in 2002; Coal of Africa agreed in 2014 to sell the idled Mooiplaats colliery to private Blackspear Holdings for US$23.47 million, and a later sale closed at R179.9 million to a consortium; Black Royalty Minerals, a junior, operates Koornfontein — [BHP release](https://www.bhp.com/-/media/bhp/documents/investors/news/delmasmediarelease.pdf?la=en); [Mining Weekly, 22 Sept 2014](https://www.miningweekly.com/article/coal-of-africa-sells-idled-mooiplaats-for-235m-2014-09-22); [Mining Technology](https://www.mining-technology.com/news/coal-closes-sale-mooiplaats-colliery-south-africa/); [Wikipedia, Black Royalty Minerals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Royalty_Minerals)
- [Confirmed] SAMREC requires public reports to be signed off by a registered "Competent Person". For coal, that person must be a registered professional natural scientist with SACNASP and recognised as a Competent Person (coal) — [Wikipedia, South African Mineral Reporting Codes](https://www.Wikipedia.org/wiki/South_African_Mineral_Reporting_Codes); [SAMCODE, coal downloads](https://www.samcode.co.za/samcode-ssc/samrec/coal)
- [Confirmed] The companion standard for coal is SANS 10320 (first edition 2004; a 2017 second edition; the Isibonelo report cites SANS 10320:2020). It includes drill-spacing guidance — [Isibonelo Technical Report, 31 Dec 2020](https://minedocs.com/22/Isibonelo-TR-12312020.pdf); [Wits thesis](https://wiredspace.wits.ac.za/bitstreams/1a454554-4d10-48a1-90d3-2e0a99c9ed3d/download)
- [Confirmed] Under these rules "inventory coal" may not be publicly reported, and exploration results may not be folded into public resource or reserve statements — [SAMCODE, coal downloads](https://www.samcode.co.za/samcode-ssc/samrec/coal)
- [Confirmed] A published competent person's report exists for Isibonelo (dated 31 December 2020) and is a real-world template for what such a document contains — [Isibonelo Technical Report](https://minedocs.com/22/Isibonelo-TR-12312020.pdf)

### Inferences
- A plausible fraud premise: a family colliery being sold (or pitching a supply contract to Sasol during the shortage) on the strength of a competent person's report whose borehole data understate dolerite or overstate quality. The buyer's due diligence would re-log core and re-assay retained samples, so the core shed and the laboratory certificates are the evidence.
- A privately held mine is not obliged to publish a SAMREC-compliant report (the code governs public reporting by listed companies), but buyers and banks ask for one. This is an inference from the "public report" wording.

- No verified example of a small family-owned colliery in the Secunda / Govan Mbeki area, or of a Sasol-area colliery sold to a small buyer.
- Seriti, Exxaro or contract-miner activity specifically near Secunda: not found.
- The steps of a mine sale (section 11 ministerial consent under the MPRDA, data room, site visit): not researched.

## 5. Underground mining methods, shifts, depth and safety

### Takeaway
Sasol's Secunda mines are bord-and-pillar operations using continuous miners, shuttle cars and roof bolters on the No. 4 Lower seam, at an average depth of about 135 m. A 2009 court record gives three overlapping ten-hour shifts. The worst recent accident was three deaths at Bosjesspruit in October 2021.

### Cited Findings
- [Confirmed] All five Secunda mines used bord-and-pillar; Sasol was also studying longwall panels and larger continuous miners with 20 t shuttle cars in place of the 16 t cars then in use — [Engineering News, 17 June 2016](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/sasol-targeting-step-change-in-coal-mining-productivity-2016-06-17)
- [Confirmed] Sasol job adverts list licensed operators for roof bolter, shuttle car, continuous miner, load-haul-dump and tractor, with roof and sidewall control and cutting sequence as duties — [Sasol careers](https://jobs.sasol.com/job/Sasolburg-Licensed-Machine-Operator-Roof-bolter%2C-Shuttle-Car%2C-Continuous-Miner%2C-Load-Haul-Dump-&-Tractor/1264174001)

## 6. Exploration drilling, core, logging and analysis

### Takeaway
Coal exploration in South Africa uses diamond core drilling (NQ at 47.6 mm or TNW at 60.5 mm core), with core laid in trays, logged by lithology and coal brightness, sampled by ply and sent for proximate analysis. A registered Competent Person signs the resource statement. Core-shed practice and laboratory sign-off details were only partly sourced.

### Cited Findings
- [Confirmed] Core diameters used in South African coalfields: NQ (47.6 mm) and TNW (60.5 mm) — [Hancox and Götz, "South Africa's coalfields", 2014](https://repository.up.ac.za/bitstream/2263/58453/1/Hancox_South_2014.pdf)
- [Confirmed] A hyperspectral study used a 44 m, 60 mm diameter core from the Witbank coalfield: bright bands of varying thickness within dull coal; bright coal is vitrinite-rich, dull coal inertinite-rich — [International Journal of Coal Geology, 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0166516224000132)
- [Confirmed] Lithologies logged in Witbank, Ermelo and Waterberg cores: coaly shale, dull banded coal and bright coal; samples taken of coal plus the rock directly above and below — [SAIMM Journal, 2024](https://scielo.org.za/scielo.php?script=sci_arttext&pid=S2225-62532024001000004&lng=en&nrm=iso&tlng=en)
- [Confirmed] Bright coal is occasionally laminated and may contain disseminated pyrite — [Hancox and Götz, 2014](https://repository.up.ac.za/bitstream/2263/58453/1/Hancox_South_2014.pdf)
- [Confirmed, Australian standard used as reference] Brightness codes on freshly broken core: bright (over 90% bright), bright with dull bands (60 to 90%), interbanded (40 to 60%), down to dull (under 1% bright) — [ACARP CoalLog training manual](https://www.ausimm.com/globalassets/coallog/coallog-v2.0-training-manual.pdf)
- [Confirmed] South African unit standards exist for preparing borehole core for logging and sampling "from core containers" — [SAQA unit standard 376139](https://allqs.saqa.org.za/showUnitStandard.php?id=376139)
- [Confirmed] Quality measures used in the Secunda dolerite studies: ash, volatile content and dry-ash-free volatiles, from proximate analysis — [University of Pretoria, Bussio MSc](https://repository.up.ac.za/handle/2263/29300)
- [Confirmed] Sasol's Secunda geological database holds more than 8,000 boreholes — [Bussio and Roberts, 2016](https://repository.up.ac.za/bitstream/handle/2263/55850/Bussio_Large_2016.pdf?sequence=1)
- [Confirmed] Sign-off is by a Competent Person registered with SACNASP (see section 4) — [SAMCODE](https://www.samcode.co.za/samcode-ssc/samrec/coal)
- [Confirmed] Commercial coal laboratories named in South African disputes: SABS, SGS South Africa and Sibonisiwe Laboratories — [Mail & Guardian, 30 Aug 2016](https://mg.co.za/article/2016-08-30-tegeta-coal-quality-hopelessly-noncompliant-says-report/)

### Inferences
- What a writer can safely describe: black cylinders of core about 60 mm across, alternating glassy bright bands and matt dull bands, grey sandstone and shale partings, the odd brassy pyrite speck, laid in rows in metre-long trays marked with borehole number and depths.
- Devolatilised ("burnt") coal next to dolerite and the dolerite itself (a hard, dark, fine crystalline rock) are visually distinct in core. So swapping or relabelling trays is a physically checkable fraud.
- Proximate analysis reports moisture, ash, volatile matter and fixed carbon, with calorific value and sulphur measured separately. This is standard laboratory practice, not from a retrieved source.

### Gaps
- Drilling rig type, drilling contractor practice, wireline geophysical logging, core-shed layout and retention periods, tray labelling conventions: no South African source retrieved.
- Float-and-sink (washability) testing and "sinks" measurement method: not sourced.
- Who signs a laboratory certificate (SANAS-accredited laboratory signatory): not sourced.
- The text of SANS 10320 was not seen.

## 7. Geology: the Highveld coalfield and dolerite

### Takeaway
The economic seam is the No. 4 Lower: about 3.7 m thick on average, gently dipping, 40 to 241 m deep. Jurassic dolerite sills and dykes cut the field, step the seam up or down by as much as 137 m and bake the coal beside them. Sasol's recent feedstock trouble, however, was reported as stone contamination ("sinks") and productivity, not dolerite.

### Cited Findings
- [Confirmed] No. 4 Lower seam: weighted average thickness 3.7 m, thinner to the south and thicker to the west near pre-Karoo basement highs; base at 40 to 241 m, average 135 m — [Sasol Exhibit 96.1, FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/314590/000141057825001910/ssl-20250630xex96d1.htm)
- [Conflict, older] The 2003 Form 20-F gave thickness from 0 to 10 m with a weighted average of 3.30 m — [Sasol Form 20-F, 2003](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/314590/000104746903007864/a2104529z20fr12b.htm)
- [Confirmed] The seam floor dips gently north to south at about 0.5 degrees — [Sasol Form 20-F, 2016](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/314590/000104746916015748/a2229729z20-f.htm)
- [Confirmed] Highveld coalfield: the No. 4 seam is the most economically important; No. 4 Lower is 4 to 12 m thick, separated from No. 4 Upper by 2 to 15 m of sandstone — [CSIR, McGill 2015](https://researchspace.csir.co.za/bitstream/handle/10204/8153/McGill_2015.pdf)
- [Confirmed] Dolerite: dykes 1 to 4 m wide, widespread, mainly trending east-west; nine sills, four of them major; seam displacements of up to 137 m — [CSIR, McGill 2015](https://researchspace.csir.co.za/bitstream/handle/10204/8153/McGill_2015.pdf)
- [Confirmed] At Secunda the sills are numbered (DO4, DO8, DO10). The No. 8 sill intersects the No. 4 Lower seam many times, with the Shondoni reserve area most affected. The No. 4 sill reaches 75 m thick but does not intersect the seam in the Secunda area. "Numerous thinner dolerites cross-cut the coal seams" — [Sasol Exhibit 96.1, FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/314590/000141057825001910/ssl-20250630xex96d1.htm); [University of Pretoria, Bussio MSc](https://repository.up.ac.za/handle/2263/29300)
- [Confirmed] A palynology study of the lower No. 4 seam near Secunda found a devolatilised zone 17 to 19 m wide beside a dolerite dyke — [University of Pretoria](https://repository.up.ac.za/handle/2263/50780)
- [Confirmed, counter-finding] Bussio and Roberts (2016), using more than 8,000 Secunda boreholes, found no correlation between dolerite position or thickness and coal quality as measured by proximate analysis. They suggest emplacement dynamics and hydrothermal fluids control the aureole, and that proximate analysis may be too crude — [Bussio and Roberts, 2016](https://repository.up.ac.za/bitstream/handle/2263/55850/Bussio_Large_2016.pdf?sequence=1)
- [Confirmed] Bussio's 2020 PhD put the thermal influence at about 5 m from the intrusion — [University of Pretoria](https://repository.up.ac.za/handle/2263/85553)
- [Confirmed] A 1987 Twistdraai study found metamorphic effects largely restricted to the visible contact aureole, with rank rising and volatile matter falling toward the contact — [University of Pretoria repository](https://repository.up.ac.za/items/159ffbfc-5929-415d-ad5b-dbdaec27c35d/full)
- [Confirmed, comparison site] At Ogies a sill caused a local rise in rank; devolatilised areas near intrusions were higher in ash and moisture — [University of the Free State](https://scholar.ufs.ac.za/items/c4c5bbe2-1d85-4341-a0a8-8bcb23bf1ed7)
- [Confirmed, comparison site] Exxaro's Dorstfontein: sills and dykes produce devolatilisation "from minimal to extensive" — [Exxaro mineral resources report, 2018](https://investor.exxaro.com/integrated-reports2018/mrr/dorstfontein-complex.php)

**Sasol's coal quality problems, 2021 to 2026**
- [Confirmed] December 2021: Secunda output guidance cut because of "lower than expected coal availability and coal quality" — [Sasol SENS, 14 Dec 2021](https://senspdf.jse.co.za/documents/SENS_20211214_S455302.pdf)
- [Confirmed] February 2023: coal quality and mining productivity still constraining Secunda — [Mining Weekly, 21 Feb 2023](https://www.miningweekly.com/article/sasols-coal-problems-continue-to-undermine-output-at-secunda-2023-02-21)
- [Confirmed] The problem is "sinks": stone and other non-coal material in the feed, at times as high as 20% from Bosjesspruit and Thubelisha. It lowers gasifier yield and causes mechanical wear — [Engineering News, 14 Mar 2025](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/repurposed-twistdraai-destoning-plant-key-to-sasols-plan-for-recovering-secundas-output-2025-03-14); [Sasol destoning fact sheet, July 2025](https://www.sasol.com/sites/default/files/2025-07/Sasol%20Fact%20Sheet%20_%20Destoning%20at%20Sasol%20Mining.pdf)
- [Confirmed] April 2025: mining output forecast cut to 28 to 30 Mt; own production reduced by a further 2 Mt; more coal bought in — [Engineering News, 17 Apr 2025](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/sasol-to-buy-in-more-coal-amid-ongoing-quality-problems-at-own-mines-2025-04-17)
- [Confirmed, company-reported] After destoning began, average sinks fell below 14% in the first quarter of FY2026 against a 12 to 14% target. Sasol expects to reopen mine sections closed for poor quality and cut third-party purchases — [Engineering News, 12 Sept 2025](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/sasol-expects-coal-destoning-investment-to-lift-secunda-volumes-2025-09-12); [Engineering News, 13 Mar 2026](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/sasol-launches-destoning-plant-to-improve-coal-quality-strengthen-operational-performance-2026-03-13)

### Inferences
- Dolerite hurts a mine in three separable ways, and a plot can use any of them: (1) a sill that cuts through the seam removes coal and steps the seam to a different elevation, so the mine must cut expensive stone ramps or abandon the block; (2) dykes are hard rock walls that continuous miners cannot cut economically, breaking panels into smaller blocks; (3) coal within metres of the contact is devolatilised and worth less to a gasifier.
- The academic disagreement is useful: the big dataset found no clean statistical link, while local studies show aureoles of 5 m to 19 m. So the effect is local and can only be found by close-spaced drilling, which is exactly what a seller could omit, or misreport, in a handful of boreholes.
- Mining thin or disturbed seam drags in roof and floor stone, which is a plausible mechanism for high sinks. No retrieved source says dolerite caused Sasol's sinks problem; do not state that link as fact.

### Gaps
- No documented case was found of dolerite specifically shortening the life of a named Secunda mine.
- Faults, floor rolls and roof conditions: not retrieved.
- The 20% sinks and 14% figures are Sasol's own; no independent check.

## 9. Frauds and scandals that could inform the plot

### Takeaway
There is strong documentary basis for a coal-quality fraud: the Brakfontein / Tegeta affair (rival laboratories, suspended scientists, samples taken without observers), organised truck-swapping at illegal coal yards, weighbridge fraud and "business forum" extortion. All documented cases concern Eskom supply, not Sasol.

### Cited Findings
- [Confirmed] Brakfontein (Gupta-linked Tegeta), 2015: Sibonisiwe Laboratories failed 15 of 30 samples; SABS failed 29 of 30. Eskom's coal scientist Dr Mark van der Riet and a laboratory services manager were suspended in September 2015; he testified he had been kept away from sampling — [Mail & Guardian, 30 Aug 2016](https://mg.co.za/article/2016-08-30-tegeta-coal-quality-hopelessly-noncompliant-says-report/); [Mail & Guardian, 26 Feb 2019](https://mg.co.za/article/2019-02-26-eskom-was-at-a-loss-after-coal-scientists-suspension/)
- [Confirmed] Eskom suspended two laboratories, SGS SA and Sibonisiwe; the trade department asked SABS to investigate possible collusion or manipulation at its Mpumalanga laboratory — [Politicsweb](https://politicsweb.co.za/politics/dti-takes-action-on-possible-irregular-sarbs-tests); [Treasury, Fundudzi report, Nov 2018](https://www.treasury.gov.za/comm_media/press/2018/Final%20Report%20-%20Fundudzi%20-%20Eskom%2015112018.pdf)
- [Claim] Tegeta alleged that Eskom staff colluded with laboratory staff to extort a bribe — [Mail & Guardian, 30 Aug 2016](https://mg.co.za/article/2016-08-30-tegeta-coal-quality-hopelessly-noncompliant-says-report/)
- [Confirmed] The Special Investigating Unit told Parliament in October 2022 that officials ensured samples were taken with no Eskom observer present and facilitated swapping of samples, and that mines and officials colluded with transporters to mix poor coal with good — [Business Day, 19 Oct 2022](https://www.businesslive.co.za/bd/national/2022-10-19-siu-tells-parliament-of-organised-crime-behind-coal-contracts-at-eskom/)
- [Confirmed] Eskom's 2023 submission to Parliament describes coal adulterated in transit and "black sites" where good coal is replaced with poor — [Eskom SCOPA submission, 26 Apr 2023](https://pmg.org.za/files/230426Submission_PDF_SCOPA_Final.pdf)
- [Confirmed] November 2022, Matla power station: a driver and his supervisor arrested with substandard coal swapped at a known illegal coal yard — [News24, 30 Nov 2022](https://www.news24.com/business/economy/more-eskom-arrests-as-coal-truck-driver-supervisor-apprehended-at-matla-station-20221130)
- [Confirmed] June 2024, Arnot power station: a load turned out to be discard coal containing stones after a swap at a Middelburg coal yard — [SAnews](https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/alleged-coal-thief-apprehended-arnot-power-station)

- [Confirmed] By mid-2023 police counted 126 arrests and about R1 billion recovered, including 42 firearms, 83 loads of coal and mining machinery — [gov.za](https://www.gov.za/blog/fight-against-coal-syndicates-gaining-ground)
- [Confirmed] November 2023: a R1.5 billion asset seizure order against illegal coal mining; a separate report of R600 million coal-theft charges against a mining firm and its directors — [Daily Maverick, 21 Nov 2023](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2023-11-21-r1-5-billion-multi-pronged-asset-seizure-order-sees-authorities-tighten-illegal-coal-mining-noose); [Sunday World](https://sundayworld.co.za/business/r600m-coal-theft-charges-hit-mining-firm-directors/)
- [Confirmed] "Business forums" act as lobby networks that organise extortion and take payment as contracts for members; they also demand jobs for affiliates. Eleven suspects were arrested in November 2024 in Sekhukhune, Limpopo — [S-RM, 30 June 2025](https://www.s-rminform.com/global-risk-hub/something-for-nothing-extortion-in-south-africas-mining-industry)
- [Confirmed] "Procurement mafia" is the phrase of Northam Platinum's chief executive: mobilising the community "for its own devices, which is really straightforward extortion" — [BusinessTech](https://businesstech.co.za/news/?p=650351)

### Inferences
- Realistic fraud mechanics, each with a documented precedent: sampling with no independent observer; two laboratories giving opposite results; suspension of the scientist who objects; swapping a truckload at a roadside yard; false weighbridge tickets; a rival using police or a "forum" to pressure a mining-right holder.

### Gaps
