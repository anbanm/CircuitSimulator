## 1. Scale and trend (GI-TOC assassination monitoring, 2000 to 30 September 2026)

### Takeaway
GI-TOC's media-derived database holds more than 2 500 incidents since 2000: a taxi-driven plateau of roughly 50 to 90 a year until 2014, a steep climb to a peak of 211 in 2018, then 124 to 145 a year, and 205 in the first nine months of 2026 (partly a monitoring artefact). The mix has shifted from taxi-dominated (46% of 2000 to 2022) to organised crime as the largest category (2025 to 2026), with political killings at a record 45 incidents before the November 2026 local elections.

### Cited Findings
**Definition and method**
- A hit requires a specific target and an arrangement between the person who ordered it and the person who carried it out, for money or other gain; attempted and thwarted hits are counted in the same series. Four motive categories: political, taxi, organised crime, personal. [FULL] — [BB26](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Julian-Rademeyer-Ballots-and-bullets-Targeted-killings-and-South-Africas-2026-local-government-elections-GI-TOC-October-2026.pdf)
- The data is an acknowledged undercount: built from press (SABINET) searches; misses rural cases, Western Cape gang hits, and hits disguised as robberies, hijackings or accidents; some are recognised as hits only years later in court. [FULL] — [BK23](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Rumbi-Matamba-The-business-of-killing-Assassinations-in-SA.-April23-web.pdf); [RG18](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/The-rule-of-the-gun_Assassination-Witness.pdf)
- SAPS publishes no separate statistic for contract killings; they sit inside the murder total. [FULL] — [BK23](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Rumbi-Matamba-The-business-of-killing-Assassinations-in-SA.-April23-web.pdf)

**What the researchers conclude about causes**
- Violence has become "a monetizable commodity"; hired killing is "moonlighting" by a pool of trained muscle. Drivers named: illegal firearms, recruitment pools in the under-regulated taxi industries of KZN, Eastern Cape and Western Cape and in Western Cape gangs, crippled police intelligence and detective capacity, slow courts. [FULL] — [BK23](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Rumbi-Matamba-The-business-of-killing-Assassinations-in-SA.-April23-web.pdf)
- Political killings cluster around municipal elections: 110 of 191 election-year political cases (2000 to 2023) fell in municipal election years. A councillor's seat brings salary, influence over housing lists and jobs, and proximity to procurement. [FULL] — [BB26](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Julian-Rademeyer-Ballots-and-bullets-Targeted-killings-and-South-Africas-2026-local-government-elections-GI-TOC-October-2026.pdf)
- "Collaborative criminal governance": politicians and officials collude with criminal actors; hitmen kill for money, not territory. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 21 May 2024](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2024-05-21-sas-elections-stand-out-globally-for-very-real-threat-of-violence-assassination-report/)
- Until about 2022 political assassination was "almost entirely an intra-party [ANC] problem"; it has since widened to the MK Party (at least 8 members killed in 2026), EFF, IFP, DA, BOSA and civic movements. [FULL] — [BK23](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Rumbi-Matamba-The-business-of-killing-Assassinations-in-SA.-April23-web.pdf); [BB26](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Julian-Rademeyer-Ballots-and-bullets-Targeted-killings-and-South-Africas-2026-local-government-elections-GI-TOC-October-2026.pdf)

### Inferences
- For a story set in 2026, "about two to three recorded hits a week nationally, more before the elections" is defensible; the true figure is higher because disguised personal hits and gang hits are undercounted.
- The 2026 jump (205 in nine months) should not be written as a tripling of killings; GI-TOC itself attributes part of it to better monitoring.

### Gaps
- No full-year 2026 total yet. No official SAPS count exists at all.
- I could not open the Politics of Murder (2024) PDF, so its 2023 category table and "108% rise in a decade" figure are second-hand.
- Province-by-category totals for 2026 (BB26 Figure 3) did not extract reliably, so no all-category provincial totals for 2026 are given.
- No per-year provincial table for 2000 to 2014 was found in text form.

## 2. The supply side: who the hitmen are, pay, middlemen, guns, vehicles

### Takeaway
Research consistently locates the labour pool in the minibus-taxi industry (especially KZN), hostels, Western Cape gangs and serving or former police and security personnel, with KZN "exporting" gunmen to other provinces. Reported fees run from a few thousand rand for domestic hits to six figures for political or underworld targets, often split among several men, and guns come overwhelmingly from domestic leakage, including police stock.

### Cited Findings
**Who they are**
- Hitmen are "often recruited from ... armed gangs in the Western Cape and the minibus-taxi industry, particularly in KZN and the Eastern Cape. And, in some cases, perpetrators are hired from law enforcement agencies." KZN is "notorious for supplying hitmen to various other provinces and sometimes other countries". [FULL] — [BK23](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Rumbi-Matamba-The-business-of-killing-Assassinations-in-SA.-April23-web.pdf)
- "Reservoirs of violence" named: the taxi industry, hostel systems, gang-affected areas. [FULL] — [MC21](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/GITOC-Murder-by-Contract-Targeted-Killings-in-eastern-and-southern-Africa-.pdf)
- Parts of the taxi industry "have supplied firearms and experienced shooters for political and organized crime hits". [FULL] — [BB26](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Julian-Rademeyer-Ballots-and-bullets-Targeted-killings-and-South-Africas-2026-local-government-elections-GI-TOC-October-2026.pdf)
- "Izinkabi" is the KZN term for hitmen; a UCT-linked study said they tend to stay in urban hostels; monitor Mary de Haas said hostels are ideal places to hide hitmen ("We saw this in Glebelands"); Gauteng taxi bosses hire KZN gunmen for their discretion. ### Inferences
- A realistic crew is small (two to six men), often from outside the province, paid a modest sum split several ways, with a local organiser between them and the person paying. The gunmen frequently do not know who the ultimate client is; this is consistent with shooters being convicted while clients are not.
- Low prices reflect labour supply, not professionalism; the same men appear in robberies (Ncengwa was already serving time for armed robberies).

- Nothing verifiable was found on security-company firearms in specific hit prosecutions, or on cloned (as opposed to false or stolen) plates in a named case.

## 3. The demand side: who orders hits and why

### Takeaway
Clients are overwhelmingly insiders: taxi rivals, party comrades competing for seats and the procurement access that comes with them, officials and contractors threatened by audits, and family members after insurance money. The Moerane Commission (2018) identified tender access as the main root cause of political murder in KZN.

### Cited Findings
- **Taxi routes and association power.** Hits target taxi bosses, drivers and association committee members over routes, ranks and internal leadership; 2018's Gauteng surge was tied to the Kempton Park Taxi Association. In 2026 the Western Cape CATA v CODETA conflict produced 23 incidents; CODETA board member Eugene "Spoed" Titus survived an ambush on 20 February and was shot dead five days later outside a school in Atlantis. [FULL] — [MC21](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/GITOC-Murder-by-Contract-Targeted-Killings-in-eastern-and-southern-Africa-.pdf); [BB26](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Julian-Rademeyer-Ballots-and-bullets-Targeted-killings-and-South-Africas-2026-local-government-elections-GI-TOC-October-2026.pdf)
- **Council seats and municipal jobs.** Local councillors are the majority of political victims; killings follow branch meetings, candidate selection and voter registration (five political deaths on the 20 to 21 June 2026 registration weekend). ANC secretary-general Gwede Mantashe, 2016: "selection of candidates for council is always a life-and-death issue." [FULL] — [BB26](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Julian-Rademeyer-Ballots-and-bullets-Targeted-killings-and-South-Africas-2026-local-government-elections-GI-TOC-October-2026.pdf); [Wikipedia: Political assassinations in post-apartheid South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_assassinations_in_post-apartheid_South_Africa)
- **Tenders and procurement.** Moerane Commission: "overwhelming evidence ... that access to resources through the tender system is the main root cause of the murder of politicians"; "criminal elements are recruited by politicians to achieve political ends". Decentralised procurement gives municipal managers and other accounting officers control of tenders, which is why those posts are fought over. [FULL, as quoted by GI-TOC] — [MC21](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/GITOC-Murder-by-Contract-Targeted-Killings-in-eastern-and-southern-Africa-.pdf); [BK23](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Rumbi-Matamba-The-business-of-killing-Assassinations-in-SA.-April23-web.pdf)
- **Whistle-blowers, auditors, investigators.** Sibusiso Sithole, Richmond municipal manager probing tender kickbacks and his own CFO, shot March 2017. - **Traditional leadership.** Counted as political since 2026: Khosi Makhuvha (Limpopo, 2 March 2026, royal house blames a chieftainship dispute; police noted robbery elements), Induna Dlungwane (Bulwer, 8 March), Induna Mwelase and his wife (Pietermaritzburg, July to August), Nkosi Mazamisa and his mother (Willowvale, 4 September). ### Inferences
- Motives overlap, which is useful for plotting and honest to the data: a councillor may own taxis, hold a chieftaincy and sit on a tender committee, and GI-TOC classifies by dominant motive only when evidence supports it.

## 4. Method patterns reported in cases

### Takeaway
The dominant pattern is a shooting by a small team at the moment the target arrives at or leaves home, or sits in a stationary vehicle, usually in the evening or early morning; personal hits are the ones most often dressed up as robberies, hijackings or accidents. Bystander deaths are frequent in case reports but not quantified.

### Cited Findings
- Weapon: firearms in 83% of cases with data (2000 to 2017); 95% shot (2015 to 2020). [FULL] — [RG18](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/The-rule-of-the-gun_Assassination-Witness.pdf); [MC21](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/GITOC-Murder-by-Contract-Targeted-Killings-in-eastern-and-southern-Africa-.pdf)
- Location (2015 to 2020): 39% at the victim's home, 12% outside the home, 27% drive-by; 16% of taxi hits at taxi ranks; victims "typically targeted when they were either arriving at their homes or leaving them". Earlier: most carried out outside the victim's home, "often late at night". [FULL] — [MC21](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/GITOC-Murder-by-Contract-Targeted-Killings-in-eastern-and-southern-Africa-.pdf); [RG18](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/The-rule-of-the-gun_Assassination-Witness.pdf)
- Outcome: 87% of recorded South African hits killed the target (taxi hits 94%, personal 77%); some plots fail through sting operations or because the hired man warns the target. [FULL] — [MC21](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/GITOC-Murder-by-Contract-Targeted-Killings-in-eastern-and-southern-Africa-.pdf)

## 5. How hits get solved, and why most do not

### Takeaway
Solved cases turn on ballistic linkage of a recovered gun across dockets, a traceable vehicle, an eyewitness or neighbour, and above all a participant who talks (plea agreement or accomplice evidence); the gunmen are convicted far more often than the client. The PKTT is the one unit with a documented record, and the attempt to disband it in December 2024 triggered the Madlanga Commission.

### Cited Findings
**Baseline**
- About 15% of murders in South Africa are solved (ISS, as cited by GI-TOC). David Bruce: of roughly 120 political killings since 2003, fewer than 10% led to convictions (as cited in 2018). [FULL] — [BB26](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Julian-Rademeyer-Ballots-and-bullets-Targeted-killings-and-South-Africas-2026-local-government-elections-GI-TOC-October-2026.pdf); [RG18](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/The-rule-of-the-gun_Assassination-Witness.pdf)
- Arrests or charges reported in at least 12 of 45 political incidents in Jan to Sep 2026, and in fewer than 10 of 78 organised-crime incidents. "Even when gunmen are arrested, the people who ordered the killings are rarely identified." [FULL] — [BB26](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Julian-Rademeyer-Ballots-and-bullets-Targeted-killings-and-South-Africas-2026-local-government-elections-GI-TOC-October-2026.pdf)
- Why not: intelligence and detective capacity "crippled by years of mismanagement ..., political interference, deep-seated corruption", loss of forensic and digital specialists to the private sector, no cluster-focused investigations, very slow courts. [FULL] — [BK23](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Rumbi-Matamba-The-business-of-killing-Assassinations-in-SA.-April23-web.pdf)

- Appointed October 2016 by Premier Willies Mchunu, chaired by Adv Marumo Moerane; heard 63 witnesses; report released 2018. [FULL, via GI-TOC] — [BB26](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Julian-Rademeyer-Ballots-and-bullets-Targeted-killings-and-South-Africas-2026-local-government-elections-GI-TOC-October-2026.pdf); [MC21](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/GITOC-Murder-by-Contract-Targeted-Killings-in-eastern-and-southern-Africa-.pdf)
- ISS's summary differs slightly: it says the commission concluded the primary motive was intra-party conflict, patronage networks and taxi conflict, and calls the recommendations "out of touch". [FULL] — [ISS/ENACT](https://issafrica.org/enactafrica/enact-observer/tracking-the-trajectory-of-targeted-killings-in-south-africa)

### Inferences
- Realistic timeline: gunmen arrested within days to a year when a gun, car or witness links them; trial two to six years later; the client, if ever charged, later still and usually only after a convicted shooter cooperates.
- A plea deal that does not require naming the client is a credible source of friction between prosecutors, detectives and the family.

### Gaps
- No verified conviction rate for commissioners versus shooters exists in any source found.
- I found no full-text court judgment detailing cellphone-tower, vehicle-tracker or plate-recognition evidence in a hit case; these techniques are not documented here beyond the items above.
- Whether the Madlanga Commission delivered a final report by 16 November 2026, and its findings on the PKTT, were not found.
- The fate of the 121 dockets is unverified.

## 6. Mpumalanga specifically

### Takeaway
Mpumalanga is a low-count province in GI-TOC's data (two political incidents in Jan to Sep 2026; grouped with four other provinces at 10% of all hits to 2017) but has a long reputation for tender-linked political murder dating from the 2009 Mbombela stadium killings, a near-zero conviction record for that era, and episodic taxi and organised-crime hits. No research was found that explains its hotspot status rigorously.

## 7. Protection and risk

### Takeaway
State protection is thin and reactive: whistle-blowers such as Deokaran had none, witnesses in and out of the formal programme have been killed, and prosecutors receive guards only after threats. Researchers and civil society ask for protection of witnesses and whistle-blowers before testimony, an independent protection body, and prosecution of those who order killings.

### Cited Findings
- Deokaran was a key SIU witness, had been on special leave for over a year, and had no state protection when shot in August 2021. [FULL, Wikipedia] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babita_Deokaran)
- Witness D: Marius van der Merwe, private security owner and former EMPD member, testified to the Madlanga Commission about an alleged 2022 torture death and cover-up involving EMPD figures (Julius Mkhwanazi, not the KZN commissioner); he declined witness protection and was shot outside his Brakpan home on 5 December 2025 in front of his family. The Justice Minister defended the programme and blamed media for his identification. A second figure in the same matter survived 16 shots in February 2026. [FULL, Wikipedia; one page carries an AI-text warning] — [Wikipedia: Madlanga Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madlanga_Commission); [Wikipedia: Marius van der Merwe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marius_van_der_Merwe)
- The commission moved to in-camera and remote off-camera testimony for sensitive witnesses; several witnesses reported threats. [FULL, Wikipedia] — [Wikipedia: Madlanga Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madlanga_Commission)
- 2026: eight people recorded as witnesses killed; Eugene Jacobs, a former murder-trial witness who had been in witness protection, was killed in Elsies River on 30 May 2026; state witness Christopher Carelse shot in Cape Town in June. [FULL] — [BB26](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Julian-Rademeyer-Ballots-and-bullets-Targeted-killings-and-South-Africas-2026-local-government-elections-GI-TOC-October-2026.pdf)
- Glebelands Eight trial: three potential witnesses murdered before trial; prosecutor Dorian Paver given protection after death threats; gallery cleared after intimidation; one witness had been in hiding since 2016; residents distrusted Umlazi police and gave statements only after a new team took over. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 27 Oct 2022](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2022-10-27-durbans-glebelands-eight-hitmen-including-former-detective-finally-convicted/)
- Threats reported and not acted on: Abahlali leaders Ayanda Ngila and Lindokuhle Mnguni (both killed 2022) had reported death threats; Dyokwe had sworn an affidavit in February 2026 that she was in hiding. [FULL] — [BK23](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Rumbi-Matamba-The-business-of-killing-Assassinations-in-SA.-April23-web.pdf); [BB26](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Julian-Rademeyer-Ballots-and-bullets-Targeted-killings-and-South-Africas-2026-local-government-elections-GI-TOC-October-2026.pdf)

[FULL; BK23 dates it February 2023, which I did not cross-check] — [BK23](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Rumbi-Matamba-The-business-of-killing-Assassinations-in-SA.-April23-web.pdf)

### Inferences
- A character who refuses witness protection is realistic (it means relocation and loss of livelihood), and a character inside it is not safe once he leaves.
