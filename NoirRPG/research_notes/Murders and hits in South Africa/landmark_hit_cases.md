# Landmark contract killings and assassinations in South Africa, 2005-2026: case files and recurring patterns

Research date: 22 November 2026. Status statements are "as of the latest source found", which is dated in each bullet.

**How to read the verification tags**

- **[FULL]** = I fetched and read the whole page (Wikipedia plain-text extract, Daily Maverick article, The Citizen / Lowvelder article, GroundUp, GI-TOC landing page).
- **[EXTRACT]** = rests only on a search-engine summary/snippet of the linked page. I could not open the page (News24, EWN, TimesLive, SABC, IOL, M&G, NPA, HRW, CPJ and others were not fetchable in this session). Treat wording and dates as provisional.
- **[UNCERTAIN]** = sources conflict, the claim is thin, or it is an untested allegation.
- Wikipedia is a tertiary source and several of these articles are lightly edited; where Wikipedia is the only source I say so.
- Legal status language is deliberate: "alleged / charged / accused" means no conviction. Several matters are still before the courts and every accused person named below who has not been convicted is presumed innocent.

## 1. Case files: who, what, first theory, what broke the case, who was convicted, how long, what is unknown

### Takeaway
Across roughly 25 cases, the usual end state is "shooters or middlemen arrested, orderer unknown or untried": only one of the assigned cases (Bozwana) produced a conviction of someone the court treated as directing the hit, and even there no motive or ultimate paymaster was established. Cases move in years, not months: 6 years to arrests in Meyiwa, 8 years to a guilty plea in Magaqa, 9 years to convictions in Bozwana, and 5 to 10 years with no arrest at all for Rhadebe, Ntshangase, Nico Swart and the Murrays.

### Cited Findings

#### 1.1 Babita Deokaran (Johannesburg, 23 August 2021)
- **Victim / why a target.** Acting chief financial officer at the Gauteng Department of Health; she had flagged suspicious payments at Tembisa Hospital and was an SIU witness on PPE procurement. [FULL] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_Babita_Deokaran)
- **What happened.** Shot at about 08:00 outside her home in Johannesburg South after the school run; a vehicle pulled alongside and fired. A passenger in her car survived and later gave evidence. [FULL] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_Babita_Deokaran)
- **First theory.** I found no source showing police ever treated this as robbery; arrests came within three days and it was framed as a hit from the start. [FULL] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_Babita_Deokaran)
- **What broke the shooter-level case.** A neighbour told police he had seen a suspicious BMW at the scene several times beforehand; the car was traced to a former SANDF member in the Pretoria area, leading to six arrests. The Sunday Times (via Wikipedia) reported she had been watched for more than a month, and that nearby CCTV had been disabled and phone-surveillance software possibly used. [FULL for Wikipedia; the CCTV/surveillance claim is second-hand and UNCERTAIN] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_Babita_Deokaran)
- **Convictions.** On 22 August 2023, one day before the second anniversary, six men pleaded guilty under a section 105A plea-and-sentence agreement: Phakamani Hadebe 22 years, Sanele Mbhele 22, Siphakanyiswa Dladla 15, Zitha Radebe 15, Siphiwe Mazibuko 15, Nhlangane Ndlovu 6. The court was told the person who actually shot her had not been arrested and the mastermind had not been identified. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 22 Aug 2023](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2023-08-22-babita-deokaran-killers-plead-guilty-and-get-jail-terms-but-the-shooter-and-assassination-mastermind-remain-at-large/)
- **The chain as described in the plea.** The agreement named two middlemen, "Khanyisani Mpungose" and "Sithole" (clan name Jobe). Mpungose allegedly told accused 1 she was "a problematic person at her place of employment", supplied the two vehicles, and with an unknown man fired the shots. The six had links to the taxi/transport industry, at most grade 11 schooling, and monthly incomes of R1,500 to R20,000. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 22 Aug 2023](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2023-08-22-babita-deokaran-killers-plead-guilty-and-get-jail-terms-but-the-shooter-and-assassination-mastermind-remain-at-large/)
- **The dead middleman.** Journalist Jeff Wicks writes that the lead Hawks detective ordered the release of Mpungose, the alleged gunman, who was himself murdered months later; Wicks concludes "evidence has been destroyed" and calls it effectively a cold case. Wikipedia says Mpungose was killed in Germiston four months after the murder. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick (Wicks book excerpt), 31 Aug 2025](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2025-08-31-babita-deokaran-murder-probe-reveals-police-failure-fading-hope-for-justice/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_Babita_Deokaran)
- **Retracted naming.** One accused alleged in a confession that former health minister Zweli Mkhize paid for the hit, then retracted it; Mkhize denied involvement. Reported payment figures vary (R2 million total per Cape Talk; R400,000 each in the retracted statement). [FULL for Wikipedia; treat figures as UNCERTAIN] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_Babita_Deokaran)
- **Her own records.** WhatsApp messages to her supervisor weeks before her death: "I am just worried that the guys in Tembisa are going to realise we are not releasing payments and know that we are on to something. Our lives could be in danger." She had flagged contracts split just under R500,000 to dodge stricter controls. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 20 Jul 2026](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-07-20-babitas-revenge-state-seizes-r326m-empire-of-tembisa-hospital-tender-kingpin/)
- **After death.** SIU later reported more than R2.04 billion stolen through 207 front companies and nine syndicates; in July 2026 the High Court confirmed forfeiture of R326 million in assets (four Lamborghinis, a Bentley, properties, a boat) from Hangwani Maumela, described by the NPA as heading a syndicate of 14 entities that got more than R400 million in Tembisa contracts. This is civil forfeiture, not a murder charge. [FULL] — [The Citizen, 24 Aug 2026](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/babita-deokarans-ghost-still-haunts-corrupt-kathrada-foundation-demands-mastermind-be-jailed/); [Daily Maverick, 20 Jul 2026](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-07-20-babitas-revenge-state-seizes-r326m-empire-of-tembisa-hospital-tender-kingpin/)
- **Status, August 2026.** Five years on, no one has been charged with ordering the killing. Acting Police Minister Cachalia: "Part of the reason we don't have justice today is that those people are in positions of power and able to protect themselves." Her brother: "the hitman does not hire himself." [FULL] — [The Citizen, 24 Aug 2026](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/babita-deokarans-ghost-still-haunts-corrupt-kathrada-foundation-demands-mastermind-be-jailed/)
- In December 2025 the Hawks reportedly said they were "not aware" of further suspects named by the convicted men. [EXTRACT] — [News24](https://www.news24.com/politics/hawks-not-aware-of-further-suspects-implicated-by-deokarans-convicted-murderers-20251218-1106)

#### 1.2 Cloete Murray and Thomas Murray (N1, Midrand, 18 March 2023)
- **Victims / why targets.** Cloete Murray (50), court-appointed liquidator of Bosasa/African Global Operations, Gupta-linked Trillian and Comair; his son Thomas (28) was legal adviser at their firm Sechaba Trust. A confidential section 417 insolvency inquiry into Bosasa had been running for more than a year. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 19 Mar 2023](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2023-03-19-police-investigate-double-murder-after-bosasa-liquidator-cloete-murray-and-son-gunned-down-in-midrand/)
- **What happened.** About 14:45 on a Saturday, gunmen fired into their car on the N1 north near the New Road off-ramp. Thomas died at the scene; Cloete died in hospital the next morning. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 19 Mar 2023](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2023-03-19-police-investigate-double-murder-after-bosasa-liquidator-cloete-murray-and-son-gunned-down-in-midrand/)
- **First theory.** Reported as a suspected assassination from day one; public speculation initially fixed on Bosasa and Gupta liquidations. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 19 Mar 2023](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2023-03-19-police-investigate-double-murder-after-bosasa-liquidator-cloete-murray-and-son-gunned-down-in-midrand/)
- **The theory that displaced it (journalism, not court findings).** Wikipedia, summarising News24's investigation, says the Murrays spent their final hours with siblings Rushil and Nishani Singh and had begun to unearth evidence about the Singhs' Ghana contracting business. The Singhs were arrested and charged with fraud involving an Investec loan (R178 million), not with the murders; Nishani Singh died in custody in October 2024. No court has linked them to the killing. [FULL for Wikipedia; link to murder is UNCERTAIN/allegation] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_Cloete_and_Thomas_Murray)

[EXTRACT] — [News24 reporting notes, Apr 2026](https://www.news24.com/investigations/reporting-notes-murray-murders-three-years-later-20260421-0493). Shibiri was dismissed from SAPS on 5 June 2026 for misconduct including "associating himself with a known criminal" (a separate matter). [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 5 Jun 2026](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-06-05-madlanga-commission-fallout-deepens-as-shibiri-dismissed-nine-officers-suspended/)

#### 1.3 Charl Kinnear (Bishop Lavis, Cape Town, 18 September 2020)
- **Victim / why a target.** Lieutenant-colonel and section commander in the Western Cape Anti-Gang Unit, investigating a firearm-licence racket involving alleged underworld figure Nafiz Modack and senior police officers. [FULL] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_Charl_Kinnear)
- **What happened.** Shot through his car window at about 15:00 as he arrived home; no security at the house. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 6 Nov 2024](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2024-11-06-charl-kinnear/)
- **Prior attempt and removed protection.** On 23 November 2019 a man was caught with a hand grenade outside his home (it fell out of his pocket in view of police). A guard detail was posted and then removed on 19 December 2019. Fareez Smith pleaded guilty in April 2022 to the attempt. [FULL] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_Charl_Kinnear)
- **Decisive evidence.** Location "pings": his phone was tracked 2,116 times (Wikipedia, Daily Maverick) or at least 2,400 times (IPID briefing) in the months before; on the day pinging ran from about 02:30 to 15:15. The State says the same pattern preceded the April 2020 attempt on lawyer William Booth. Debt collector and ex-rugby player Zane Kilian was arrested two days later. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 20 May 2026](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-05-20-charl-kinnears-widow-says-court-closing-nafiz-modacks-defence-was-long-overdue-/); [Daily Maverick, 6 Nov 2024](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2024-11-06-charl-kinnear/)
- **The warning that was not acted on.** The owner of a company that sold pings noticed the constant tracking on 3 September 2020 and alerted a Hawks warrant officer in Gauteng; IPID found the Hawks "failed to initiate a criminal investigation into the illegal monitoring" although they knew who was involved. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 3 Jan 2022](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2022-01-03-cellphone-towers-dont-lie-the-24-hour-potential-killer-in-your-pocket-could-also-help-solve-your-murder-heres-how/)
- **Accused.** Modack was arrested in April 2021. He, Kilian and others (15 accused, about 122 to 124 charges) pleaded not guilty in January 2024. Modack denies involvement and has claimed in court that police killed Kinnear. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 9 Jun 2025](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2025-06-09-nafiz-modack-convicted-of-corruption-trial-for-multiple-murders-continues/)
- **Trial progress.** 20 May 2026: Judge Henney closed Modack's defence case under s342A(3)(d) of the Criminal Procedure Act after months of delay. The widow noted the accused had been in custody for years and the family had been attending court since 2019. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 20 May 2026](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-05-20-charl-kinnears-widow-says-court-closing-nafiz-modacks-defence-was-long-overdue-/)
- September 2026: a co-accused reportedly testified he received an instruction from Modack to kill Kinnear but claimed he was drawn into a police plot to frame Modack. [EXTRACT, single report] — [EWN, 8 Sep 2026](https://www.ewn.co.za/2026/09/08/co-accused-admits-nafiz-modack-ordered-hit-on-charl-kinnear)
- **Status.** No verdict on the murder charges found as of November 2026 (six years on). The gunman had not been identified publicly as of January 2022 ("a lone gunman, who is still at large"). [FULL for the 2022 statement] — [Daily Maverick, 3 Jan 2022](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2022-01-03-cellphone-towers-dont-lie-the-24-hour-potential-killer-in-your-pocket-could-also-help-solve-your-murder-heres-how/)
- **Related conviction.** On 6 June 2025 Modack was convicted of corruption for paying a police brigadier R146,600 (2011 to 2013); the brigadier's 2019 plea agreement was the key evidence, 14 years after the conduct. Investigators said witnesses only became willing once Modack was in custody. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 9 Jun 2025](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2025-06-09-nafiz-modack-convicted-of-corruption-trial-for-multiple-murders-continues/)
- **Fallout inside the police.** IPID's report (classified "top secret" in 2022, declassified 1 November 2024) implicated nine officers, including the former Anti-Gang Unit head, for failing to act on the threat; the NPA initially declined to prosecute. The widow laid culpable-homicide charges in August 2022. IPID also opened a case against the then National Commissioner for not cooperating. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 6 Nov 2024](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2024-11-06-charl-kinnear/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_Charl_Kinnear)

#### 1.4 Senzo Meyiwa (Vosloorus, 26 October 2014) and the two dockets
- **Victim.** Orlando Pirates and national-team goalkeeper and captain. Shot at the family home of his girlfriend, singer Kelly Khumalo. [FULL] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senzo_Meyiwa)
- **First theory.** A robbery gone wrong: two armed intruders demanding valuables; only one phone was taken. Police released identikits; one man was arrested on 31 October 2014 and freed on 11 November 2014 for lack of evidence. The case then went cold for six years. [FULL] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senzo_Meyiwa); [Daily Maverick, 20 Jun 2022](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2022-06-20-senzo-meyiwa-murder-how-the-5-accused-ended-up-in-the-dock/)
- **Scene handling.** The forensic officer testified he arrived almost four hours after the shooting, took no DNA from the door handle the intruders supposedly used, and did not test people in the house for gunshot residue; a statement claimed someone cleaned the house before police arrived. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 20 Jun 2022](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2022-06-20-senzo-meyiwa-murder-how-the-5-accused-ended-up-in-the-dock/)
- **Docket one (the trial).** Five men were charged in October 2020. The lead investigator's affidavit called it "the contractual murder of Senzo Meyiwa", not a robbery, but did not say who ordered it. Evidence cited: two 2020 confessions by one accused (to a police officer and to a magistrate), a ballistic link between the Meyiwa firearm and a gun used in another 2014 murder, phone photographs showing an accused in clothing witnesses described, and witnesses placing the alleged driver in the getaway car. Several accused were already serving sentences for other killings. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 20 Jun 2022](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2022-06-20-senzo-meyiwa-murder-how-the-5-accused-ended-up-in-the-dock/)
- **Docket two (Vosloorus CAS 375/1/2019).** Opened about five years after the killing; an unsigned draft indictment named the seven people who were in the house and proposed charges of murder and defeating the ends of justice. The DPP called it "an internal opinion by a junior State advocate which was without merit"; the defence said its late disclosure violated the accused's rights and that an arrest warrant for Khumalo had existed in 2020. The NPA said it would decide on that docket after the trial; in 2024 a police witness told the court it was a "defeating" docket and the only murder docket was the one before court. None of the people in the house has been charged. [FULL for DM; EXTRACT for the rest] — [Daily Maverick, 20 Jun 2022](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2022-06-20-senzo-meyiwa-murder-how-the-5-accused-ended-up-in-the-dock/); [SABC](https://www.sabcnews.com/sabcnews/controversial-second-docket-in-senzo-meyiwa-murder-case-brings-trial-a-halt/); [News24, Aug 2022](https://www.news24.com/news24/southafrica/news/senzo-meyiwa-npa-to-decide-on-second-murder-docket-when-current-trial-is-done-20220829); [EWN, Oct 2024](https://ewn.co.za/2024/10/17/only-one-docket-into-senzo-meyiwas-murder-exists-court-hears)
- **Trial.** Began 2022 in the Pretoria High Court; restarted from scratch in July 2023 under a new judge after the first fell ill. [FULL] — [The Citizen, 5 Jan 2025](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/south-africa/courts/senzo-meyiwa-murder-trial-end-in-2025/). A trial-within-a-trial of about five months ended with the confessions ruled admissible. [EXTRACT] — [Sunday World](https://sundayworld.co.za/news/confessions-in-meyiwa-murder-case-admissible-court-rules/)
- **Status.** Still running in October 2026 with an accused testifying; no verdict found as of November 2026, twelve years after the killing. [EXTRACT] — [SABC, Oct 2026](https://www.sabcnews.com/sabcnews/senzo-meyiwa-murder-trial-postponed-to-next-tuesday/)

#### 1.5 Brett Kebble (Johannesburg, 27 September 2005)
- **Victim / context.** Mining magnate (JCI, Western Areas, Randgold & Exploration), major ANC funder, forced out of his companies on 30 August 2005 amid missing Randgold Resources shares; facing prosecution for fraud at the time of his death. [FULL] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brett_Kebble)
- **What happened.** Shot several times at about 21:00 in his Mercedes near a bridge over the M1 in Abbotsford, on his way to a dinner. [FULL] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brett_Kebble)
- **How the first reading changed.** Private forensic scientist David Klatzow was first to suggest he was shot by people known to him using low-velocity "reduced charge" ammunition of the kind used by security operatives, and first to suggest assisted suicide. The family rejected suicide. [FULL] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brett_Kebble)
- **Arrest and theory.** Glenn Agliotti, a convicted drug dealer and friend of national police commissioner Jackie Selebi, was arrested on 16 November 2006; he had phoned Selebi from near the scene shortly after the shooting. The NPA's 2008 indictment accepted that Kebble arranged his own death. [FULL] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brett_Kebble)
- **The shooters turned witnesses.** Security chief Clinton Nassif hired Mikey Schultz, Nigel McGurk and Faizel Smith; Schultz testified in 2010 that he fired the shots and that all instructions came from Nassif, so he could not implicate Agliotti directly. The shooters and Nassif received section 204 indemnity. [FULL for Wikipedia; EXTRACT for s204 detail] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brett_Kebble); [M&G, 25 Nov 2010](https://mg.co.za/article/2010-11-25-glenn-agliotti-cleared-of-kebble-murder/)
- **Outcome.** Agliotti was discharged in November 2010: the court found no prima facie case. Nobody has been convicted of the killing. The Scorpions were criticised for indemnifying the men who pulled the trigger ("botched prosecution"). [FULL] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brett_Kebble)
- **Collateral conviction.** Selebi was convicted of corruption on 2 July 2010 and sentenced to 15 years on 3 August 2010 (payments via Agliotti); released on medical parole in July 2012. [FULL] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jackie_Selebi)
- **Other hits disclosed in evidence.** Testimony described an August 2005 shooting of Allan Gray fund manager Stephen Mildenhall and alleged orders to kill others, including a magazine editor. [FULL] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brett_Kebble)
- **Unknown.** Wikipedia: "the complete circumstances surrounding his death remain unclear." Time from murder to end of the only trial: five years.

#### 1.6 Kiernan "AKA" Forbes and Tebello "Tibz" Motsoane (Florida Road, Durban, 10 February 2023)
- **What happened.** Shot at close range outside a restaurant by two gunmen who crossed the street; captured on CCTV and widely circulated. Police say Forbes was the target and Motsoane was hit "by coincidence". [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 28 Feb 2024](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2024-02-28-six-arrested-including-alleged-mastermind-for-murder-of-rapper-aka-police-minister-cele/)
- **Structure of the team as described by police.** A coordinator, two shooters, two spotters (one inside the restaurant), and a supplier of hired firearms and vehicles. Forbes was followed from the airport to his hotel to the restaurant. The plan had been to shoot him in his vehicle. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 28 Feb 2024](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2024-02-28-six-arrested-including-alleged-mastermind-for-murder-of-rapper-aka-police-minister-cele/)
- **Evidence trail and timeline.** Mercedes-Benz recovered 6 March 2023; firearm recovered 22 April 2023; cartridges linked to a second shooting scene on 23 June 2023; BMW that trailed him recovered 2 August 2023. Arrests on 22 April, 24 October and 14 December 2023 were made on other cases to hold suspects without tipping off the rest; two brothers were arrested in Eswatini on 24 February 2024; announcement on 27 February 2024, just over a year after the murder. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 28 Feb 2024](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2024-02-28-six-arrested-including-alleged-mastermind-for-murder-of-rapper-aka-police-minister-cele/)
- **Bail-stage evidence.** Cellphone records placing an accused near the scene at 22:17:50 (murder at 22:21:07), CCTV of a spotter eating in the restaurant, a pistol ballistically linked to cartridges beside Forbes, and bank records: the alleged coordinator received R803,000 the day after the murder and paid out to co-accused; he says it was a business deal. [FULL] — [The Citizen, 27 Mar 2024](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/south-africa/courts/cctv-cellphone-records-link-accused-to-aka-tibz-murder-state-argues-in-bail-case/)
- **Motive / orderer.** Police said in February 2024 the motive was not yet known. Who paid the R803,000, and why, has not been established publicly in the sources I read. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 28 Feb 2024](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2024-02-28-six-arrested-including-alleged-mastermind-for-murder-of-rapper-aka-police-minister-cele/)
- **Extradition and trial.** The Ndimande brothers were extradited from Eswatini in November 2025 after about 21 months and remanded; their matter was transferred to the Durban High Court in April 2026. [FULL] — [The Citizen, 17 Apr 2026](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/south-africa/courts/changes-made-in-aka-tibz-murder-trial-case/). Trial of seven accused began in early October 2026; all pleaded not guilty; a surveillance analyst from the airport testified he had received death threats. [EXTRACT] — [EWN, 7 Oct 2026](https://www.ewn.co.za/2026/10/07/first-witness-takes-the-stand-in-aka-and-tibz-murder-trial); [TimesLive, 26 Oct 2026](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/south-africa/2026-10-26-aka-shooting-footage-puts-murder-accused-to-sleep/)
- **Status.** Trial under way, no verdict as of late October 2026.

#### 1.7 Armand Swart (Vereeniging, 17 April 2024): the wrong man
- **Victim.** Engineer at Q Tech, shot in his car outside his workplace. He was not the whistleblower. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 22 Oct 2025](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2025-10-22-armand-swart-killing-suspect-kt-molefes-nephew-on-the-run-since-2024/)
- **Alleged motive (commission testimony, untested in court).** Q Tech had supplied Transnet with springs for 40 years at about R4 each; in 2023 it learned a middleman company was reselling them to Transnet at R151 each under an unadvertised tender. A whistleblower reported it; on 12 April 2024 Transnet investigators demanded documents from a Transnet buyer, who allegedly turned to his uncle, businessman Katiso "KT" Molefe. ## 2. How often was the first public explanation (robbery, hijacking, random crime) wrong, and what corrected it?

### Takeaway
In the modern, high-profile professional cases police usually call it a hit within hours because nothing is stolen; the "robbery" misreading is commonest in older, domestic-setting or low-visibility cases (Meyiwa, Mahlangu, Hanekom). The more common first error is not "robbery" but a wrong motive: the public fixes on the victim's most famous enemy while the evidence later points somewhere smaller and closer.

### Cited Findings
- **Robbery first, hit later.** Meyiwa was explained as a botched robbery in 2014; by 2021 the lead investigator's sworn affidavit called it a "contractual murder". The correction came from a cold-case team's confessions and ballistics six years later. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 20 Jun 2022](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2022-06-20-senzo-meyiwa-murder-how-the-5-accused-ended-up-in-the-dock/)
- **Break-in first.** IPID investigator Mandla Mahlangu died in an "alleged 'botched' break-in" days before he was to testify; arrested men allegedly confessed it was ordered and paid for. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 3 Jan 2022](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2022-01-03-cellphone-towers-dont-lie-the-24-hour-potential-killer-in-your-pocket-could-also-help-solve-your-murder-heres-how/)
- **Hijacking first.** Informant Jaco Hanekom's 2023 death was "initially reported as a hijacking and drive-by shooting"; it was reframed only when a retired officer testified at a commission in 2025 to 2026. [FULL, Wikipedia only] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaco_Hanekom)
- **Suicide-by-proxy displacing murder.** Kebble's death was first a murder mystery; a private forensic scientist's reading of the ammunition and powder burns, then the shooters' testimony, turned it into an "assisted suicide". [FULL] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brett_Kebble)
- **Hit from the outset.** Van der Merwe (firearm, wallet and phone left on the body), Deokaran, the Murrays, Kinnear, Nico Swart ("all the hallmarks of a planned hit"), Rantsofu (CCTV). [FULL] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marius_van_der_Merwe); [Daily Maverick, 24 May 2021](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2021-05-24-richards-bay-minerals-gm-shot-dead-in-latest-violent-incident-to-hit-operations/)
- **"Usually, nothing is taken during these shootings, pointing away from opportunistic crime."** In several cases there are signs of prior surveillance, coordinated escape and insider information. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 15 Apr 2026](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-04-15-10-professionals-assassinated-in-three-years-zero-masterminds-arrested/)
- **Wrong motive rather than wrong crime type.** The Murrays: the public looked to Bosasa and the Guptas; News24's reporting pointed to a different, smaller matter they were working on in their last hours. [FULL for Wikipedia summary] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_Cloete_and_Thomas_Murray). Deokaran: first linked to a R332 million PPE scandal; her own messages and later SIU work pointed to Tembisa Hospital payments. [FULL] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_Babita_Deokaran); [Daily Maverick, 20 Jul 2026](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-07-20-babitas-revenge-state-seizes-r326m-empire-of-tembisa-hospital-tender-kingpin/). Magaqa: read as ANC factionalism; the State's case is a municipal tender. [FULL] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sindiso_Magaqa)
- **Wrong victim.** Armand Swart was killed in place of a colleague; this was only stated publicly by a police witness at a commission 18 months later. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 20 Oct 2025](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2025-10-20-envelopes-and-missing-evidence-madlanga-commission-hears-allegations-of-murder-cover-up/)
- **Officials minimising.** After Rantsofu's killing the municipality "was quick to deny claims that [she] was a whistleblower"; after van Niekerk's the Justice Minister said "there is no indication yet of what could have led to this crime". [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 15 Apr 2026](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-04-15-10-professionals-assassinated-in-three-years-zero-masterminds-arrested/); [Daily Maverick, 7 Sep 2025](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2025-09-07-rising-death-toll-of-sa-corruption-busters-like-bouwer-van-niekerk-highlights-cost-of-accountability/)
- **Alternative-motive smears.** In the Rhadebe matter an internal police note played up the victim's taxi-industry past and an old, already-solved murder to dilute the mining motive. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick / amaBhungane, 9 May 2023](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2023-05-09-murder-of-bazooka-radebe-in-xolobeni-hawks-general-accused-of-shutting-down-investigation/)
- **A real robbery killing for comparison.** Alex FM's Joshua Mbatha: phones and shoes taken, several similar shootings the same night; the paper "could not establish" a hit. Hlatshwayo likewise. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 15 Jul 2022](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2022-07-15-alex-fm-music-manager-among-at-least-5-people-killed-overnight-in-alexandra/)
- **Classification is unstable.** GI-TOC notes its "personal hit" count "may change as unresolved cases, or cases that appear at face value to have other motives, move into specific categories when more evidence becomes available". [FULL] — [GI-TOC, 2 Oct 2026](https://globalinitiative.net/analysis/targeted-killings-south-africa-2026-local-government-elections/)

### Inferences
- Rough tally across the cases here: an outright "ordinary crime" first reading that later proved wrong in about three (Meyiwa, Mahlangu, Hanekom); wrong or contested motive in at least four (Murrays, Deokaran, Magaqa, Kebble); wrong victim in one (Armand Swart); recognised as a hit immediately in most of the rest. This is my count from the sources above, not a published statistic.
- What corrects a misreading, in order of frequency in these files: (1) an arrested participant talking, (2) ballistic cross-matching to other scenes, (3) a journalist or private investigator, (4) a commission of inquiry giving a platform to a sidelined detective, (5) the victim's own messages.
- A robbery cover is hard to sustain when the victim is shot many times with a rifle and keeps his wallet; realistic cover stories in these files are instead "hijacking", "house break-in", or a staged alternative motive from the victim's past.

### Gaps
- No systematic figure exists for how often police first log a hit as robbery; SAPS does not disaggregate targeted killings from murder ([GI-TOC, 2023](https://globalinitiative.net/analysis/assassinations-targeted-killings-south-africa/), FULL).

## 3. What types of evidence recur as decisive?

### Takeaway
Three things do most of the work: ballistic cross-linking of one firearm to many scenes, phone and vehicle location data, and an arrested insider who talks. CCTV and neighbours' observations find the car; financial records and the victim's own files explain the motive but have rarely been enough, so far, to charge the person who paid.

### Cited Findings
- **Ballistics / firearm cross-matching.** One set of rifles and pistols seized after the Armand Swart shooting was linked to about 30 cases. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 2 Jun 2026](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-06-02-the-cop-who-allegedly-buried-the-bullets-forensic-analyst-faces-second-tampering-charge/). In the AKA case cartridges linked the scene to an earlier shooting and a recovered pistol to both. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 28 Feb 2024](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2024-02-28-six-arrested-including-alleged-mastermind-for-murder-of-rapper-aka-police-minister-cele/). In Meyiwa a ballistic match tied the gun to another 2014 murder; reports say the pistol should have been destroyed in 2017. [FULL / EXTRACT] — [Daily Maverick, 20 Jun 2022](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2022-06-20-senzo-meyiwa-murder-how-the-5-accused-ended-up-in-the-dock/); [TimesLive, 29 Dec 2020](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/south-africa/2020-12-29-breakthroughs-and-new-allegations-inside-senzo-meyiwas-murder-case-in-2020/). The Richmond hitman was caught because a gun in his possession linked several murders. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 25 Sep 2025](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2025-09-25-political-killings-task-team-probe-leads-to-hitmans-conviction-while-madlanga-commission-continues/)
- **Ballistics is also where cases are sabotaged.** A first report omitted 15 cartridges; the analyst is charged with later removing the firearm and ammunition; in the van der Merwe case the rifle was allegedly tampered with. - **Cellphone location data.** Kinnear: thousands of pings, with times matching the murder to the minute, plus payments to the man doing the pinging. [FULL, Wikipedia] — [Wikipedia: Cat Matlala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cat_Matlala). An unclaimed burner phone was among four recovered at the Swart arrest. ### Inferences
- The decisive physical object in a realistic South African hit story is the gun, because the same weapons are reused and rented across many jobs; one recovered rifle can tie a nightclub DJ, an engineer and an actress into a single docket.
- The decisive human is rarely an outside eyewitness; it is a junior participant who was underpaid or frightened, or a survivor in the car.
- Evidence types fail in a characteristic order: scene evidence first (late arrival, car returned, no swabs), then laboratory evidence (omitted or removed exhibits), then witnesses (vanish, die, recant).

### Gaps
- Court judgments were not readable, so the weight a judge actually gave each evidence type is known only for bail rulings (van der Merwe, Matlala trial).

## 4. How far up the chain do convictions reach, and what protects the person who ordered the killing?

### Takeaway
Convictions usually stop at shooters, drivers and recruiters; the layer above is protected by cut-outs who are themselves killed or vanish, by cash and tender-based payment, by friends inside the police, and by time. Where an orderer has been reached it has been through a shooter's admissions (Magaqa, pending) or a taxi-boss-level organiser being convicted without motive (Bozwana).

### Cited Findings
- "Over the past three years, at least 10 professionals have been murdered, with motives suspected to be linked to their work, without any masterminds being arrested, despite the alleged gunmen being charged in some cases." [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 15 Apr 2026](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-04-15-10-professionals-assassinated-in-three-years-zero-masterminds-arrested/)
- GI-TOC analysts: "High-profile assassinations are complex and multi-layered; often, the hitmen themselves don't know who the mastermind is"; intermediaries, informal payment and no paper trail mean arrests are "often limited to gunmen, drivers or facilitators". [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 15 Apr 2026](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-04-15-10-professionals-assassinated-in-three-years-zero-masterminds-arrested/)
- **Cut-outs who die.** Deokaran: the two middlemen "were never seen again by the six accused"; one was released by police and murdered months later. [FULL] — [GI-TOC, 2 Oct 2026](https://globalinitiative.net/analysis/targeted-killings-south-africa-2026-local-government-elections/); [GI-TOC, 20 May 2024](https://globalinitiative.net/analysis/the-politics-of-murder-criminal-governance-and-targeted-killings-in-south-africa/). A search extract gives 131 targeted killings in 2023 (46 organised crime, 45 taxi, 31 political). [EXTRACT]

### Inferences
- A typical chain in these files has four or five links: beneficiary (official, tender holder or businessman), a relative or fixer, a recruiter from the taxi or security world, a crew (spotters, driver, shooters), and a supplier of hired guns and cars. Each link knows only its neighbours.
- The orderer's best protection is not secrecy but the death or disappearance of the single person who met both sides.
- The realistic route to an orderer is slow: years in custody, a co-accused who decides to plead, and a specialised unit that survives political attack long enough to use the plea.

### Gaps
- No verified national figure for the share of contract killings in which the orderer is convicted. The "one conviction in fourteen" Mpumalanga figure is from Wikipedia and is old.
- Whether any orderer in the PKTT's "more than 30 life sentences" was a senior official is not broken down in the sources read.

## 5. What happened to witnesses, investigators and prosecutors on these cases?

## 6. What role did the victim's own records (reports, emails, recordings, files) play after death?

### Takeaway
The victim's paper trail usually survives better than the murder docket: it identifies the motive, drives journalism, SIU findings and asset forfeiture, and sometimes commission testimony, but by itself has not yet convicted an orderer. Killers know this: in at least one case the laptop and documents were taken, and in another the supplier was deleted from the procurement system.

### Cited Findings
- **Messages that name the fear.** Deokaran's WhatsApps ("Our lives could be in danger"; praying the head of department would approve an investigation) were published in Wicks's book and reporting, and her flagged payments became the basis of SIU findings of R2.04 billion in losses and a R326 million forfeiture. "The Gauteng Health Department did not investigate, and it fell to Wicks, the police, the Asset Forfeiture Unit and now the National Treasury... completing Deokaran's work." [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 20 Jul 2026](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-07-20-babitas-revenge-state-seizes-r326m-empire-of-tembisa-hospital-tender-kingpin/); [The Citizen, 24 Aug 2026](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/babita-deokarans-ghost-still-haunts-corrupt-kathrada-foundation-demands-mastermind-be-jailed/)
- **Betrayal from inside.** Wicks: "A person or group within the Gauteng Health Department must have tipped off powerful actors." This is his inference, not a finding. [FULL] — [Daily Maverick, 31 Aug 2025](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2025-08-31-babita-deokaran-murder-probe-reveals-police-failure-fading-hope-for-justice/)

### Inferences
- In these files the victim's documents answer "why" quickly and "who paid" almost never; the people named in an audit are suspects by motive only, and prosecutors have preferred fraud charges and forfeiture, which the documents can prove, over murder charges, which they cannot.
- For a detective story: the valuable object is often a duplicate. The laptop is gone, but there is a message to a supervisor, a draft emailed to a private address, a report already handed to a council committee, or a statement lodged with police before the killing.
- Victims are not always clean; the same files that reveal the motive can reveal the victim's own exposure, which defence teams and internal police notes then use.

### Gaps
- I found no case here in which a victim's records were formally admitted and relied on in a murder conviction of an orderer.
- The content and fate of Mafole's report and Machaba's stolen files are not public in the sources read.
