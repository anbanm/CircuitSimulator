# South African "murder mystery" cases, 2004-2026: case files and pattern analysis

Status date: 22 November 2026. Descriptive background for fiction only. These are real cases and real people; nothing here is a judgment on anyone beyond what courts found.

**How to read the verification tags**

- **[FULL]** = I read the full page text (Wikipedia article via API, or the full article on Daily Maverick, The Citizen and its Caxton local titles, or ISS).
- **[EXTRACT]** = rests only on a search-engine extract or summary of the page; the page itself was not opened (News24 is blocked by a Cloudflare challenge in this session; IOL, TimesLIVE, M&G, SAFLII, NPA, SAMRC and EWN are not on the fetch allowlist).
- **[UNCERTAIN]** = sources conflict, or the point could not be confirmed.

Wikipedia has no article (under any title I tried) for the Lotz, Panayiotou, Packham, Rohde, Krugersdorp, Griekwastad (own article), Stella or Joshlin Smith cases, so those rest on news sources. No SAFLII judgment was read in full.

## Case files: what happened, what was contested, how each ended

### Takeaway
Of roughly 30 cases reviewed, the classic "stranger intruder" explanation was almost always the false one: where a court made a finding, the killer was a husband, boyfriend, son, daughter, wife, aunt or neighbour, and the cover story was a hijacking, a farm attack, a robbery, a suicide or a disappearance. The cases that remain open or ended in acquittal (Lotz, Horner, Coligny, Coka, Gardee so far, the Van Rooyen girls, Joshlin Smith's whereabouts) mostly failed on forensic handling or witness reliability, not on a lack of suspects.

### Cited Findings

#### A. The listed cases

**1. Inge Lotz, Stellenbosch, 16 March 2005 (unsolved; boyfriend acquitted)**
- [FULL] Lotz, 22, a Stellenbosch University master's student, was found killed in her flat in a secure complex; no forced entry, nothing taken, no sexual assault. The district surgeon who did the autopsy two days later told police to "look for a hammer" — [Daily Maverick, 4 Oct 2015](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2015-10-04-hitchenss-razor-amateur-sleuths-present-compelling-new-evidence-in-inge-lotz-case/)
- [FULL] Her boyfriend Fred van der Vyver was charged after an ornamental hammer (a gift from her) was found in his bakkie; police said a fingerprint lift ("Folien 1") came from the cover of a DVD she rented that day, which would place him at the flat. The defence said the lift came from a drinking glass. After a roughly 10-month trial he was **acquitted**; the court found the hammer was not the murder weapon — [Daily Maverick, 12 Jun 2014](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2014-06-12-inge-lotz-murder-nine-years-later-two-amateur-forensic-sleuths-once-again-point-fingers-at-fred-van-der-vyver); [Daily Maverick, 4 Oct 2015](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2015-10-04-hitchenss-razor-amateur-sleuths-present-compelling-new-evidence-in-inge-lotz-case/)
- [EXTRACT] Acquittal was in November 2007 by Judge Deon van Zyl, who criticised the police investigation and said a court cannot convict on suspicion — [M&G, 29 Nov 2007](https://mg.co.za/article/2007-11-29-lotz-murder-accused-found-not-guilty/)
- [FULL] A second pillar of the State case was a bloodstain on the bathroom floor said to match his sneaker, which would have broken his alibi (he said he was at his office in Pinelands). The police expert travelled to the US to consult footprint expert WJ Bodziak, who disagreed with him; the officer came home claiming Bodziak agreed, later admitted misrepresenting this, and the State persisted anyway. The family flew Bodziak out at "great expense" and he discredited the testimony — [Wikipedia: Minister van Polisie v Van der Vyver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_van_Polisie_v_Van_der_Vyver)
- [FULL] Civil suit: in August 2011 Judge Anton Veldhuizen ruled on the merits that Van der Vyver could pursue a R46 million malicious-prosecution claim. Forensic scientist David Klatzow (hired by the Van der Vyver family) called the investigation a "shambles" and said police labs were "on the edge of collapse" — [Daily Maverick, 16 Aug 2011](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2011-08-16-van-der-vyver-wins-civil-claim-but-will-the-police-ever-pay/)
- [EXTRACT] The Supreme Court of Appeal overturned that ruling in 2013 (he had not proved malice or causation) and the Constitutional Court dismissed his application later in 2013 — [gov.za statement](https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/minister-nathi-mthethwa-welcomes-supreme-court-appeal-decision-van-der-vyver); [IOL, 24 Aug 2013](https://iol.co.za/news/2013-08-24-end-of-the-road-for-inge-lotz-case/). [UNCERTAIN] Claim size is R46m in Daily Maverick and R30m in the government statement.
- [FULL] Afterlife of the case: Antony Altbeker's book *Fruit of a Poisoned Tree* (2010) argued a miscarriage of justice against Van der Vyver; amateur investigators Thomas and Calvin Mollett (*Bloody Lies*, 2014; *Bloody Lies Too*, 2015) argued the opposite, re-testing evidence with a replica hammer in a Piketberg flat. They say a bloodied towel bearing a mark was returned to its evidence bag and "has since gone missing". The Western Cape DPP told them the case would not be reopened and that there were no other suspects. These are the Molletts' claims, not court findings — [Daily Maverick, 12 Jun 2014](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2014-06-12-inge-lotz-murder-nine-years-later-two-amateur-forensic-sleuths-once-again-point-fingers-at-fred-van-der-vyver); [Daily Maverick, 4 Oct 2015](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2015-10-04-hitchenss-razor-amateur-sleuths-present-compelling-new-evidence-in-inge-lotz-case/)

**2. Van Breda family, De Zalze golf estate, Stellenbosch, 27 January 2015 (son convicted)**
- [FULL] Martin, Teresa and Rudi van Breda were killed with an axe; daughter Marli (16) survived with severe head injuries and retrograde amnesia. Henri (20) had minor lacerations. He phoned his girlfriend at 4:42 am, searched online for emergency numbers, and called emergency services almost three hours later. Axe and knife came from the house; no forced entry — [Wikipedia: Van Breda murders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Van_Breda_murders)
- [FULL] He claimed an attacker who was "an axe-wielding black man, wearing dark clothes, gloves and a balaclava-type mask". He surrendered 13 June 2016 (17 months later), trial began 4 April 2017, convicted 21 May 2018 (three murders, attempted murder, obstruction), sentenced 7 June 2018 to three life terms; the SCA refused leave to appeal on 7 November 2018 — [Wikipedia: Van Breda murders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Van_Breda_murders)
- [EXTRACT] Judge Siraj Desai found no evidence of a security breach at the estate, that the wounds were self-inflicted, that the emergency call showed a "lack of urgency", and that the case was circumstantial: "the cumulative effect of all the pieces concludes the puzzle" — [Cape Times/Independent, 21 May 2018](https://independentonsaturday.co.za/capetimes/news/2018-05-21-vanbreda-ruling-no-security-breach-evidence-conduct-inexplicable); [Sowetan, 21 May 2018](https://www.sowetanlive.co.za/news/south-africa/2018-05-21-henri-van-breda-found-guilty-of-family-axe-murders/)

**3. Jayde Panayiotou, Port Elizabeth (Gqeberha), April 2015 (husband convicted)**
- [FULL] The schoolteacher was kidnapped outside her home in Kabega Park while waiting for a lift to work and shot on the outskirts of KwaNobuhle. Her husband Christopher had used bouncer Luthando Siyoni as middleman to hire a killer. On 24 November 2017 Judge Dayalin Chetty sentenced Christopher Panayiotou to life; Sinethemba Nenembe got life plus 15 years; Zolani Sibeko 15 years for conspiracy. The judge said the killing was "months in the making" and involved "a string of would-be assassins". Siyoni was refused indemnity and a warrant issued — [The Citizen, 24 Nov 2017](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/south-africa/jaydes-murder-a-business-deal-and-panayiotou-showed-no-remorse)
- [EXTRACT] The break was a police sting: Siyoni, under police direction, got Panayiotou into a bugged car at a garage on 29 April 2015 and the conversation was filmed. The defence called it "fruit of the poisonous tree" and alleged Siyoni was tortured; after a trial-within-a-trial the video was ruled admissible (12 June 2017) — [News24, 12 Jun 2017](https://www.news24.com/SouthAfrica/News/panayiotou-video-recording-is-admissible-20170612); [TimesLIVE, 15 Jun 2017](https://www.timeslive.co.za/amp/news/south-africa/2017-06-15-it-looks-like-a-hit-now-panayiotou-discusses-murder-in-shocking-video/)
- [EXTRACT] Leave to appeal refused by the High Court (May 2018) and SCA; he approached the Constitutional Court in June 2019 — [News24, 10 Jun 2019](https://www.news24.com/SouthAfrica/News/panayiotou-approaches-constitutional-court-to-challenge-sentence-20190610). [UNCERTAIN] Constitutional Court outcome not found.

**4. Gill Packham, Constantia/Diep River, Cape Town, 22 February 2018 (husband convicted)**
- [FULL] She failed to arrive at work; her body was found in the boot of her burnt-out BMW near Diep River station. Rob Packham was convicted 20 May 2019 of murder and defeating the ends of justice, sentenced to 22 years. Two witnesses placed him at the scene (one saw him drive away in a white Audi SUV as the car burned). Judge Elize Steyn rejected his hijacking suggestion and called him an "accomplished liar" leading a double life. SCA petition refused April 2020 — [Daily Maverick, 23 Apr 2020](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2020-04-23-wife-killer-robert-packham-fails-in-bid-to-appeal-against-22-year-jail-sentence/)
- [FULL] The prosecutor's theory: cornered by an affair, he killed her, "making up cover stories along the way, getting rid of evidence, and setting it up to look like a hijacking". He said the couple had been planning to renew their vows. The Constitutional Court later dismissed his application — [The Citizen](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/south-africa/courts/constitutional-court-ruling-signals-end-of-the-road-for-wife-killer-rob-packham)
- [EXTRACT] Trial detail: his work phone was off for two hours and eight minutes that morning; a second phone was placed by tower data; a neighbour's CCTV showed a BMW at 07:34; her blood was found in the garage and on his car's door handle; a colleague testified he asked her to lie about his whereabouts — [News24, 25 Mar 2019](https://www.news24.com/southafrica/news/rob-packhams-phone-was-turned-off-the-morning-his-wife-disappeared-expert-20190325); [News24, 20 Mar 2019](https://www.news24.com/SouthAfrica/News/blood-and-a-broken-axe-what-detectives-found-at-packhams-constantia-home-20190320); [Cape Times, 13 Mar 2019](https://www.pressreader.com/south-africa/cape-times/20190313/281509342505343)

**5. Susan Rohde, Spier Hotel near Stellenbosch, July 2016 (husband convicted; sentence cut on appeal)**
- [FULL] Jason Rohde, then a property-company chief executive, said his wife hanged herself with a hair-curler cord on the hotel bathroom door after learning he had rekindled an affair. Judge Gayaat Salie-Hlophe convicted him of murder and of defeating the ends of justice for rearranging the scene; sentence in February 2019 was an effective 20 years. On 5 October 2021 the SCA dismissed the conviction appeal but found the High Court erred in finding smothering and assault; he was to be sentenced on the basis of manual strangulation only: 15 years (the prescribed minimum) plus 3 years. He had been on R200,000 bail since 2019 and had to report to prison — [The Citizen, 5 Oct 2021](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/south-africa/courts/convicted-wife-killer-jason-rohdes-jail-sentence-reduced-to-15-years-5-october-2021)
- [EXTRACT] The trial was a battle of pathologists: the State's said manual strangulation and smothering, with the ligature mark made after death; the defence's private pathologist said the injuries were consistent with hanging but "I can't be categorical" — [News24, 22 Nov 2017](https://www.news24.com/SouthAfrica/News/pathologists-battle-it-out-in-rohde-murder-trial-20171122); [TimesLIVE, 12 Oct 2017](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/south-africa/2017-10-12-staged-suicide-beaten-up-woman-pathologists-verdict-on-susan-rohde/)

**6. Anni Dewani, Gugulethu/Khayelitsha, Cape Town, 13 November 2010 (hired men convicted; husband acquitted)**
- [FULL] On honeymoon, the couple's taxi was hijacked; driver and husband were put out and she was found shot the next morning. A palm print on the taxi led to Xolile Mngeni (arrested 16 Nov); a township informant led to Mziwamadoda Qwabe; he named hotel receptionist Monde Mbolombo. All first described a robbery, then changed their accounts to a murder-for-hire at the husband's behest. Driver Zola Tongo pleaded guilty 7 Dec 2010 (18 years); Qwabe pleaded guilty Aug 2012 (25 years); Mngeni was convicted 19 Nov 2012 (life) and died in custody 18 Oct 2014; Mbolombo got immunity. Mngeni and Qwabe alleged police assault or suffocation before their statements — [Wikipedia: Murder of Anni Dewani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_Anni_Dewani)
- [FULL] Shrien Dewani was extradited 7 April 2014, tried from 6 October 2014 and **acquitted** on 8 December 2014 when Judge Traverso granted a section 174 discharge at the close of the State case, finding no credible evidence linking him to the crime; the key witnesses contradicted themselves and each other. The court also found earlier findings in the Mngeni trial rested on flawed forensic evidence and perjury. A UK coroner declined to resume an inquest (Oct 2015) — [Wikipedia: Murder of Anni Dewani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_Anni_Dewani)

**7. Nomia Rosemary Ndlovu, Tembisa/Olifantsfontein/Bushbuckridge, 2012-2018 (convicted 2021; again 2026)**
- [FULL] A Tembisa police sergeant convicted of six murders (her live-in partner and five relatives) for life and funeral cover, collecting about R1.4 million. Victims were found beaten, stabbed or poisoned between March 2012 and January 2018; the last, a nephew, was last seen with her in Bushbuckridge, Mpumalanga. She was arrested after being recorded asking an undercover officer and another man posing as hitmen to burn her sister and five children in their house. Six concurrent life terms in 2021 plus terms for fraud, incitement and the attempted murder of her mother. Colleagues recalled a gambling problem and loan sharks — [Wikipedia: Rosemary Ndlovu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosemary_Ndlovu)
- [EXTRACT] The would-be hitman went to police; a recording device was hidden in the car on the drive to Bushbuckridge. Her defence was that the footage "was all an act". A fellow officer's suspicion had been raised by a "bundle" of insurance policies — [TimesLIVE, 14 Sep 2021](https://www.timeslive.co.za/amp/news/south-africa/2021-09-14-it-was-all-an-act-alleged-killer-cop-nomia-rosemary-ndlovus-explanation-of-damning-video/); [TimesLIVE, 9 Sep 2021](https://timeslive.co.za/news/south-africa/2021-09-09-bundle-of-insurance-policies-fuelled-policemans-suspicion-that-thembisa-cop-may-be-killing-for-cash/)
- [EXTRACT] On 20 May 2026 she and former colleague Nomsa Mudau were convicted of incitement to murder Mudau's husband (a 2018 plot); each got 10 years on 14 October 2026, Ndlovu's running concurrently — [TimesLIVE, 14 Oct 2026](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/south-africa/2026-10-14-rosemary-ndlovu-co-accused-both-get-10-years-for-murder-plot/); [EWN, 14 Oct 2026](https://www.ewn.co.za/2026/10/14/rosemary-ndlovu-slapped-with-10-additional-years-behind-bars-for-hit-plot)

**8. Griekwastad (Griquatown), Northern Cape, Good Friday 6 April 2012 (teenage son convicted)**
- [FULL] Farmer Deon Steenkamp, wife Christelle and daughter Marthella (14) were shot on their farm; the surviving 15-year-old son, who stood to inherit, was convicted in 2014 of the murders, of raping his sister and of lying to police; 20 years — [Wikipedia: Griekwastad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Griekwastad)
- [EXTRACT] Convicted 27 March 2014 by Judge President Frans Kgomo, who rejected the farm-attack account: "There is no possibility a lone ranger could have entered the house unnoticed. The dogs would have barked." The court found the parents were killed to conceal the rape — [M&G, 27 Mar 2014](https://mg.co.za/article/2014-03-27-teen-found-guilty-on-all-griekwastad-charges/)
- [EXTRACT] Contested forensics: a gunshot-residue test on his hands was negative while two shirts tested positive; the defence said a blood-soaked jacket and mat were burned instead of being booked in — [IOL, 27 Mar 2013](https://iol.co.za/news/2013-03-27-no-gunpowder-on-teens-hands/); [IOL, 5 Nov 2013](https://iol.co.za/news/2013-11-05-griquatown-crucial-evidence-left-out/)

**9. The "Modimolle Monster", Limpopo, 3 January 2012 (not a whodunit; a small-town manhunt)**
- [FULL] Johan Kotze lured his estranged wife Ina Bonnette to his rented house, where three day labourers he had hired were hiding; she was tortured and raped, and her son Conrad (19), summoned by a call from her phone, was shot. Kotze was on the run for eight days and was caught when he turned up at the home of his friend and landlord, a neighbourhood-watch member who alerted police. On 17 July 2013 Kotze and Andries Sithole got life for murder; two co-accused were acquitted of murder but got life for rape. He claimed provocation and a psychological disorder; a three-member State panel found no mental disorder — [Wikipedia: Johan Kotze (criminal)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johan_Kotze_(criminal))
- [FULL] Background useful for fiction: married at least five times; an earlier wife died by suicide in 2010 after financial ruin; in the Northern Cape he was nicknamed "MacGyver" as a "slippery character", was implicated in a 1990 killing and **acquitted** in 1993 of murdering a 64-year-old woman for lack of evidence — [Wikipedia: Johan Kotze (criminal)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johan_Kotze_(criminal))

**10. Uyinene Mrwetyana, Claremont, Cape Town, 24 August 2019**
- [FULL] The UCT student was told by a post office employee to come back for a parcel after closing time; he raped and killed her inside and burned and dumped her body in Lingelethu West. She was missing for nine days (#BringNeneHome). Luyanda Botha confessed and received three life sentences on 15 November 2019, under three months later. The case triggered #AmINext protests, a UCT shutdown and presidential promises on sentencing and the sex-offender register — [Wikipedia: Murder of Uyinene Mrwetyana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_Uyinene_Mrwetyana)

**11. Tshegofatso Pule, Roodepoort, June 2020**
- [FULL] Eight months pregnant, found shot and hanging from a tree in Durban Deep. Hitman Muzikayise Malephane pleaded guilty in February 2021 (20 years) and testified against her boyfriend Ntuthuko Shoba, who was convicted in March 2022 and sentenced to life on 29 July 2022 — [Wikipedia: Murder of Tshegofatso Pule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_Tshegofatso_Pule)
- [FULL] Related: Karabo Mokoena (2017). **12. Coligny, North West, 20 April 2017 (convicted, then acquitted on appeal)**
- [FULL] Matlhomola Moshoeu (15) died after two farm managers, Doorewaard and Schutte, caught him with stolen sunflower heads and put him on their bakkie; they said he jumped off on the way to the police station. Rioting, looting and arson followed in Coligny and Lichtenburg; a farmhouse was torched after they got bail; the local magistrate, a Coligny resident, recused himself citing safety. The High Court in Mahikeng sentenced them to 18 and 23 years on the evidence of a single eyewitness. On appeal both were **acquitted**: the witness was found inconsistent, no cartridge cases were found where he said shots were fired, and investigators had collected no blood from the vehicle, the alleged assault sites or the jersey he said he used. They had spent 13 months in jail. AfriForum funded the appeal and its private prosecution unit assisted — [Wikipedia: Coligny, South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coligny,_South_Africa)

**13. Brendin Horner, Paul Roux/Senekal, Free State, 1 October 2020 (accused acquitted; unsolved)**
- [FULL] The 21-year-old farm manager was found tied to a fence post, stabbed and strangled; his bakkie was seen on township CCTV that evening and found 15 km away with blood from two men. Two men were arrested on 3 October with bloodstained clothing. On 6 October protesters stormed the Senekal magistrate's court and burned a police vehicle; on 16 October farmers and EFF supporters faced off. In November 2021 both accused were **acquitted**: DNA did not match and nothing linked them to the killing. President Ramaphosa rejected the framing of the killing as a race crime — [Wikipedia: Killing of Brendin Horner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_of_Brendin_Horner)

**14. Krugersdorp "Electus per Deus" killings, 2012-2016**
- [FULL] A group calling itself Electus per Deus ("Chosen by God") murdered 11 people. On 19 August 2019 Cecilia Steyn (13 life terms, described as the mastermind), Zak Valentine (8) and Marcel Steyn (7) were sentenced on 32 counts including murder, fraud, racketeering and robbery. Marinda Steyn, a schoolteacher and Marcel's mother, was already serving 11 life terms; John Barnard 20 years. Cecilia Steyn maintained her innocence. The judge found it shocking that the group called themselves Christians — [The Citizen, 19 Aug 2019](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/south-africa/courts/krugersdorp-killers-get-multiple-life-sentences)
- [EXTRACT] The killings began as a feud inside a church group and became killings for money; victims were made to hand over bank cards and PINs, and ATM CCTV identified the Steyn siblings. Le Roux Steyn took a plea deal and testified. The group also faked a member's death for an insurance payout — [News24, 5 Jun 2019](https://www.news24.com/southafrica/news/the-krugersdorp-killings-explained-who-was-murdered-how-and-why-20190605); [News24, 10 Oct 2018](https://www.news24.com/News24/court-hears-how-krugersdorp-killers-faked-death-of-one-of-their-own-for-life-insurance-payout-20181010). [UNCERTAIN] Le Roux Steyn's sentence is 25 years in most extracts and 35 in The Citizen.

**15. Leigh Matthews, Johannesburg, 9 July 2004**
- [FULL] Abducted from a university car park; her father paid R50,000 ransom; her body was found on 21 July in veld at Walkerville. Detective Piet Byleveld took over on 24 August and arrested fellow student Donovan Moodley on 4 October. He pleaded guilty on 25 July 2005 (life). The judge found he had not acted alone; Moodley later claimed he was framed, and all appeals up to the Constitutional Court (2010) and a 2012 retrial bid failed. Byleveld's biography says he believed there were accomplices; none has been charged — [Wikipedia: Murder of Leigh Matthews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_Leigh_Matthews)

**16. Gert van Rooyen disappearances, 1988-1990 (unsolved)**
- [FULL] At least six girls vanished; Van Rooyen and partner Joey Haarhoff died in a murder-suicide on 15 January 1990 after a victim escaped. Belongings of missing girls were found in his Pretoria house, but no bodies ever were. The house was demolished and sifted in 1996; bones found on a KZN beach in 2007 did not match. A note in a child's hand found in a Midrand shop in June 1990 was, the mother said, confirmed by police handwriting experts. A son was convicted of perjury in 2001 over conflicting statements — [Wikipedia: Gert van Rooyen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gert_van_Rooyen)

**17. Joshlin Smith, Middelpos, Saldanha Bay, missing since 19 February 2024**
- [FULL] Her mother Racquel "Kelly" Smith, the mother's boyfriend Jacquen Appollis and friend Steveno van Rhyn were convicted of kidnapping and trafficking and on 29 May 2025 each got life plus 10 years; the court sat in Saldanha — [The Citizen, 29 May 2025](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/joshlin-smith-sentencing-kelly-and-co-accussed-handed-life-sentences)

The case turned on an accomplice, Laurentia Lombaard, whose evidence contained "multiple contradictions and lies" which she admitted; she was given indemnity. Two accused said their statements were extracted by torture; admitted after a trial-within-a-trial. Leave to appeal refused 13 August 2025. Residents said the accused would not be safe if released; the mother's shack had been burned. Police searched again on 25 July 2025; the missing-person docket remains open — [Daily Maverick, 18 Aug 2025](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2025-08-18-joshlin-smiths-family-still-searches-for-answers-after-kidnappers-appeal-bids-denied/)

#### B. Mpumalanga

**18. Hillary Gardee, Mbombela/Sabie, April 2022 (unresolved; trial pending)**

## Who is the killer most often, and what do national statistics and femicide research say?

### Takeaway
For women, the killer is most often a current or former intimate partner; for murders generally, where a relationship could be established, most victims knew the offender and about half of the motives recorded were arguments. In the mystery cases above the hidden killer was nearly always inside the household or one step from it.

### Cited Findings
- [EXTRACT] SAMRC's fourth national femicide study (1999 to 2020/21): intimate partner femicides were 60.1% of femicides; intimate partner killings fell from four women a day (1999) to three (2009) and have not fallen since; they rose in the first Covid year while other femicide fell; fewer than 1 in 5 intimate partner femicide cases ended in a conviction; cases with no identified perpetrator have increased; Eastern Cape has the highest rate — [SAMRC press release](https://www.samrc.ac.za/press-releases/south-africas-femicide-crisis-persists-new-report-highlights-increase-intimate); [University of Pretoria repository](https://repository.up.ac.za/items/e71c8ca2-f7e8-4a21-9390-7389502af835)
- [EXTRACT] The paper's abstract gives an intimate partner femicide rate of 5.5 per 100,000 women, almost five times the global average; the 2017 study gave 4.9 (or 4.6 in a SAMRC brief) against 9.5 in 1999 — [University of Pretoria repository](https://repository.up.ac.za/items/e71c8ca2-f7e8-4a21-9390-7389502af835); [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10796052). [UNCERTAIN] Which year the 5.5 figure refers to.
- [FULL] SAPS docket analysis of 2,912 murders from 2015: a motive could be established in only 60% of dockets; of those, 51.5% arose from an argument or misunderstanding, 20% during another crime such as robbery, 10% vigilantism. Where a relationship was established, 70% of offenders were known to the victim, almost half of those as acquaintances; 11% of cases related to domestic violence; 96% of identified perpetrators were men. 20% of murders happened at the victim's home; half at weekends. Detection rates: 24% overall, 33% for murders of women and children — [ISS Today, 7 Nov 2017](https://issafrica.org/iss-today/what-do-we-know-about-murder-in-south-africa)
- [FULL] In 2016/17 men were 82% of murder victims, women 14%, children 4%; farmers, councillors and police together were about 1% of murders — [ISS Today, 7 Nov 2017](https://issafrica.org/iss-today/what-do-we-know-about-murder-in-south-africa)
- [EXTRACT] In recent SAPS quarterly releases "arguments, misunderstanding, road rage and provocation" remains the largest recorded category (1,158 murders in Oct-Dec 2025; 898 in Jan-Mar 2026), ahead of vigilantism, revenge, gangs and robbery — [The Citizen](https://citizen.co.za/news/south-africa/crime/saps-crime-statistics-leading-causes-of-murder)
- [FULL] Farm attacks: the 2003 committee of inquiry found most were driven by material gain and "very few cases have political overtones" — [Wikipedia: South African farm attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_African_farm_attacks)

### Inferences
- Tally of the hidden or disputed killer across the cases above where a court convicted: husband or male partner (Panayiotou, Packham, Rohde, Shoba, Mantsoe, Müller, Bylsma as ex-boyfriend); wife or widow (Maqubela, Mugadui, eMbalenhle); child of the family (Van Breda, Griekwastad, Sebati); wider kin (Ndlovu; Joshlin Smith's mother); neighbours and fellow congregants (Krugersdorp); a service worker known by sight (Mrwetyana); a fellow student who was a stranger (Matthews). Paid strangers appear only as instruments.
- The "employee" as killer appears mainly as hired labour directed by someone closer (Kotze's day labourers, Panayiotou's bouncer), and as farm guards acting with owners at Pampoenkraal.

- SAMRC full reports were not opened; exact proportions for unidentified perpetrators and for convictions by year are missing.

Five cover stories recur: hijacking, house robbery or farm attack by an unknown intruder, suicide, "she left and never arrived", and theft by the victim. They were undone by ordinary records and physical inconsistencies far more often than by a dramatic confession.

### Inferences
- The recurring tells, in order of frequency: (1) time gaps the suspect cannot explain (a phone switched off, a late emergency call); (2) the survivor's light or absent injuries; (3) no sign of entry at a place that is fenced, gated, dog-guarded or access-controlled; (4) paperwork done too early (policy queries, flight bookings, wills); (5) the weakest accomplice talking.
- The stock South African fear (the black intruder, the farm attack, the hijacker) is exactly what makes these cover stories plausible to a town, which is why they are chosen.

## What evidence and investigative failures recur, and what role did private experts, private investigators and journalists play?

### Takeaway
Acquittals and stalled cases trace to a short list: disputed lifts and marks, exhibits lost or never collected, a single unreliable witness, statements said to be beaten out of suspects, and witnesses who vanish. Private money shapes outcomes on both sides: defence families import experts, complainant communities fund lobby-group lawyers, and private detectives and reporters sometimes obtain the confession police did not.

Who is suspected first, and who is believed, follows the town's existing lines. Well-off white accused were disbelieved slowly and defended expensively; in farm cases each side reads the same body as proof of its own story, and a weak docket then confirms both sides' distrust of the state.

- [FULL] Respectability as cover: a police sergeant (Ndlovu), a schoolteacher in a prayer group (Krugersdorp), a constable's teenage daughter (Sebati), an acting judge's wife (Maqubela), a businessman planning to renew his vows (Packham). - [FULL] Public attention is uneven: Mrwetyana and Pule became national causes; ISS notes that most information about ordinary victims is never released — [Wikipedia: Mrwetyana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_Uyinene_Mrwetyana); [ISS](https://issafrica.org/iss-today/what-do-we-know-about-murder-in-south-africa)

### Inferences
- In a small town the first accusation is a social act: it names the outsider the community already fears. The second accusation, against an insider, costs the accuser their standing, which is why it tends to come from a relative, a junior colleague or a hired hand with nothing to lose.
- Lobby groups, political parties and neighbourhood watches arrive before the forensic report does. Their presence at the first court appearance fixes the public story long before any finding.
- Language shapes the record: statements are taken in one language and tested in another, and several of these cases turned on what an accused "really said" to police (Dewani, Joshlin Smith, Sebati). This is my inference; no source read here analyses translation directly.

### Gaps
- No academic study of race or class effects on South African murder investigations was read; the points above are drawn from case reporting and are illustrative.
- Language of police statements and court interpretation was not directly documented in the sources read.

## How long do such cases take, and how do they usually end?

### Takeaway
A contested, circumstantial murder case typically takes two to four years from killing to verdict and up to six with appeals; confessions and plea agreements finish in months; cases that depend on missing witnesses or weak forensics drift for years or end in acquittal. Cold cases break after five to thirteen years through a confession, not a laboratory.

### Inferences
- Endings by type in this set of about 30: circumstantial conviction after a long trial is the commonest outcome for domestic whodunits; accomplice-turned-witness is the commonest route for hired or group killings; outright confession is the commonest route for cold cases; acquittal clusters in racially charged rural cases built on one witness or late DNA.
- These are the famous cases and so over-represent convictions. The SAMRC and ISS figures suggest the typical ending is no arrest or no conviction at all.
- For a small-town story, the honest time scale is: arrest within days or not for 18 months; bail; a trial in the nearest High Court seat or a circuit sitting in the town itself two to three years on; appeal petitions for two more.
