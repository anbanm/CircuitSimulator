## 1. SAPS crime statistics by station, and which precinct is worst

### Takeaway
eMbalenhle is the district's worst precinct by official police account (Nov/Dec 2025 briefing) and is among Mpumalanga's highest-crime stations, with murder, house robbery and gender-based violence named as its main problems; Secunda records a similar total volume but its profile is property crime ("other theft"), not violence. Official station-by-category tables for 2022/23 to 2025/26 could not be retrieved, so the full comparison table asked for is a gap.

### Cited Findings
- [CONFIRMED-EXTRACT] At a crime briefing on 29 November 2025, police said eMbalenhle leads crime across the Gert Sibande District and is among the stations with the highest crime in Mpumalanga; murder, house robberies and gender-based violence were named as the major challenges. — [Ridge Times, 9 Dec 2025](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2025/12/09/embalenhle-struggles-with-highest-crime-rate-in-district/)
- [AGGREGATOR] eMbalenhle precinct: 2,414 reported crimes in April 2025-March 2026, down 4.1% on 2024/25; most frequently reported category is serious assault (assault GBH) with 533 cases. — [SafeSuburb, Embalenhle](https://safesuburb.co.za/mpumalanga/gert-sibande/embalenhle/embalenhle/)
- [AGGREGATOR, conflicting basis] A second aggregator using calendar year 2025 gives eMbalenhle 1,805 crimes, 47 murders (2.6% of the total), 40 residential robberies and 24 "sexual assault" cases, a 4.9% decrease on 2024, and an unofficial rate of 7,127 per 100,000. The two aggregators' totals disagree (2,414 vs 1,805), probably because of different periods and category sets. — [SafetyBrief, Embalenhle](https://www.safetybrief.co.za/province/mpumalanga/gert-sibande/embalenhle)

- [CONFIRMED-EXTRACT] SAPS publishes reported counts per precinct, not a per-resident rate, so there is no official "crime rate" for eMbalenhle. — [SafeSuburb, Embalenhle](https://safesuburb.co.za/mpumalanga/gert-sibande/embalenhle/embalenhle/)
- [CONFIRMED-EXTRACT] In the 2024/25 fourth-quarter (Jan-Mar 2025) release, the six Mpumalanga stations with the most sexual-offence cases were Masoyi, Calcutta, KaBokweni, Acornhoek, Vosman and eMbalenhle. Provincially, sexual offences rose from 899 to 1,027 and robbery with aggravating circumstances fell from 2,146 to 1,805 over the comparison quarter. — [Lowvelder, 30 May 2025](https://www.citizen.co.za/lowvelder/news-headlines/local-news/2025/05/30/crime-statistics-mpumalanga-registers-a-sharp-increase-in-rape-incidents/)
- [CONFIRMED-EXTRACT] 332 people were murdered in Mpumalanga in April-June 2024 (DA release citing SAPS Q1 2024/25). — [DA Mpumalanga, Sept 2024](https://mpumalanga.da.org.za/2024/09/over-300-people-murdered-in-mpumalanga-between-april-and-june-2024)
- [CONFIRMED-EXTRACT] In one week (3-11 August 2026) 79 cases were opened at eMbalenhle station, including murder, attempted murder and rape; police singled out rapes of minors as the main worry and partly blamed parental negligence. — [Ridge Times, 19 Aug 2026](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/local-crime-news/2026/08/19/79-cases-reported-at-embalenhle-police-station-in-one-week-2/)

- [CONFIRMED-EXTRACT] In September 2023 the Community Safety MEC said the Govan Mbeki cluster of stations was among those with the highest crime in the province. — [Ridge Times, 12 Sept 2023](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2023/09/12/mec-tackles-police-at-evander-and-embalenhle/)
- [CONFIRMED-EXTRACT] The official source for station tables exists: SAPS Q3 2025/26 release (Oct-Dec 2025). Not opened. — [SAPS PDF](https://www.saps.gov.za/services/downloads/2025/2025-2026_-_3rd_Quarter_WEB.pdf)

### Inferences
- Roughly 47 murders a year in one township precinct (if the aggregator figure is right) is about one a week, which fits the police's own "murder is the main challenge" statement. Treat 47 as indicative only.
- Secunda town (formerly white, plant-adjacent suburbs, malls) and eMbalenhle (township 10-15 km away) have near-equal case volumes but opposite profiles: Secunda is theft/property-heavy with 223 violent crimes; eMbalenhle is assault-GBH-heavy. A realistic Secunda detective handles theft, fraud, burglary and the occasional domestic murder; the homicide load sits at eMbalenhle and is prosecuted through Evander.
- Court geography inferred from cases below: eMbalenhle and Evander matters first appear in **Evander Magistrate's Court**; Secunda matters in **Secunda Magistrate's/Regional Court**; serious murders go to the **Mpumalanga High Court sitting in Middelburg or on circuit in Delmas** (also Breyten); **Bethal Regional Court** takes regional matters from the east of the municipality.

### Gaps
- No official per-category counts (murder, attempted murder, aggravated robbery, house/business robbery, hijacking, theft out of motor vehicle, sexual offences, drug-related, commercial crime) for Secunda, eMbalenhle, Evander, Kinross, Bethal or Leandra for 2022/23-2025/26. The SAPS station spreadsheet and ISS Crime Hub were unreachable.
- Could not confirm whether Trichardt has its own station (the aggregators list Secunda, Embalenhle, Evander, Kinross as precincts; Trichardt appears to fall under Secunda — unverified).
- No Bethal or Leandra figures at all.

## 2. Documented murders and serious cases, 2018-2026

### Takeaway
The documented pattern is dominated by intimate-partner and domestic killings, house-robbery murders of elderly residents, tavern shootings, and killings involving police and security officers; the one clear political killing (a ward councillor and a ward committee member in eMbalenhle) had no reported arrests. Serious cases end in the High Court at Middelburg or Delmas, usually on guilty pleas or confessions.

**Intimate-partner / domestic**

He fled and was arrested in Mozambique; the Secunda station commander, Col Vena Sigo, confirmed the arrest. He pleaded guilty in the High Court at Middelburg: 40 years for murder plus 5 years for defeating the administration of justice (concurrent), declared unfit to possess a firearm. Reported August 2026. — [Ridge Times, 6 Aug 2026](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/local-crime-news/2026/08/06/high-court-sends-secunda-wife-killer-to-jail-for-40-years/); [LNN: arrested in Mozambique](https://www.citizen.co.za/network-news/lnn/article/secunda-murder-suspect-arrested-in-mozambique/); [Briefly](https://briefly.co.za/south-africa/240080-secunda-murder-case-husband-arrested-mozambique-wife-dead-bag/)

### Inferences
- How cases are actually solved in the documented record: confession and guilty plea (pensioner case, wife case); the suspect handing himself over (constable); flight and cross-border arrest (Mozambique is the natural bolt-hole via the N17/N4 corridor); arrest in transit to Gauteng; and, rarely, a circumstantial case run to trial (the Serbian matter). None of the extracts describes forensic breakthroughs; ballistics is mentioned as a pending step.
- Delay is a consistent texture: 4 years (2022 killing, 2026 sentence), 10 years (2011 to 2021).
- Sentencing range seen: 7 years (security officer), 18 years (girlfriend killing), 40 years (wife killing on a plea), life (premeditated or rape-murder).

### Gaps
- No documented contract killing with a proven paymaster, no taxi-conflict killing and no tender-linked killing was found for Secunda/eMbalenhle/Evander in 2018-2026. Searches returned taxi violence only elsewhere in Mpumalanga (e.g. Mkhuhlu, March 2023). One headline refers to bail for a Mpumalanga officer accused of stealing an R5 rifle "linked to a taxi boss murder" — location not established. — [LNN](https://www.citizen.co.za/network-news/lnn/article/bail-for-mpumalanga-cop-who-allegedly-stole-r5-rifle-linked-to-taxi-boss-murder/)
- Exact date, arrests and outcome in the councillor double murder not established.
- SAFLII judgments were not reachable; no court-record verification of any sentence.

## 3. Crime tied to the Sasol plant and the mines

### Takeaway
Documented plant-linked crime is mostly supplier-side fraud (substituted goods, invalid guarantees, B-BBEE fronting) investigated by the Secunda-based Hawks and heard in Evander or Secunda magistrates' courts, plus small insider theft (a driver colluding with a gate guard). At Evander the documented mine crime is illegal mining at Winkelhaak with deaths among rival groups. No sabotage prosecution or organised Sasol copper/diesel/coal syndicate case was found.

### Cited Findings
- [CONFIRMED-EXTRACT] November 2021, Secunda: Hawks arrested a truck driver and a security guard at a gate of a Sasol-complex company in the early hours of a Sunday for product worth about R100,000; the driver had been communicating with the guard by cellphone and the guard was charged with conspiracy. Both appeared in Secunda Magistrate's Court (conspiracy; possession of suspected stolen property). Sasol later said the product was petrol, not chemicals, which police called "bizarre and curious". — [IOL, 15 Nov 2021](https://iol.co.za/news/crime-and-courts/2021-11-15-security-guard-and-driver-in-court-for-allegedly-stealing-chemicals-from-sasol/); [The Citizen, Nov 2021](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/south-africa/crime/2909698/sasol-chemicals-theft-saga-update-november-2021/)
- [CONFIRMED-EXTRACT] 2022: Hawks arrested the directors of an industrial-equipment supplier after an internal Sasol audit found they supplied cheaper products (actuators) than those billed; they appeared in Evander Magistrate's Court; Hawks said they had already repaid money to Sasol. — [IOL](https://www.iol.co.za/news/crime-and-courts/directors-arrested-for-allegedly-defrauding-sasol-by-supplying-cheap-goods-disguised-as-premium-cace4c83-7005-448b-bffc-a3207a0965ec)

### Inferences
- The realistic "plant crime" for fiction is quiet and paper-based: invoice/substitution fraud found by internal audit, then handed to the Hawks' Secunda office; and gate-level collusion between drivers and contract security. The petrol-versus-chemicals dispute shows complainant and police can disagree about what was even stolen.
- Regional pattern (Eskom Tutuka, Matla) suggests copper theft with inside help from staff and security supervisors is the norm on Highveld industrial sites; it is plausible but **undocumented here** for Sasol Secunda.

### Gaps
- No documented Sasol Secunda case of sabotage, diesel or coal syndicate theft, or arrests of Sasol Mining employees was found. Absence in search extracts is not proof of absence; Sasol handles much internally.
- No Evander illegal-mining incident dated 2022-2026 was found.
- Outcomes of the R279m, R400m and equipment-substitution cases not established.

## 4. Police: station problems, IPID cases, mob justice

### Takeaway
eMbalenhle station is in documented crisis: its own commander said in February 2025 that crime was "out of control", detectives carry about 150 dockets each, fewer than half its vehicles ran in late 2024, and nine of its members were arrested in 2025 including officers charged with two separate deaths in custody or pursuit. Residents allege collusion with criminals and missing dockets, and mob killings recur.

### Cited Findings
- [CONFIRMED-EXTRACT] 12 February 2025: at a meeting with residents and victims at the station, the eMbalenhle station commander, Col Khuselwa Matrose, admitted crime was out of control; a community leader said police were failing; residents alleged officers tell complainants to trace suspects themselves.
