# Sasol's company town shows its seams

Secunda is a Sasol-built company town of about 40,000 people on the Mpumalanga Highveld, planned from 1974 by Swiss architect Max Kirchhofer as low-density "quarter cells" threaded with green strips, and it still has almost exactly the shape he gave it. Everything in it faces the coal-to-liquids plant about 3 km to the south-south-west, whose 301 m chimney, cooling towers and flares are in sight and often earshot of town, and which between 70% and 80% of residents depend on. The former black township eMbalenhle, about three times as populous on a ninth of the land, sits on the far side of the plant, downwind, and is where the sewage spills, power cut-offs, burned municipal offices and distrusted police station are. In 2026 the town is a place of patched potholes, dry taps, a municipality owing Eskom more than R6 billion, private armed response arriving before the police, and a plant that has been written down to nothing on Sasol's books but is still running. That is solid ground for a grounded detective game, and the documented record of coal-quality fraud, truck swaps and compromised laboratories in Mpumalanga gives the invented plot real precedents. The limit is visual and street-level: all five researchers worked only from search-result extracts, no page was read in full, no map was opened and no photograph was viewed. The institutions, addresses, dates and numbers below are usable; compass layout, building appearance, vehicle liveries, uniforms and night views are not confirmed and must be checked against Street View and the named photo sets before anything is drawn.

## How to read this report: three grades of evidence

Page fetching was blocked for every host the researchers tried, so every "confirmed" fact here means "stated in a search extract attributed to the cited page", which is one step weaker than a page read end to end. This report uses three grades. A statement with a citation and no qualifier was stated by a primary, official or reputable source in the extract. A statement described as **weakly sourced** rests on one directory, one estate-agent advert, a party statement or a source that conflicts with another. A statement described as **unverified** or **general knowledge** has no source at all from this research; it is the researchers' background knowledge of Highveld towns and is included only because an artist needs a starting hypothesis. ## A 1974 Swiss plan still fixes the map

### Sasol founded the town in two years and named it "second"

Sasol's subsidiary Sasol (Transvaal) Townships was tasked with establishing the town in 1974 ([countrytravel.co.za](https://countrytravel.co.za/mpumalanga/secunda)). The **first township area was proclaimed on 28 June 1976**; construction of Sasol Two began the same year ([Sasol heritage page](https://www.sasol.com/celebrating-our-heritage)). The name is Latin for "second", the second Sasol coal-to-fuel town after Sasolburg, and Sasol Two was commissioned on 1 March 1980 and Sasol Three on 10 May 1982 ([Wikipedia, Secunda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secunda,_South_Africa)), although Wikipedia's Secunda CTL article gives 1980 and 1984 ([Wikipedia, Secunda CTL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secunda_CTL)). Sasol commissioned **Max Kirchhofer**, the planner of Sasolburg, in 1974; he envisaged about 13,000 residents in a place that then had no tar roads or telephones ([Bloomberg, 19 Jan 2024](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-01-19/south-african-town-that-rose-with-coal-faces-uncertain-future)). The sources name two different "first residents", so that detail should not be used.

Kirchhofer's own 1982 account places the plant on the southern slopes of the Bankspruit valley, about 7 km south-west of Trichardt and 8 km south-east of Evander, with the town site south of a provincial road then being built between Evander and Trichardt and contiguous with Trichardt across the railway line ([Kirchhofer 1982](https://journals.co.za/doi/pdf/10.10520/AJA1012280X_443)). Planners chose to develop Trichardt and Secunda as one town called Secunda while Evander stayed separate ([Wikipedia, Secunda](https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Secunda_(town))). An environmental impact report puts the town about **3 km north-north-east of the plant** and eMbalenhle about 5 km west of it ([RHDHV, Sasol gas turbine EIR](http://www.rhdhv.co.za/media/201210/EIR/Sasol%20SGP-Final%20EIR.pdf)).

### Quarter cells, schools within 800 m, and a continuous green network

The design drew on garden cities, British new towns and the Radburn neighbourhood concept, in which superblocks separate footpaths from car traffic through interior greenways and culs-de-sac ([Geoforum, 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0016718524001866)). The residential areas are called **"quarter cells"** and are bordered by main roads and green strips ([Wikipedia, Secunda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secunda,_South_Africa)). Kirchhofer's paper says each stage of growth kept "a simple coherent layout pattern, contained within a circular road", with a ring road giving access to cul-de-sac streets; that **primary schools sit at the centre of each precinct within 800 m walking distance**, reachable without crossing a main road; and that a continuous system of landscaped green strips runs through the housing in place of isolated parks ([Kirchhofer 1982](https://journals.co.za/doi/pdf/10.10520/AJA1012280X_443)). He also describes a separate network of pedestrian and cycle paths to schools and local shops, and tree belts marking "the break between work and leisure", including one 32 m wide along provincial road P9-5 above a high-pressure Sasol gas main ([Kirchhofer 1982](https://journals.co.za/doi/pdf/10.10520/AJA1012280X_443)). The town centre was sized to the town, fringed by housing, and served by a ring street giving access to its parking areas; a garbled extract gives one dimension as 700 m ([Kirchhofer 1982](https://journals.co.za/doi/pdf/10.10520/AJA1012280X_443)).

The plan was also a class and race map. At a meeting on 19 July 1979 the planners set out four social classes tied to housing types and locations, with the best units placed against the highest-grade green strips, named "Green Area" and "Duckpond"; Secunda received the lowest residential density in the region and eMbalenhle the highest. The same study calls Secunda "perhaps the most pertinent grassroots example" of the apartheid city model and says the original spatial structure "remains largely unaltered today" ([Geoforum, 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0016718524001866)). Residents still say "Die Groenstrook" for the area overlooking the Duck Pond ([Geoforum, 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0016718524001866)), a tourism piece names the pond "Eende Dam" with play areas and jungle gyms ([Getaway](https://getaway.co.za/?p=15514992)), and the green strips are now a crime concern: a January 2025 headline reads "Crime spikes in Secunda's green strips" ([Ridge Times, 30 Jan 2025](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/local-crime-news/2025/01/30/crime-spikes-in-green-strips/)). Underpasses were not confirmed for Secunda by any extract, and neither was any ring road around the whole town.

### Suburbs are numbered extensions, not named neighbourhoods

Census 2011 treats Secunda as one main place of 173.71 km² with 23 sub-places, nearly all called "Secunda Ext N": Ext 22 is the most populous extension (5,361 people), followed by Ext 20 (2,481) and Ext 9 (2,440), with the largest unit simply "Secunda SP1" (10,339 people) and a sub-place called "Sasol Secunda" covering the plant ([Census 2011, Secunda](https://census2011.adrianfrith.com/place/866006)). A game can safely write addresses as "Secunda Ext 22". Estate agents overlay a few names: **Sunset Park** is well attested, with three- and four-bedroom houses on stands of roughly 930 to 1,220 m² at about R0.9 million to R2.1 million in 2025 ([MyProperty](https://www.myproperty.co.za/properties/sunset-park-house-for-sale-ent0308591)). "Secunda Central", "Terra Nova", "Kruinpark", "Cosmos Valley" and "The One Secunda" appear in listings and are weakly sourced ([Property24](https://www.property24.com/townhouses-for-sale/secunda/mpumalanga/110)); "Suidheuwel" and "Noordrand" appeared in listing URLs but could not be confirmed as Secunda suburbs. Which extension lies in which direction, and when each was proclaimed, is unknown. A 1,250-hectare "Secunda New Town" (Ext 50 and 51) is planned as infill between eMbalenhle, Secunda and Evander ([Urban Scene Architecture](https://www.urbanscene.co.za/projects/p/secunda-new-town-development)).

### The typical house is a tan-brick bungalow with accretions

Bloomberg in January 2024 described "neighborhoods of bungalows built with tan bricks and painted in earth tones" along a tree-lined boulevard ([BizNews reprint](https://www.biznews.com/news/sasols-secunda-town-built-coal-uncertain-future)). That one sentence is the only sourced description of what the houses look like. Estate-agent adverts, which are weak sources but consistent, show stands of 866 to 1,303 m² and a recurring kit: swimming pool, lapa with built-in braai, double garage plus two to five carports, Wendy houses, a separate flatlet and a JoJo water tank, at R1.4 million to R2.9 million for three to five bedrooms ([MyProperty](https://www.myproperty.co.za/en-za/for-sale/mpumalanga/secunda)). Newer stock is townhouse complexes and gated estates advertising electric fencing, cameras and guards ([Mitula](https://homes.mitula.co.za/homes/security-complex-secunda)). At the lower tier, some residents were moved into 75 to 90 m² houses on subdivided stands ([Geoforum, 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0016718524001866)), and Sasol Mining has built employee housing in both Secunda and eMbalenhle ([Sasol](https://www.sasol.com/index.php/sasol-mining-hands-over-218-houses-part-its-housing-project)). Roof material, boundary-wall type and the original Sasol house designs were not sourced.

The many carports and flatlets fit a documented local industry. Guesthouses market directly to contractors and shutdown crews: one is called "Qhambalala Contractors Guesthouse" ([Plek](https://plek.co.za/products/qhambalala-contractors-guesthouse-secunda-secunda-mpumalanga-south-africa)) and the lofts at Lake Umuzi ran a "Sasol Shutdown 2024" page ([Lake Umuzi](https://old.lakeumuzi.co.za/?p=4046)). The municipality's draft housing plan cites "demand for accommodation linked to industrial activity" and backyard rental pressure ([Govan Mbeki draft Housing Sector Plan 2026](https://www.govanmbeki.gov.za/wp-content/housing/housing_sector_plans2026_draft_strategy.pdf)).

### Condition is better documented than appearance

No extract describes the materials, colours, canopies, signage or storey heights of any CBD building. What is documented is the street life. Rough sleepers were removed by the municipality and police in 2018, were back by 2020 ([Ridge Times, 21 Mar 2020](https://citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2020/03/21/municipality-fails-answer-homeless-people-secunda-cbd)), and in **June 2026** were again putting up shelters "using tents, boxes and clotheslines along boundary walls" at the Nico Diederich and Walter Sisulu corner beside the taxi rank ([Ridge Times, 10 Jun 2026](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/local-news/2026/06/10/concerns-mount-over-vagrant-activity-in-secunda-cbd/)). Businesses, not the municipality, cleaned the CBD in 2020, and a private firm filled potholes, repainted parking bays and claimed 24-hour CCTV in 2021 ([Ridge Times, 22 Apr 2021](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2021/04/22/imojoe-international-takes-on-clean-up-challenge-secunda-cbd/)); residents ran an "Adopt a pothole" drive ([Ridge Times, 22 Jan 2021](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2021/01/22/adopt-a-pothole-in-secunda/)); and in December 2025 private companies with Sasol backing patched main roads themselves ([Ridge Times, 24 Dec 2025](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2025/12/24/companies-take-hands-and-fix-roads-in-secunda/)). Police said in 2024 that at least 20% of crimes reported weekly are thefts out of vehicles around the CBD, a hotspot for remote jamming ([Ridge Times, 24 Sep 2024](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2024/09/24/stay-alert-as-crime-spikes/)).

### The mall, the lake, the club and the casino cluster near the plant gate

**Secunda Mall opened on 23 October 2013**, anchored by Checkers Hyper, Edgars, Game, Pick n Pay and Woolworths with six Ster-Kinekor cinemas ([Secunda Mall](https://shopsecundamall.co.za/about-us)). It has open and undercover parking and two trading levels, claims over 90 stores and about 650,000 visitors a month, and is the area's only regional centre ([SA-Venues](https://www.sa-venues.com/things-to-do/mpumalanga/secunda-mall)). Lake Umuzi Waterfront says the mall, the "Sasol Synfuels Main Gate", the Sasol Recreational Club and Curro Secunda are "only 2 minutes away" and Graceland about five ([Lake Umuzi](https://old.lakeumuzi.co.za)), so the leisure cluster sits on the plant side of town. Lake Umuzi is a dam-side precinct with a water park, a thatched lapa-style restaurant, lodging and an indoor Action Sports arena; its restaurant list changes between sources ([SA-Venues](https://www.sa-venues.com/things-to-do/mpumalanga/lake-umuzi-waterfront/)). Graceland, now trading as Peermont Walmont at Graceland, has a casino with roughly 300 to 380 slot machines and about 19 tables, a hotel of about 98 rooms and an 18-hole course attributed to Gary Player ([SA-Venues](https://sa-venues.com/things-to-do/mpumalanga/graceland-hotel-casino); [World Casino Directory](https://www.worldcasinodirectory.com/casino/graceland-hotel-casino-country-club-resort-1565)). Its address on Embalenhle Road places it between the two communities.

## The plant fills the horizon and the mines ring it

### One 301 m chimney, mirrored halves and permanent plumes

The tallest structure in Secunda is the **301 m chimney** at the steam plant ([Wikipedia, Secunda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secunda,_South_Africa)). It is a multi-flue stack with four brick-lined concrete flues of 4.6 m diameter inside a concrete windshield of 26 m internal diameter; one source gives a 292 m windshield with 300 m flues, so "about 300 m" is the safe wording ([Wikiwand, Sasol II](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Sasol_II); [SkyscraperPage](https://skyscraperpage.com/b6525)). A 2018 Daily Maverick piece speaks of "the two main stacks" emitting 22.4 million cubic metres of gas an hour at 185°C ([Daily Maverick, 22 Jan 2018](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2018-01-22-op-ed-foul-air-on-the-highveld-the-sour-smell-of-environmental-racism/)), so whether an artist should draw one dominant stack or two is unresolved.

The plant is laid out as **mirrored West and East halves** (Sasol Two and Sasol Three), which is why its units are numbered in pairs. It has 17 coal-fired boilers in two steam plants and a main flare system of two flares, one West and one East, plus local flares at the coal-lock units ([Sasol, Secunda Operations Synfuels document](https://www.sasol.com/sites/default/files/2025-03/Secunda%20Operations%20Synfuels.pdf); [SRK Atmospheric Impact Report, 2021](https://cdn-web-content.srk.com/upload/user/image/ANNEXURE_A_SYNFUELS_Atmospheric_Impact_Report20210831154201071.pdf)). One source counts 84 Lurgi-type fixed-bed gasifiers, 42 per half ([Portfolio Planning PLUS](https://portfolio-pplus.com/SiteMains/Details/1367)). The cooling towers are natural-draught towers from the late 1970s and early 1980s, refurbished from 2009; the article mentions "four towers" without saying whether that is the site total, so the **number of cooling towers is unverified** ([Mining Weekly, 11 Feb 2014](https://www.miningweekly.com/article/iwc-performs-online-refurbishment-of-utility-cooling-towers-at-sasol-secunda-2014-02-11)). The licensed extent of the operation is 24.05 km² ([Sasol, Secunda Operations Synfuels document](https://www.sasol.com/sites/default/files/2025-03/Secunda%20Operations%20Synfuels.pdf)), and roughly 35 million tonnes of coal a year go into the gasifiers ([Oxpeckers, Sept 2026](https://oxpeckers.org/2026/09/carbon-colossus/)). Residents use gate names as landmarks: local reports name **Charlie 1** and **Charlie 3** ([Ridge Times, 6 Sep 2018](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2018/09/06/solidaritystrike-white-employees-sasol-community-strike-charlie-1-secunda/)).

Around the plant are very large ash facilities: Fine Ash Dam 6, built south-west of Dam 5, has over 1.6 million m² of liner ([WBHO](https://www.wbho.co.za/portfolio/sasol-fine-ash-dam-6/)), and gasification ash is red and white-to-grey fused clinker stacked on a coarse-ash dump ([University of Pretoria, Mahlaba 2011](https://repository.up.ac.za/bitstream/2263/16641/1/Mahlaba_Physical(2011).pdf)). No source describes the plant at night, flare noise or flaring frequency.

### Shutdown season arrives in late winter and fills every bed

Sasol runs a statutory maintenance shutdown every year. The one precisely dated example ran from **30 August to 24 September 2021**, planned at 1.3 million man-hours, with Sasol warning the town to expect an influx of people and heavier traffic ([Ridge Times, 31 Aug 2021](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2021/08/31/sasols-yearly-shutdown-begins/); [Sasol media release](https://sasol.com/media-centre/media-releases/sasol-maintenance-secunda-shutdown-2021)). The on-site workforce was expected to approach 20,000 ([Sasol statement](https://www.sasol.com/sasol-statement-sasol-enhances-existing-preventative-workplace-covid-19-controls-its-upcoming)). In 2010 about 14,500 additional workers and over 100 contractors came in, **all public accommodation in the area was fully occupied**, and Sasol housed about 3,900 people itself ([Sasol](https://www.sasol.com/sasol-secunda-successfully-executes-largest-shutdown-its-kind)). Shutdowns come as "phase" shutdowns of part of the plant or "total" shutdowns of the East or West factory ([TMS Group](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/tmsgroupindustrialservices_sasol-secunda-eastern-total-and-phase-shutdown-activity-6981586959362084864-mJ4i)). "September shutdown" is a safe phrase; dates and contractor numbers for 2024 to 2026 were not found.

### Five underground mines, three replacements and 20 km conveyors

Sasol Mining's Secunda complex began as five underground mines: Bosjesspruit, Brandspruit, Middelbult, Syferfontein and Twistdraai ([Mail & Guardian, 17 Oct 2007](https://mg.co.za/article/2007-10-17-sasol-strike-enters-fourth-day-fuel-output-spared/)). A R14 to R15.3 billion programme replaced three of them: **Impumelelo for Brandspruit, Shondoni for Middelbult, Thubelisha for Twistdraai** ([Mining Weekly, 8 June 2017](https://www.miningweekly.com/article/sasol-on-track-with-r153-billion-mine-replacement-project-2017-06-08)). Coal reaches the plant by overland conveyor: 21 km from Shondoni, billed as the longest single-flight conveyor without a booster drive in Africa, and 27 km from Impumelelo; Bosjesspruit's Irenedale shaft is tied to its main shaft by a 17.9 km underground conveyor ([Sasol Mining](https://sasol.com/index.php/what-we-do/energy/sasol-mining)). The coal converges on Sasol Coal Supply, which has East and West stockpile areas ([WSP geotechnical scoping report, 2026](https://www.wsp.com/-/media/service/south-africa/2026-documents/za0068671-2768---sasol-g2p-dsr/09-appendix-g1_geotechnical-scoping-report_part-1.pdf)). Shaft and section names such as Irenedale, Riversdale, Tweedraai and Weltevrede read as farm names, which suggests mines here are named for the farm they sit on; that is an inference, not a sourced rule. What a shaft complex looks like on the surface was not sourced.

All the Secunda mines are **bord-and-pillar** operations ([Engineering News, 17 June 2016](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/sasol-targeting-step-change-in-coal-mining-productivity-2016-06-17)). Sasol job adverts list licensed operators for continuous miner, shuttle car, roof bolter and load-haul-dump ([Sasol careers](https://jobs.sasol.com/job/Sasolburg-Licensed-Machine-Operator-Roof-bolter%2C-Shuttle-Car%2C-Continuous-Miner%2C-Load-Haul-Dump-&-Tractor/1264174001)). A court record of a 2009 underground sit-in gives three overlapping ten-hour shifts: **night 22:00 to 08:00, morning 07:00 to 17:00, afternoon 16:00 to 02:00** ([Labour Appeal Court, 9 Sept 2021](https://lawlibrary.org.za/akn/za-gp/judgment/zalac/2021/291/eng@2021-09-09/source)). Those times are 17 years old and plant shift times were not found at all. The worst recent mine accident killed three employees at Bosjesspruit on 26 October 2021 ([Engineering News, 28 Oct 2021](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/three-employees-killed-at-sasols-bosjesspruit-colliery-2021-10-28)), and one of two men who died at Twistdraai/Thubelisha in 2019 was reportedly run over by a shuttle car ([Ridge Times, 1 Oct 2019](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2019/10/01/two-die-sasol-mine-two-monthts/)).

### A 3.7 m seam cut by dolerite, and a quality crisis about stone

The economic seam is the **No. 4 Lower: 3.7 m thick on average, with its base 40 to 241 m down (average 135 m)** ([Sasol Form 20-F Exhibit 96.1, FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/314590/000141057825001910/ssl-20250630xex96d1.htm)), dipping south at about half a degree ([Sasol Form 20-F, 2016](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/314590/000104746916015748/a2229729z20-f.htm)). Jurassic dolerite cuts the coalfield as dykes 1 to 4 m wide, mostly trending east-west, and as nine sills that displace the seam by as much as 137 m ([CSIR, McGill 2015](https://researchspace.csir.co.za/bitstream/handle/10204/8153/McGill_2015.pdf)). At Secunda the sills are numbered; the No. 8 sill intersects the seam many times, with the Shondoni reserve area worst affected ([Sasol Exhibit 96.1, FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/314590/000141057825001910/ssl-20250630xex96d1.htm)).

How far dolerite spoils the coal is contested, and the disagreement is useful. One study found a devolatilised zone 17 to 19 m wide beside a dyke near Secunda ([University of Pretoria](https://repository.up.ac.za/handle/2263/50780)); a 2020 doctorate put the thermal influence at about 5 m ([University of Pretoria](https://repository.up.ac.za/handle/2263/85553)); and a study of **more than 8,000 Secunda boreholes found no correlation** between dolerite position or thickness and coal quality by proximate analysis ([Bussio and Roberts, 2016](https://repository.up.ac.za/bitstream/handle/2263/55850/Bussio_Large_2016.pdf?sequence=1)). The effect is local and shows only in close-spaced drilling.

Sasol's real feedstock trouble from 2021 was reported as **"sinks"**, stone and other non-coal material in the feed, at times 20% from Bosjesspruit and Thubelisha, which cut gasifier yield and damaged equipment ([Engineering News, 14 Mar 2025](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/repurposed-twistdraai-destoning-plant-key-to-sasols-plan-for-recovering-secundas-output-2025-03-14)). Sasol bought coal on the open market from December 2021 ([News24, 14 Dec 2021](https://www.news24.com/fin24/companies/industrial/sasol-secunda-production-drops-over-significant-coal-supply-shortfall-20211214)), said in April 2025 it would buy more ([Engineering News, 17 Apr 2025](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/sasol-to-buy-in-more-coal-amid-ongoing-quality-problems-at-own-mines-2025-04-17)), converted the Twistdraai export plant into a 10 Mt a year destoning plant opened on 13 March 2026 ([Engineering News, 13 Mar 2026](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/sasol-launches-destoning-plant-to-improve-coal-quality-strengthen-operational-performance-2026-03-13)), and by February 2026 was getting about 12% sinks and starting to cut outside purchases ([Mining Weekly, 23 Feb 2026](https://www.miningweekly.com/article/sasol-to-begin-reducing-external-coal-purchases-as-destoning-plant-raises-quality-2026-02-23)). No source links the sinks problem to dolerite. Sasol also bought about 5.2 Mt a year from Thungela's Isibonelo colliery under a 20-year contract whose end date the sources give variously as 2025 or 2026 ([Meridian Economics](https://transitionpetrochemicals.meridianeconomics.co.za/coal-3/)).

Exploration practice is sourced only in outline. South African coalfields are drilled for core of 47.6 mm (NQ) or 60.5 mm (TNW) diameter ([Hancox and Götz, 2014](https://repository.up.ac.za/bitstream/2263/58453/1/Hancox_South_2014.pdf)); core is logged as bright coal, dull banded coal and coaly shale, with bright coal sometimes carrying disseminated pyrite ([SAIMM Journal, 2024](https://scielo.org.za/scielo.php?script=sci_arttext&pid=S2225-62532024001000004&lng=en&nrm=iso&tlng=en)); quality is measured by proximate analysis; and a public resource statement must be signed by a **Competent Person** registered with SACNASP under the SAMREC code and SANS 10320 ([SAMCODE](https://www.samcode.co.za/samcode-ssc/samrec/coal)). Core-shed layout, tray labelling, wireline logging and who signs a laboratory certificate were not sourced.

## eMbalenhle lives downwind, and three older towns flank it

### eMbalenhle: three times the people on a ninth of the land

eMbalenhle was established in 1978 on the farm Langverwacht 282 IS as a dormitory town for Sasol workers ([Govan Mbeki IDP 2011-12](https://cogta.mpg.gov.za/IDP/GertSibande2011-12/GovanMbeki2011-2012.pdf)), with much of its housing built by Sasol ([Wikipedia, Embalenhle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embalenhle)). Census 2011 counted **118,889 people in 35,404 households on 19.65 km²**, 99% Black African, with isiZulu the first language of 49.8% and isiXhosa and Sesotho about 10% each ([Census 2011, Embalenhle](https://census2011.adrianfrith.com/place/866011)). Secunda the same year had 40,198 people on 173.71 km², 71.9% white and 69.5% Afrikaans-speaking ([Census 2011, Secunda](https://census2011.adrianfrith.com/place/866006)). Those figures are 15 years old; no Census 2022 place-level data was found, and the municipality as a whole grew from 294,538 to 310,117 ([citypopulation.de](https://citypopulation.de/en/southafrica/admin/mpumalanga/MP307__govan_mbeki)). Like Secunda, eMbalenhle is divided into numbered extensions, the largest being Ext 18 with 17,462 people; informal settlement is recorded in parts of Extensions 4, 5, 7, 9, 11 and 18 ([draft Housing Sector Plan 2026](https://www.govanmbeki.gov.za/wp-content/housing/housing_sector_plans2026_draft_strategy.pdf)). Named places in reporting include "Wellas", "Crossroads" and the taxi rank "Blue Rank". No source describes its house forms.

The township was placed behind a buffer. The Geoforum study gives the separation as about 17 km by the fastest route, with the Sasol site, Evander and the Winkelhaak mines forming the buffer ([Geoforum, 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0016718524001866)); Wikipedia says 10 km ([Wikipedia, Embalenhle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embalenhle)). The likeliest reading is 10 km in a straight line and 17 km by road. Road numbering between the two is inconsistent in the sources: the R580 is named as the eMbalenhle-Secunda link, the R546 as the Evander-Standerton road past eMbalenhle, the Evander-Secunda road as "R580/R77, also called the P185", and "P216/1" as the eMbalenhle-Secunda road under rehabilitation ([The Citizen](https://citizen.co.za/news/news-cns/1811751/sasol-apparently-not-formally-informed-of-todays-mpumalanga-protest/); [Ridge Times, 22 Jan 2026](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2026/01/22/motorists-dodge-huge-potholes-along-p185/); [Business Day, 7 Feb 2024](https://www.pressreader.com/south-africa/business-day/20240207/281522230987186)). The road network needs a map check.

### Burned offices, a looted mall and sewage in the streets

In October 2017 protesters stoned and burned the main municipal building and its branch office and torched trucks, after Eskom power interruptions tied to municipal debt; about 100 people were arrested ([News24, 16 Oct 2017](https://www.news24.com/News24/protesters-burn-down-municipal-offices-vehicles-in-mpumalanga-20171016)). In **June 2018** protesters burned tyres between Extension 12 and the turn-off to Sasol's Charlie 3 gate and looted and burned **Mall@eMba**, the post office and a filling station ([Ridge Times, 26 Jun 2018](https://citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2018/06/26/police-already-arrested-35-people-protest-embalenhle); [News24, 27 Jun 2018](https://news24.com/Video/SouthAfrica/News/watch-footage-shows-razed-gutted-mpumalanga-mall-after-violent-protests-20180627)). The municipal offices were rebuilt in 2019, burned again in an electricity protest in 2021 and reopened in January 2023 ([Ridge Times, 28 Jan 2023](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/local-news/2023/01/28/gmm-offices-reopen-in-embalenhle/)). Whether the mall was rebuilt is unverified, and no dated protest between 2021 and 2026 was found.

Since then the story is infrastructure. The municipality was fined R200 million by the Bethal Regional Court in 2023 for discharging untreated sewage ([Ridge Times, 10 Jul 2025](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2025/07/10/da-demands-auditor-general-probe-into-claims-on-embalenhle-sewer-plant-2/)). In January 2024 residents fetched water from sewer manholes and streams ([Ridge Times, 30 Jan 2024](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/local-news/2024/01/30/ongoing-water-crisis-angers-community/)). Sewage spills are reported from Ext 20 in 2024, Wellas in 2025, into homes in March 2026 and in Ext 18 in July 2026 ([Ridge Times, 10 Mar 2026](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/local-news/2026/03/10/residents-furious-as-sewage-spills-into-homes-2/); [Ridge Times, 16 Jul 2026](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/local-news/2026/07/16/residents-demand-permanent-solution-to-recurring-sewage-spills-2/)).

## Daily life runs on the plant's calendar

### Work, unions and the "locals first" fault line

The town has an annual rhythm as well as a daily one. Shutdown brings thousands of outside artisans; a 2015 report quoted an eMbalenhle resident who rented rooms to contract workers saying contractor lay-offs threw the area into turmoil ([Sunday Times, 15 Mar 2015](https://www.pressreader.com/south-africa/sunday-times-1107/20150315/282853664428013)). Union membership carries social meaning. NUM, CEPPWAWU, SACWU and Solidarity signed Sasol Mining's 2016 wage deal while AMCU struck ([Engineering News, 10 Nov 2016](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/unions-sasol-mining-ink-wage-deal-amcu-members-go-on-strike-2016-11-10)). In 2018 Solidarity struck over the exclusion of white employees from the Khanyisa share scheme, timing the action against the September shutdown; local headlines called it the "white Sasol employees strike" ([Ridge Times, 12 Sep 2018](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2018/09/12/white-sasol-employees-strike/)). Unemployed eMbalenhle residents protested at the Charlie gates over jobs and labour brokers from 2015 to 2018 ([Ridge Times, 19 Feb 2018](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2018/02/19/sasol-is-willing-to-engage-with-locals/)), and one protest convened by a community forum targeted workers from outside the area and attacked buses ([Ridge Times](https://citizen.co.za/ridge-times/?p=108152)). A fatality occurred at Secunda Operations on 16 April 2026, and an August 2026 analysis was headlined "Deadly Secunda plant puts Sasol at crossroads" ([Business Times, 29 Aug 2026](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/business/2026-08-29-news-analysis-deadly-secunda-plant-puts-sasol-at-crossroads/)); the cause was not visible. Current wages, staff-bus arrangements and plant shift patterns were not sourced.

### Taxis guard their routes with force

The Vukanini Taxi Association ("Vukta") dominates the eMbalenhle-Secunda-Evander commute. In **February 2020** its drivers reportedly impounded two Megabus buses, more than 20 taxis were vandalised, the R546 was declared a no-go zone and a taxi driver was shot dead at Blue Rank ([The Citizen](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/south-africa/violence-erupts-in-embalenhle-and-evander-as-megabus-vukta-taxi-association-clash)). A film crew working on a Unicef project was assaulted and robbed in the same days, allegedly by taxi association members ([News24, 8 Feb 2020](https://www.news24.com/SouthAfrica/News/sanef-condemns-attack-on-journalist-covering-taxi-violence-20200208)). In February 2025 alleged association members tried to stop commuters using lift clubs, and Sasol said employees may choose their own transport ([Ridge Times, 7 Feb 2025](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/local-news/2025/02/07/alleged-taxi-association-members-try-to-stop-passengers-from-using-lift-clubs/)). Fares, routes and rank names in Secunda were not found.

### Language, school, sport and going out follow the old plan

Secunda's older suburbs were about two-thirds Afrikaans-speaking at the last detailed counts, eMbalenhle about half isiZulu with isiXhosa, Sesotho and isiNdebele behind ([Census 2011, Embalenhle](https://census.adrianfrith.com/place/866011)). The town has three public high schools, Hoërskool Secunda, Hoërskool Oosterland and the English-medium Highveld Park High School, plus Curro private schools ([Ridge Times, 24 Jan 2023](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/local-news/2023/01/24/hoogies-and-secundas-matrics-perform-best-3/); [Curro](https://curro.co.za/schools/curro-secunda-high-school)). Rugby belongs to the Sasol Secunda Rugby Club and Hoërskool Secunda ([Ridge Times, 27 Jan 2026](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2026/01/27/sasol-secunda-rugby-club-joins-sibanye-stillwater-super-league-2026/)); soccer to **Secunda Stars FC**, based in eMbalenhle and sponsored by the local Hollywoodbets branch ([Hollywood Foundation](https://hollywoodfoundation.co.za/programmes/sponsorship/soccer-sponsorships/secunda-stars-fc)). National chains such as Wimpy and McDonald's serve as landmarks in local crime reports ([Ridge Times, 14 Jun 2018](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2018/06/14/two-lure-man-to-wimpy-and-then-rob-him/)). No church was verified by name in any settlement, and no tavern, shisanyama or everyday food was sourced. The researchers' unsourced expectation, generic to Highveld townships, is pap and grilled meat, kota and vetkoek from street vendors, and braais in the suburbs.

### Frost, thunderstorms, cosmos and the smell

Secunda sits at about 1,600 m with summer thunderstorms and cold, dry winters with regular frost ([Wikipedia, Secunda](https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Secunda_(town))). Rain comes as heavy late-afternoon storms from about November to March ([Wikipedia, Climate of South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Climate_of_South_Africa)). The district is marketed as "Cosmos Country" ([SA-Venues](https://www.sa-venues.com/attractionsmpl/secunda.php)); on the Highveld generally, **cosmos flowers pink, purple and white from about mid-March to May**, peaking in late March and early April along roads and railway lines ([Getaway](https://getaway.co.za/?p=15483705)). No Secunda-specific sighting was sourced. The winter fire season officially starts on 1 June, with firebreaks burned before it ([Ridge Times, 29 Jun 2025](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2025/06/29/experts-share-practical-tips-to-prevent-veld-fires-3/)).

The air is the most heavily documented local health issue. Government has attributed regional "rotten egg" episodes to hydrogen sulphide and named Sasol Secunda as a significant source, while Sasol says its emissions are within licence limits ([DFFE statement](https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/forestry-fisheries-and-environment-sulphurous-odour-across-parts-gauteng-12)). Easterly winds carry the plume over eMbalenhle ([Greenpeace Africa](https://www.greenpeace.org/africa/en/blogs/12888/where-theres-smoke)), which ranked second-worst for air quality among South African towns in 2022 ([The Citizen](https://citizen.co.za/witbank-news/lnn/article/pollution-pall-over-mpumalanga-town-sasols-air-quality-reaction)). In **September 2026** a CREA study, an NGO estimate, attributed about 1,000 deaths a year across the Highveld to the plant's emissions; residents told Daily Maverick of a childhood smell "like urine or rotten eggs", migraines that worsen when wind carries fumes in, and of pinning their hopes on one day working for Sasol ([Daily Maverick, 8 Sep 2026](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-09-08-mpumalanga-community-speaks-out-as-study-links-sasol-pollution-to-1000-deaths/)). Pollution and the hope of a Sasol job live in the same households. Sasol's position is that ambient results reflect the whole area and cannot be attributed to it alone ([Sasol](https://sasol.com/sustainability/environment/air-quality)).

## One employer, a bankrupt municipality and private everything

### Dependency, unemployment and debt

A Sasol vice-president told Bloomberg that "around 80% of Secunda works for Sasol in one form or another" ([Bloomberg, 19 Jan 2024](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-01-19/south-african-town-that-rose-with-coal-faces-uncertain-future)); a 2026 report says more than 70% of residents depend on it ([Mining Weekly, 29 May 2026](https://www.miningweekly.com/article/report-highlights-challenges-risks-facing-sasol-amid-the-just-energy-transition-2026-05-29/)). The municipality's expanded unemployment rate is given as 34.9%, year unclear ([Wits Mandela Institute](https://www.wits.ac.za/media/wits-university/faculties-and-schools/commerce-law-and-management/research-entities/mandela-institute/documents/research-publications/WRT_WSA%20Profile_Mpumalanga_Govan%20Mbeki_Narrative.pdf)). Loan sharks who hold borrowers' bank cards, grant cards and PINs are well documented across Mpumalanga, for example a Hawks raid in Mbombela in May 2024 ([Mpumalanga News, 21 May 2024](https://www.citizen.co.za/mpumalanga-news/news-headlines/local-news/2024/05/21/update-hawks-raid-alleged-mbombela-loan-sharks-offices-confiscate-id-books-and-bank-cards/)), but no Secunda or eMbalenhle case was found.

### Business forums are a provincial pattern, not a documented Secunda one

Nationally, "business forums" arrive at sites demanding 30% of contract value for members ([GI-TOC, 2022](https://globalinitiative.net/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/GITOC-Extortion-or-Transformation-The-construction-mafia-in-South-Africa.pdf)), and extortion of the mining sector is reported ([S-RM, 30 June 2025](https://www.s-rminform.com/global-risk-hub/something-for-nothing-extortion-in-south-africas-mining-industry)). In Secunda itself the documented event sits at the lawful end: in October 2020 a local youth chamber of commerce marched on Sasol demanding easier vendor registration and procurement access ([Ridge Times, 6 Oct 2020](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2020/10/06/local-youth-chamber-of-commerce-and-industry-demands-changes-in-sasol/)). **No extortion incident at Sasol Secunda, Sasol Mining or a Secunda contractor was found.** Coal and diesel theft specific to Secunda were not found either. Cable theft is local and serious: a suspected theft cut power to the Secunda CBD for at least three days from 20 January 2026 ([Ridge Times, 23 Jan 2026](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2026/01/23/update-the-latest-on-prolonged-electricity-interruption-in-secunda/)).

### The municipality cannot keep water in the pipes

Parliament's Cogta committee described Govan Mbeki in 2021 as on the "brink of financial ruin" ([Parliament](https://www.parliament.gov.za/news/govan-mbeki-brink-financial-ruin)). Its **Eskom debt reached R6.37 billion by December 2025** ([Ridge Times, 11 Dec 2025](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2025/12/11/govan-mbeki-municipalitys-eskom-debt-reaches-r6b-despite-smart-meter-progress/)); a DA petition, a partisan source, puts its Rand Water debt at about R850 million ([Change.org](https://www.change.org/p/urgent-complaint-of-systemic-water-service-failure-by-govan-mbeki-municipality)). In 2026 higher-lying parts of Secunda went 15 consecutive days without reliable water, with reservoirs reported at 0% ([Joburg ETC](https://www.joburgetc.com/news/secunda-water-crisis-protest-council-meeting/); [AfriForum](https://www.artikels.afriforum.co.za/en/its-a-disaster-parts-of-govan-mbeki-municipality-still-without-water-after-more-than-a-week-reservoirs-at-0/)). The audit outcome improved to unqualified with findings in February 2026, with the Auditor-General still flagging that the municipality is not financially sustainable ([Ridge Times, 7 Feb 2026](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2026/02/07/govan-mbeki-municipality-receives-unqualified-audit-opinion-with-findings-2/)). Two distinct electricity grievances follow: eMbalenhle's is Eskom cut-offs tied to municipal non-payment, and Secunda's is a failing, stolen cable network.

### Crime is answered by private response first

Local reports show armed business robberies, house robberies and hijackings met by private armed-response firms, a Community Policing Forum and AfriForum volunteers coordinating on the **Zello push-to-talk app**. In July 2021 the CPF chairman, alerted on Zello to an ATM robbery at a Sasol filling station, joined a tactical team in a high-speed chase into Evander ([Ridge Times, 23 Jul 2021](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2021/07/23/secunda-cpf-arrests-robbers/)); in December 2021 robbers fleeing toward Evander were pursued by several security companies, the CPF, AfriForum and police ([Ridge Times, 9 Dec 2021](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2021/12/09/armed-robbers-hit-bala-bala-in-secunda-three-caught-at-evander/)). Gate-motor theft spiked in 2023 ([Ridge Times, 24 Aug 2023](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/local-news/2023/08/24/gate-motor-theft-rises-in-secunda/)). A security firm warned in April 2026 that community WhatsApp alert groups spread misinformation when poorly run ([Ridge Times, 13 Apr 2026](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2026/04/13/security-firm-highlights-risks-of-community-whatsapp-alerts/)). Precinct crime statistics were not retrieved.

### The transition has turned from closure to reprieve with dread

Sasol impaired the Secunda liquid-fuels refinery in full in August 2023, R35.3 billion ([Engineering News, 23 Aug 2023](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/secunda-refinerys-post-2030-output-cut-as-sasol-struggles-to-pin-down-gas-supply-2023-08-23)), then in 2025 cut emission-reduction spending by about 70% while keeping its 30% by 2030 target and dropping the assumption that Secunda output must fall ([Engineering News, 20 May 2025](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/sasol-slashes-emission-reduction-capex-but-insists-30-target-remains-intact-2025-05-20)). Sasol's own estimate is that an unplanned exit from coal-to-liquids would cost about 24,900 jobs ([BusinessTech](https://businesstech.co.za/news/?p=870298)). In Mpumalanga mining communities the documented mood is that "there's no belief people will benefit" from a just transition ([Daily Maverick, 16 Oct 2022](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2022-10-16-theres-no-belief-people-will-benefit-mpumalanga-mining-communities-deep-mistrust-of-just-transition/)).

## News, police and courts split along the same lines

### One free weekly, and no confirmed FM community station

The solidly documented news organ is the Caxton free weekly **Ridge Times**: 35,000 copies to Secunda, Trichardt, Bethal, Evander, Kinross and Kriel, delivered on Tuesday or Wednesday depending on which of its pages is right ([About Ridge Times](https://citizen.co.za/ridge-times/?p=81462)). A second title, The Bulletin, carries Secunda sport ([The Bulletin](https://thebulletin.co.za/sport/secunda-rugby-keeps-vereeniging-at-bay)); its ownership was not established. Radio is thin. The only FM frequency tied to Secunda in a source is the commercial station MPowerFM on 105.8 ([Wikipedia, MPowerFM](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/MPowerFM)). "Emba Community FM", "Eastern Highveld FM" and "SecRadio Online" appear as online streams in directories ([Streema](https://streema.com/radios/Emba_Community_FM); [SecRadio Online](https://hearthis.at/secradioonline/)); the MDDA lists a youth-founded "Mpumalanga East Community Radio" licensed for "Secunda East" ([MDDA](https://www.mdda.org.za/mpuma.html)); and a 2007 gazette records an application for a 1-watt "Secunda FM" on 97.6 from a shopping centre ([Government Gazette 30139](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201409/30139.pdf)). **No community station was confirmed on air on FM in Secunda or eMbalenhle.**

Nationally, a community broadcasting licence goes to a non-profit, covers no more than a district municipality, and needs both a service licence and a radio frequency spectrum licence from ICASA ([Gazette 42323, 2019](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201903/42323gon439.pdf); [ICASA, 2026](https://www.icasa.org.za/news/2026/icasa-licenses-18-new-community-radio-stations)). MDDA grants exist but have been unreliable ([Daily Maverick](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/?p=459356)).

### Three stations, two courts, a High Court in Delmas and a mortuary at Evander

Secunda has its own SAPS station with holding cells ([Global Detention Project](https://www.globaldetentionproject.org/countries/africa/south-africa/detention-centres/2608/secunda-police-station)) and its own magistrate's court, which takes first appearances in murder cases; the sequence in reported cases runs first appearance, postponement for legal aid, bail application, postponements awaiting the Director of Public Prosecutions, then transfer to **the High Court in Delmas** ([Ridge Times, 20 Apr 2026](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/local-news/2026/04/20/man-appears-in-secunda-court-for-wifes-murder/); [Ridge Times, 30 Mar 2022](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2022/03/30/watson-makes-an-appearance-in-secunda-magistrates-court/)). eMbalenhle and Evander each have a SAPS station, and the Evander Magistrate's Court handled the bail of **five eMbalenhle police officers arrested in late 2025**; the charges were not visible ([Ridge Times, 2 Dec 2025](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2025/12/02/five-arrested-embalenhle-cops-make-bail/)). eMbalenhle SAPS has a record of complaints: poor service, a community demand for IPID intervention over police brutality in October 2025, and the highest crime rate in the district ([Ridge Times, 21 Oct 2025](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2025/10/21/community-demands-ipid-intervention-against-police-brutality-2/); [Ridge Times, 9 Dec 2025](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2025/12/09/embalenhle-struggles-with-highest-crime-rate-in-district/)). Evander is listed among Mpumalanga's forensic pathology units ([PMG question NW686](https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/25268/)); that it serves Secunda is an inference. In May 2026 bodies piled up in Mpumalanga state mortuaries because forensic officers refused to perform incisions ([Middelburg Observer, 21 May 2026](https://www.citizen.co.za/middelburg-observer/news/2026/05/21/bodies-piling-up-in-state-morgues-as-officers-down-scalpels/)).

National practice fills in procedure. Case numbers take the form "CAS 191/09/2021" and belong to the station where the case was opened ([PMG](https://pmg.org.za/files/231128_Presentation_Raseale_Petition_t.pdf)). Police often open an **inquest docket** and convert it to murder once the post-mortem shows foul play ([EWN, 7 Aug 2026](https://www.ewn.co.za/2026/08/07/post-mortem-suggests-st-stithians-pupil-died-of-strangulation-at-mpumalanga-lodge)). SAPS ranks run constable, sergeant, warrant officer, lieutenant, captain, major, lieutenant-colonel, colonel, brigadier and the general ranks ([Wikipedia, SAPS](https://en.Wikipedia.com/wiki/South_African_Police_Service)).

### A private investigator is a registered security provider with no police powers

Private investigation is regulated as a security service; working unregistered with PSIRA is a criminal offence, and section 15 of the Private Security Industry Regulation Act deals with investigators ([ProtectionWeb](https://www.protectionweb.co.za/opinion-and-analysis/private-investigations-regulated-as-security-service/); [PSIR Act s15](https://source.acts.co.za/private-security-industry-regulation-act-2001/n305_15__private_investigators.php)). A private person's handgun falls under section 13 of the Firearms Control Act, the licence for self-defence, and may be carried in public holstered or fully covered ([SAFLII](https://www.saflii.org/za/legis/consol_act/fca2000192/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firearms_regulation_in_South_Africa)). The limits a writer will want, that an investigator has only a citizen's arrest power, cannot intercept communications and cannot obtain bank or phone records without consent or subpoena, are general knowledge consistent with these sources but not confirmed by a primary text in this research.

## What the town looks like: a short sourced list and a long unsourced one

### What sources support

The sourced visual record is narrow. The landscape is defined by "massive cooling towers, tall chimneys, and steel structures" within view of town ([Wikipedia, Secunda](https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Secunda_(town))). The cooling towers are 45-year-old concrete. Smoke "towers out of the stacks and inevitably is blown into the township", and published photographs show the towers over eMbalenhle's roofs ([Greenpeace Africa](https://www.greenpeace.org/africa/en/blogs/12888/where-theres-smoke)). Houses are tan-brick bungalows in earth tones on tree-lined boulevards. Roads are patchwork: "big, deep potholes" on the Evander approach, collapsing roads in Trichardt, truck damage on Evander's Camborne Road ([Ridge Times, 19 Jul 2025](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2025/07/19/the-lack-of-repairs-on-roads-by-gmm-in-evander-is-a-growing-concern-for-residents/)). Underground workers wear a **one-piece boiler suit in white, yellow or orange**, a hard hat with cap-lamp fitment and company branding, and gumboots with metatarsal guards ([Sasol PPE standard](https://www.sasol.com/sites/default/files/2023-08/9.13%20%20Generic%20and%20Sector%20Specific%20PPE%20Standards%20%281%29_0_1.pdf)). Most minibus taxis nationally are Toyota HiAce or Quantum 16-seaters with one small association sticker ([HSRC Press](https://hsrcpress.ac.za/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Township-Economy-2I-7.pdf)). Gasification ash is red and grey clinker. Gold headgears and tailings mark Evander.

### What is only the researchers' general knowledge

Everything in this paragraph is unsourced and must be checked against photographs. The land is flat to gently rolling, so the plant shows as towers on the horizon from many kilometres. At night the structures carry white and orange work lights, red aircraft-warning lights and flickering flares, with an orange glow under low cloud. Houses are single-storey face brick with tiled or sheet roofs behind precast concrete or brick walls topped with electric-fence strands, with armed-response boards on gateposts and sliding motor gates. Traffic lights are "robots", four-way stops are common, and street names are on kerbstones or small pole plates. Vehicles are mostly white bakkies; site vehicles carry a whip flag, an amber beacon and chevron tape; coal trucks are dust-black side-tipper interlinks; SAPS vans are white with blue and yellow markings and a caged canopy; police wear blue. Surface workers wear two-piece overalls with reflective bands in contractor colours. eMbalenhle is small block houses, backyard rooms, zinc shacks at the edges, spaza shops in containers, taverns, tent churches and winter coal smoke. The countryside is maize, sunflower and soya in a green stormy summer and blond frosted grass with burnt black firebreaks in winter, with blue-gum rows, pylons, enclosed conveyors on trestles, grey ash dams and pale gold tailings. Winter mornings have a brown inversion layer and sunsets are strongly orange.

## What the invented colliery, radio station and fraud can lean on

### The family colliery is pure invention, so its details must come from the documented pattern

No small family-owned colliery was found in the Secunda area, and no Sasol-area mine has been sold to a small buyer; the junior acquisitions on record happened elsewhere in Mpumalanga, such as Delmas Colliery going to Kuyasa Mining in 2002 and the idled Mooiplaats colliery sold by Coal of Africa ([BHP release](https://www.bhp.com/-/media/bhp/documents/investors/news/delmasmediarelease.pdf?la=en); [Mining Weekly, 22 Sept 2014](https://www.miningweekly.com/article/coal-of-africa-sells-idled-mooiplaats-for-235m-2014-09-22)). The story is therefore adding something the real district does not visibly have, which is safe for a fiction but means nothing about the mine can be checked against a local model. It should be named in the local manner, a farm name plus "Colliery", and should not borrow a real Sasol shaft or farm name. It would not have a 20 km conveyor; it would truck coal by road to a buyer's stockpile or a rail siding, and the trucking leg is exactly where documented frauds occur. Its realistic customer is the open market that Sasol entered in December 2021 and widened in April 2025. The timing has an edge the plot can use: since the destoning plant came on line, Sasol has been cutting outside purchases, so in 2026 a small supplier's window is closing. The mine's geology should be the district's: a seam a few metres thick at around 100 m depth, worked bord-and-pillar with continuous miners, and broken by dolerite.

### The fraud has real precedents, all of them about Eskom

Every element of a coal-quality fraud has a documented South African case, and none involves Sasol. In the Brakfontein affair one laboratory failed 15 of 30 samples and SABS failed 29 of 30, and the Eskom coal scientist who objected was suspended ([Mail & Guardian, 30 Aug 2016](https://mg.co.za/article/2016-08-30-tegeta-coal-quality-hopelessly-noncompliant-says-report/)). The Special Investigating Unit told Parliament that officials arranged for samples to be taken with no observer present and for samples to be swapped ([Business Day, 19 Oct 2022](https://www.businesslive.co.za/bd/national/2022-10-19-siu-tells-parliament-of-organised-crime-behind-coal-contracts-at-eskom/)). Truckloads have been swapped for discard coal at illegal yards ([SAnews](https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/alleged-coal-thief-apprehended-arnot-power-station)), and weighbridge clerks have issued tickets for coal never delivered ([Jacaranda FM](https://www.jacarandafm.com/news/news/driver-clerks-arrested-stealing-tons-eskom-coal/)). The story should treat these as the mechanics its characters know from the news, and should invent the buyer's staff and the laboratory instead of implying wrongdoing by real Sasol employees or a named laboratory.

Two variants are available and differ in how grounded they are. A fraud in delivered coal, with sinks hidden by a compromised sampler or a swapped load, maps directly onto precedent and onto the one quality measure the real plant cares about. A fraud in the mine's resource report, with boreholes that understate dolerite, fits the geology well, because the academic record shows the damage is local and visible only in close drilling, and because core is physical evidence: dolerite and burnt coal look different from banded bright and dull coal in a tray. But "salting" exploration core has no South African coal precedent in this research, a private mine is not obliged to publish a SAMREC-compliant report, and the mechanics of core sheds and laboratory certificates were not sourced. That variant needs a geologist's read before it is written.

### The radio station should be fictional in name, frequency and licence history

A small community station is credible as a non-profit with a board, two ICASA licences, a footprint capped at the district and an unreliable grant history. Because no real local station was confirmed on air, an invented one fills a believable gap. It should avoid the names found in directories (Emba Community FM, Eastern Highveld FM, SecRadio Online, Secunda FM, Mpumalanga East Community Radio) and the two frequencies tied to the town (105.8 and 97.6). Its realistic threats are documented: taxi-industry muscle during a route dispute, and death threats around marches and council politics ([Ridge Times, 15 Dec 2022](https://www.citizen.co.za/ridge-times/news-headlines/2022/12/15/vf-councillor-opens-case-against-speaker-of-govan-mbeki-municipality-for-alleged-death-threat/)). Its competition is the free weekly, Facebook and WhatsApp groups. The typical programming of such a station was not sourced.

### Jurisdiction gives the plot its structure

Where a body is found decides who handles it. In Secunda town the case goes to Secunda SAPS and the Secunda Magistrate's Court; in eMbalenhle to a station with five officers on bail and a community asking for IPID, and on the evidence to the Evander court; a trial goes to Delmas. A docket that opens as an inquest and waits on a delayed post-mortem is both procedurally correct and timely given the 2026 mortuary action. The detective, as a PSIRA-registered investigator, has no powers the police have, and will spend the game dealing with security-company control rooms, CPF volunteers and Zello chatter in the suburbs, and with a very different, more distrustful policing reality across the buffer. A "business forum" character should be written the way real forums present themselves, as local empowerment, with intimidation as an implication the game adds and not as a reported local fact. Real security companies, taxi associations, unions and named individuals from the crime reports should be replaced with inventions wherever the story attributes wrongdoing.

## Conclusion

The research supports a more specific Secunda than "a coal town". Its distinctiveness is that a single 1970s drawing still organises everything: who lives against the Duck Pond and who lives downwind, which court a body goes to, which road a protest closes to stop the shift change, where the casino sits between two populations. The present-day texture that makes it noir already exists in the record and needs no exaggeration: a company that has written its own plant down to nothing but keeps it running, a municipality that cannot pay for water, residents painting their own road markings, and volunteers chasing robbers toward Evander while the police open the docket afterwards. The coal-quality plot is unusually well matched to the place, because for five years the town's real anxiety was the quality of its coal.

The research does not yet support drawing the town. Words about institutions, dates and money are reliable enough to write from; almost nothing about surfaces, colours, layouts and light is. The most efficient next step is not more reading but four concrete checks: open a map and fix the compass layout and road numbers; walk the CBD, a suburb, the R580 and eMbalenhle in Street View; pull the Sadiki and Swiegers photo sets for the skyline; and obtain Kirchhofer's 1982 paper, which holds the plans the whole town was built from. A coal geologist's review of the core and laboratory scenes would close the one gap that reading cannot.
